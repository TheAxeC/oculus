## What to build (code and shell; no data, wire or config change)

1. **A rail button under a terminal palette is one glyph over one label, as under any theme.** The bracket pseudo-elements are not drawn on a stacked rail control (`body.rice .tool.rail-control.stacked`, or the stacked class alone, since the brackets are a row's shape); the glyph slot holds a stand-in of up to three characters and keeps the rail's column aligned: under `body.rice` the slot is a fixed `3ch` box, centred, and under every other theme the `1.2em` box it is. Measured on the tree by the browser spec of item 4: a rail button under mocha is 34 px high like the light theme's, its glyph span's scroll width does not exceed its client width, and its computed `::before` content is `none`.
2. **The flyout lists and the rail's tooltips paint over the panes with the wallpaper on.** Under `body.wall` the rail's stacking context paints above the column: `position: relative` and a z-index above the panes on `.rail` (a value the shell owns, ordered against `shell/DropLayer.svelte`'s and the palette's), or the blur moved off the element the lists descend from (a pseudo-element carrying the `backdrop-filter`, which contains nothing); the worker chooses, says which in the report, and keeps `ui/Menu.svelte`'s coordinate comment true. Measured by item 4.
3. **A tooltip bubble is never contained or clipped by the surface its control sits on.** The bubble is mounted on the document body (a Svelte action that moves the span to `document.body` while open and removes it on destroy, `aria-describedby` still naming it), so no filtered or clipping ancestor contains it; or, if the measurement of item 4 shows a card's bubble whole and on top under mocha with the wallpaper on, the report says so with the numbers and this item is closed by that measurement alone. Either way the spec of item 4 asserts it.
4. **Tests.** (a) `tests/browser/rail.spec.ts` (new), under mocha through the rail's theme menu with the wallpaper on (the toggle as the appearance spec drives it): the System flyout opened, the element at the centre of its list inside the list; the Theme menu likewise; a rail button's height 34 px and its glyph span not overflowing (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth`) and no `::before` content; a card control with tooltip words hovered on the Projects board, its bubble visible, its rect inside the viewport, and the element at its centre inside the bubble. (b) `tests/ui/primitives.test.ts` or a new `tests/ui/glyph.test.ts`: the glyph slot's terminal-palette rule exists in the source with the `3ch` width and the stacked rail control's bracket rule is absent, in the source-audit shape of `tests/views/type-scale.test.ts`. (c) The existing `tests/browser/keyboard.spec.ts`, `appearance.spec.ts` and `tests/shell/rail.test.ts` pass unchanged; no existing selector moves.
5. **`DESIGN.md`:** if the rail's stacking rule or the bubble's mount is a design fact (a z-order the shell owns, a bubble on the body), one sentence in the wallpaper paragraph (line 206) or the shell's, no history.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The stacked rail control draws no brackets under a terminal palette; the stand-in itself carries its shape (`[@]`), which is the cockpit's look for a glyph and the reason the brackets read as a mess when doubled.
2. The glyph slot is `3ch` under a terminal palette and `1.2em` otherwise, in `ui/Glyph.svelte`, so every rail of glyphs stays one column.
3. The z-order fix is the shell's (the rail above the panes under a wallpaper), or the blur on a pseudo-element; the worker's choice, reported.
4. The tooltip bubble on the body through an action, if the measurement shows it contained.
