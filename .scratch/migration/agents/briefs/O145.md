## What to build

When this step is done, a deck is arranged in the Present view's Overview, on tiles that show the slides, and the Slides view no longer exists.

1. **Tiles draw their slides.** Each tile of the Overview draws its slide through `Slide.svelte` inside `Stage.svelte`, scaled to the tile, with the slide's place and title under it. The tile keeps what it has now: the project's hue on its left edge, the outline of the current slide, the shell's card cursor and its keys, a press showing the slide and closing the grid, and the slide menu on a right-click. A tile is not a live control surface: a press anywhere on it acts as the tile's press, and no control inside the drawn slide takes the press or the focus (make the drawn slide `inert`).
2. **The deck in its order, with each group at its place.** The Overview draws the entries of `order:` in order. A file, summary or grid entry draws its slides as tiles. A `slides:` entry of a group draws a group header (the `GroupHead` every grouping uses: the group's name and the count of its decks, `members.length`, not `DeckGroup.count`) with the slides of its member decks under it as tiles, in the group's order. A group no `slides:` entry places, but a summary or grid entry names, is headed at its first such entry. A group no entry names is drawn at the end under its header. A member's slides are known by the key prefix `${member.doc.name}/` (`read.ts:460`), which every member slide carries.
   - Every deck and every entry can be arranged, so each has a tile: a member deck that draws no slide in the Overview (every member of a group placed only by a summary or grid, as `ideas` is; a member whose `present.yml` is empty or unreadable) is one title tile naming the deck, with the same drag and menu as a slide tile. An `order:` entry that places no slide (a `summary`, `grid` or `{ slides: x }` naming no group, `order_group_unknown` in `read.ts:373-376` and `:454-457`; a file that is missing or holds no slide) is one placeholder tile naming the entry and its finding, moved by drag and by Move Earlier and Move Later like any entry.
   - A slide with no `source` (`read.ts:342`) is drawn as a tile with no arranging control.
   - `Present.svelte` draws the Overview, open, whenever the deck has an `order:` entry or a group, even with no slide; `Empty` stays for a deck with neither, and for a file that cannot be read.
3. **The group's rule, one sentence.** Each group header carries its `find` as one plain sentence read from `Deck.keys`, built from catalog strings: what it finds (tiers, stages, forms, status chips, each list as words of the engine's vocabulary in the order the file holds them) and where (the folder globs), and whether it adds what it finds (`discover`) and how it sorts. A group with no `find` says it shows the project decks it names. An Edit Rule control on the header opens, under the header, the form the Slides view draws for find, sort and discover (`GroupKeys.svelte`'s fields and the `findForm` and `findValue` logic), which writes at its Apply control and closes; Cancel closes it with nothing written.
   - A key typed in a field inside the Overview stays with the field: `Present.svelte`'s `keys` handler returns when `inControl(event.target)` holds, for every key it takes, and Escape in an open form (Edit Rule or the deck's Edit) cancels the form before it closes the grid.
4. **Arranging.** Every change writes at once through `writeDeckSlides` with the bases the deck now holds, and the deck is read again at the next push, as an edit of a slide is; History undoes it. A refused write writes nothing and shows the server's sentence in the Present toolbar's status line (`acts.said`), and the Overview draws the file as it is. From a write until its answer and the next push of the deck, every arranging control is disabled, so the next act is built on the bases the first act wrote.
   - Drag a tile of a file, summary or grid entry, a placeholder tile, or a group's header, to another place among the entries: the `order:` entry moves. Dropped on a group's place (its header or its tiles), the entry moves before or after that group's header, by the half of the target it is dropped on.
   - Drag a member deck's tile (any of its slides, or its title tile) within its group: the deck moves in the group's `slides:`. The place in `slides:` counts the named decks only, and missing names keep their places; a drop among the decks found by discovery names the deck at the end of `slides:`. A deck found by discovery and not named is named at its new place by the move.
   - Drag a member deck's tile onto another group's header or into its tiles: it leaves its group and joins that one at the place it is dropped, both lists in one write, as `withCross` does. A deck found by discovery and not named in its group is added to the target's `slides:` only, in one write. Dropped on a group that holds it already, nothing is written and the status line says so.
   - A drop outside any target writes nothing and says nothing.
   - A tile's menu gains, for a member deck, Remove from Group (takes the deck out of the group's `slides:`; a deck found by discovery, which `slides:` does not name, gets no such item), and for every tile and header Move Earlier and Move Later, the keyboard path for each drag above. Their menu ids are distinct from the slide acts' (`edit`, `new`, `duplicate`, `delete`; `present.ts:364`, `acts/menu.ts:34-36`) and are dispatched apart from `acts.act`.
   - Each group ends in an Add tile whose press opens a menu of the decks its `find` matches that it does not hold (`candidates`), each by its title; a pick adds the deck at the end of the group's `slides:`. A candidate another group names is offered too, as the server accepts it. With no candidate the tile says so and opens nothing.
   - Missing names (`missing`) and conflicts are drawn on the group header as tags naming them, never skipped.
   - Every member, candidate, missing and conflict label the Overview draws goes through `plainText`, as `deckLabel` does (item 7).
   - Group headers do not fold in the Overview, so no key is added to the view's saved state.
   - The card cursor's keys move over the slide and title tiles as today; headers, Add tiles and placeholder tiles are buttons in the tab order, and Enter on them is their press.
   - A deck whose `present.yml` cannot be read draws `Empty` with the read's finding, as today, and offers no arranging control and sends no write.
5. **The deck's own keys.** The Overview opens with a header line holding the deck's title, an Edit control that opens the Slides view's Deck fields (`Keys.svelte`: title and tag tones, with their logic `keyShown`, `withKey`, `keyChanges`, `tonesOf`, `withTone`, `deckHues`) under it with Apply and Cancel, written through `writeDeckSlides`'s `keys`.
6. **The Slides view retired.** `src/lib/views/academic/slides/` and `src/lib/state/life/slides.ts` are deleted; the logic the Overview reuses moves into a state module named for arranging a deck (`src/lib/state/life/arrange.ts`, keeping `state/life` at 10 items), each function kept only if it has a caller. `config/text/views/academic/slides.yml` is deleted; every string the Overview still uses moves into `config/text/views/academic/present.yml` under the `present` group, renamed for what it says there, and every key nothing reads goes. `Bar.svelte`'s Slides button and `Present.svelte`'s `onslides` go. A saved layout that holds a `slides` tab shows it as an unknown view (`docs/HELP.md:266` says so of every removed view); no code reads or converts it.
7. **Labels in plain text.** `deckLabel` returns the title through `plainText`, so the deck menu, the tab title, the audience window's title (`src/routes/present/show/+page.svelte:29,211`, unchanged, which calls it) and every label built from it read "Comparing tumors across patients with no template alignment"; a title that is empty after `plainText` (`<i></i>`) gives `deck.name`. The slide itself still draws the title's markup.
8. **Every sentence true.** Every comment, catalog line and document that names the Slides view or describes the deck's lists is rewritten to the Overview: the hits listed under "What is on the tree", `docs/HELP.md:331` rewritten as short bullets of what a user does in the Overview, `docs/HELP.md:266` with `slides` taken out of the list, `docs/templates.md:30`, `config/text/server.yml:147`, the head comment of `GroupHead.svelte:65-66`, `tests/checks/config/config-folder.test.ts:303` with the deleted file taken out, and `tests/routes/edit/deck-slides.test.ts:297-303` with its expected sentence made the new text of `server.yml:147` and its title and row no longer naming the view. The generated diagrams are regenerated by `npm run diagrams` and `docs/palette.md` by `npm run docs:palette`.

Each file is under the size rule: `Present.svelte` stays at or under 520 lines, so the Overview and its new parts live in a new folder `src/lib/views/academic/present/arrange/`, `Overview.svelte` moved into it with `mv` (so `present/` stays at 10 items), each new file opening with the tree's head comment; every component there stays at or under 520. A comment rewritten in `writes.svelte.ts` keeps the file at or under 880. Every control is a `ui/` primitive; every string a catalog line; every hue a token.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Every arranging act writes at once, as an edit of a slide does, and History undoes it; the Slides view's Save and its unsaved work go. The rule and the deck's keys are edited in a form with Apply and Cancel, so a half-typed glob is never written.
2. Move Earlier and Move Later in the tile and header menus are the keyboard path for every drag.
3. A group named only by a summary or grid entry is headed at its first such entry with a title tile per deck; a group no entry names is drawn at the end. An entry or a deck that draws no slide gets a placeholder or title tile, so everything the file lists can be arranged.
4. The time the Overview takes to draw on the portfolio deck is measured by the orchestrator at the look, from the press of `O` to the last tile drawn, on main before and on the step after; the bar is 1 s after. A step over the bar is a finding at landing.
5. The drawn slide in a tile is `inert`, so the tile is the press target and nothing inside it takes the focus.
6. Group headers do not fold in the Overview, so the view's saved state gains no key; the card cursor stays on slide and title tiles, and headers, Add tiles and placeholders are reached in the tab order.
7. While a write is in flight the arranging controls are disabled, so every act is built on the bases the act before it wrote.
