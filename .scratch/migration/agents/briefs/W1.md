(c) `tests/browser/appearance.spec.ts` gains one spec: mocha selected through the rail's theme menu, a board opened (the helpers of `tests/browser/board.spec.ts` and `rail.ts`), then a card's computed `background-color` has an alpha below 1, the pane body's is transparent and the `.wallpaper` layer is present; the rail's wallpaper toggle takes the layer away and the card's alpha back to 1. ## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The translucency rule lives in the component that paints the surface, beside the colour it modifies; no shared wallpaper stylesheet, so a reader of a component sees both.
2. The pane body paints nothing over a wallpaper; the pane's frame and strip stay.
3. The toggle is three-state in the store (`boolean | null`), and `UI_VERSION` goes to 2 with a version 1 record read as null for that one field.
4. No new key in `config/appearance.yml`: the three numbers it has are the whole configuration.
