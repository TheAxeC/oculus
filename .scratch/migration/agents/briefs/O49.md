## What to build

Each item names where it comes from.

1. (Ruling 202, "an "Open View" submenu ... in the tab strip's and the empty pane's right-click menus".) `stripMenu` gains an item "Open View" whose `items` are its submenu, as the third item of its first group, after Open File... and New Text File (Decisions 1). It shows on the pane of a page and on the pane of a float, with or without a tab, locked or not. Its words are a new text key `ui.strip_open_view` in `config/text/ui.yml`, after `strip_open_file`, with the value `Open View` (the ruling's words).
2. (Ruling 202, "grouped by rail page", "the views apps add included".) The submenu holds one item per section of `arrange(listedViews(views()), readGroups(config), [])`, in that order: a section with a label is an item with that label and the section's glyph (`groupGlyph`) whose own submenu lists the section's views in the section's order, each with its label (`registrationLabel`) and its glyph (`registrationGlyph`); the views of the last section with no label follow the group items directly in the Open View submenu, with no heading (Decisions 3). The groups are the rail pages in the rail's order and then the `nav_groups:` groups, Float among them (Decisions 7). An app's view is listed under the group the Views view lists it under, only while it is listed (its app active), and carries its container's title (`heading`, or the text of `headingKey`) as the item's hover words (`title`) (Decisions 9). Documents of `docs:` are not listed (Decisions 4). The fold is the existing `arrange`; no second grouping is written.
3. (Ruling 202, "opening the picked view as a tab in that pane".) Whether a picked view is held is decided at the pick, never from the menu as it was drawn: the menu is a snapshot taken at the right-click, and another browser tab or window sharing the layout may open or close a unique view while it is open. At the pick, a view that is not a unique view held by a page or float opens as a new instance, a tab of the pane whose menu it was, by `openInPane(view, undefined, page, path)`, `page` being that pane's own host, the page whose tree holds it (for a pane of a page drawn inside another page, the inner page) or the float. A view the registry no longer holds at the pick (`viewFor(view)` undefined, an app disabled or removed while the menu was open) opens nothing.
4. (Ruling Open View unique.) A unique view that a page or float holds is listed with a `hint` naming its holder, and its pick shows it there. The holder is the one `openView` shows: `opening.ts` answers it through a function beside `heldInstance` that reuses `holderOf` and answers the holding host, exposed on the layout store as `heldInstance` is (`Opening`, `opening.ts:72-92` and its return at line 489; `layout.svelte.ts:215` and `:827`), so the hint and the pick never name two different holders. The hint is that host's `label` when it is a page (`isPage`), or `t('ui.float_label')` when it is a float (Decisions 5). At the pick, a unique view still held is shown by `openView(view)`, as every opener does, and the pane the menu was opened on is left as it is; one no page or float holds at the pick opens in the pane as item 3 says. A unique view held by the very pane or page the menu is on is shown where it is, and the pane gains no second tab.
5. (`spec.md`, the size rule and the folder rule.) The menu's items and the pick are built in one new module, `src/lib/shell/panes/strip/open-view.svelte.ts` (Decisions 2), which reads the registry, the config and the layout from the client as `close-question.svelte.ts` does and answers the Open View item and the handling of its picks. Its pick ids carry a prefix of their own that no other strip or pane id starts with. `pane-view.ts`'s `stripMenu` takes the item as a fact and places it; `PaneView.svelte` makes the module, hands its item to `stripMenu` and gives its picks to it before `stripPicked`, and stays at or under 520 lines (split by concern in this step if the change would pass it). The new module opens with the head comment the tree's files open with (layer, what it is, why it is shaped so).
6. (`spec.md`, a public surface with its page, and comments true of the code.) Every comment and page line the change makes false is made true, each rewrapped as a whole paragraph at 100 columns: the head comment of `pane-view.ts` (lines 1-5, "with what each of its picks runs", since the Open View picks run in the new module); the doc comment of `stripMenu` (`pane-view.ts:296-308`) and the head comment of `PaneView.svelte` (lines 43-48), which name Open View among the menu's items; the doc comment of `MenuItem.hint` (`menu-items.ts:24`), which says the field holds the keys that run the choice and must say it holds the muted words at the item's end, the keys that run it or, for a view held elsewhere, where it is held. `docs/HELP.md` "Pages and the pane tree": line 64 names every view that exists once, as `grep -rln "unique: true" src/lib/views` counts them (twelve at the base, the Apps view among them); one bullet after line 75 says what Open View does: where it is, that it lists the views by rail page, apps' views included, that the pick opens in that pane, and that a view open once and held elsewhere names where it is and the pick goes there.
7. (`spec.md`, Token discipline.) `docs/diagrams/` is regenerated by `npm run diagrams`; no diagram is edited by hand.

No config key, wire format or vocabulary is added; the one text key is the ruling's words. The step reports, as `spec.md` asks, for the new pick-id prefix and the key `ui.strip_open_view`: what an empty, a duplicated and a colliding value gives.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Open View is the third item of the strip menu's first group, after Open File... and New Text File: the group holds the menu's openers, and VS Code's editor group menu has no Open View item to place it by.
2. The item and its picks live in `strip/open-view.svelte.ts`, a client-reading module beside `close-question.svelte.ts`: `PaneView.svelte` has 10 lines of room, and `stripMenu` stays a function of the facts it is handed.
3. The views of the section with no label are listed in the Open View submenu after the group submenus, with no heading, as the Views view lists them after its groups with no heading. On the tree today that section is empty: every listed registration names a rail page or `float` (`grep -rhoE "^\s+group: '[^']+'" src/lib` over the view registrations).
4. Documents of `docs:` are left out: the ruling names views, and a document opens through Open File... and the Views view.
5. A float has no name, so its hint is the floating pane's accessible name, `ui.float_label` ("Floating pane"); no new text.
6. A locked pane takes the view: Lock Group keeps out tabs opened from elsewhere (`docs/HELP.md:72`), and a pick from the pane's own menu is opened in that pane on purpose.
7. Float is listed as a group after the rail pages, though it is no rail page: the views of the shipped float register under `float` (`config/defaults/layout.yml:26-27`), the Views view lists them under Float, and leaving the group out would leave those views out of the menu.
8. "The tab strip's right-click menu" is the menu of the strip's empty area, which is the empty pane's menu too (`stripMenu`); a right-click on a tab keeps the tab's own menu, as VS Code's tab context menu is apart from its editor group menu.
9. An app's view carries its container's title as its hover words: the Views view tells two views of the same name apart by the container subtitle above them, which a submenu cannot draw, and the `hint` is kept for where a view is held.
