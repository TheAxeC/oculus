## What is on the tree (read on main at 7fd52a0e)

- `src/lib/server/hpc/ssh.ts` line 27: `export const KILL_GRACE_MS = 2000;`, used at line 41 as the delay before the escalation.
- Seen failing under load on 2026-09-16 (M2's round, M2's landing, one of three runs alone) and again on 2026-09-17 at load averages of 14 to 32 during this plan's parallel runs; green every time it is run alone on an idle machine (five runs on 2026-09-17, 5 of 5 each).
- The case's own comment says the poll window is "the grace itself, plus a second for the signal and the reap, and not a round number that would still pass if the escalation were slower than the constant says".

## What to build

1. **The case holds under load without widening what it proves.** The rule it exists for is that a command ignoring SIGTERM is killed within the grace after the budget, measured from when the escalation was due, not from when the test's own timer happened to run. Make the case measure that: take the moment the rejection landed, and hold the kill to `KILL_GRACE_MS` after it plus the time the machine took to give the test its turn, which the case measures rather than assumes (for example by timing a known-length sleep in the same run and using it as the machine's slack).
2. **The same for its sibling** ("never returns at the budget and leaves no child behind").
3. **No constant widened.** `KILL_GRACE_MS` stays 2000, and a slower escalation than the constant says still turns the case red. Prove that with the revert: raise the escalation delay in `server/hpc/ssh.ts` above the grace and show the case red under the new measurement as well as under the old.
4. **The load the case is proven at is stated**: run it with the machine loaded (a background load you make in the test's own scratch folder and stop afterwards, or the plan's own parallel runs if they are in flight), and say in the report at what load average the case passed, from `uptime`.
