## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. HELP.md's length is at most 450 lines and a bullet at most three sentences. The plan says "short bullets" and names no number; 450 lines keeps HELP.md about half the 880-line limit with room for later steps, and three sentences keeps a bullet readable in one look. The kept content of seven guides that today hold some 870 lines of long paragraphs fits that bound only as the selection rule of item 2 cuts it, which is the purpose of the step.
2. The Apps guide is at most 350 lines, with its reference parts kept whole in substance (every key, method, channel, refusal and limit) and its tour and large example cut. An app is built against the bridge, so the reference is what a builder of an app needs and the tour is what it does not.
3. DATA-MAP's "oculus configuration files" section becomes the hub's layer, `.oculus/config/`, with its four files and what each holds for this hub, and one sentence naming HELP.md's Configuration section for oculus's own settings and the keys the layer may set; the other rows and sections that describe oculus's own configuration files go with it (item 4).
