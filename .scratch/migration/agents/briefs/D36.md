## What to build

When the step is done, saving a file a linter takes, or showing a source tab of such a file with no unsaved text, runs that linter on the file on disk in the background; its findings are Monaco markers in every editor of the file on the page and rows of the Problems view under the file, with VS Code's severities, sources and codes; a quick fix, a disable comment and the rule's documentation are offered where the linter gives them; a linter that is missing, failing, not approved or switched off says so on the status bar and never throws.

1. **The program runner, once.** `run` and its types move from `src/lib/server/content/latex/tools.ts` to a new `src/lib/server/core/program.ts` (core 9 to 10 items), with `onPath` and D43's `programPath` from `content/latex/recipes.ts`; `tools.ts`, `recipes.ts`, `endpoint.ts`, `synctex.ts`, `texdoc.ts`, `server/machine/pty/latex/latex.ts` (its import at :54), `server/machine/lsp/adapters/texlab/adapter.ts` (:15) and `adapters/texlab/links.ts` (:17) import them from there and behave as today. `run` gains three things: the option `findings`, the exit codes whose stdout is the answer (`done` with the code); the option `signal`, an `AbortSignal` of the server's own that kills the child and answers `stopped`; and the answer `overflow` when the output passes the output limit (`tools.ts:19`, 16 MiB), which today reads as `timeout` because `execFile` kills the child (`tools.ts:55`). The head comments of `tools.ts`, `program.ts` and `synctex.ts:6` are rewritten.
