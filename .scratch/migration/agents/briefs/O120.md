## What to build

1. **The meta row takes no height when nothing in it is drawn at rest.** In the shared card. The two controls of a record card carry one attribute, `data-card-control`: the strip in `CardActions.svelte` and the opener in `RecordCard.svelte`. `.face` becomes `position: relative`. Under `@media (hover: hover)`, `Card.svelte` holds one rule for a `.meta` with no child other than such a control: `position: absolute; top: 8px; left: 12px; right: 12px; margin: 0; gap: 0`, so the row's box is the face's content box on a card with a bar and one without, `measure` reads that width as its line, and the controls stay at the line's end. On a screen that does not hover the controls are drawn at rest, so the row stays in the flow, as now. The row itself takes no pointer, so the title under its empty part stays pressable; a control in it does. A control in such a row paints solid `var(--card)` in every theme, a wallpaper included, as the small controls of `tokens.css` stay solid, so the words under it do not run through it. The row's `gap` is 0 so no slit of the title shows between the strip and the opener; `measure` reads the gap from the page. The rule reads whether a child exists, in CSS, so no kind says anything: no prop, no JavaScript, no measure. `data-card-actions`, which `git grep -n data-card-actions -- src tests` shows nothing reads, is replaced by the new attribute.
2. **A row with something drawn at rest stays as it is.** A card with a chip, a Tag, a hub's mark or a tag keeps its row in the flow, the controls beside the chips as now.
3. **Hovering moves nothing.** On a card covered by item 1, the card's height, and so the place of every card below, is the same at rest, under the pointer, with the focus inside, with a menu open and under the keyboard cursor. At rest the card loses the 6px margin of the empty row, which is the one height change a reader sees.
4. **Career's colours.** `career-view.ts` chooses them: each row of `GROUPS` gains `tone`, and a `STRATEGY_TONE` export names the strategy card's, as a hue name of the palette. `CareerGroup` carries `tone`. `CareerCard.svelte` takes `tone` and hands it to `RecordCard`. `Career.svelte` passes `STRATEGY_TONE` to the strategy card and `group.tone` to each row's card. Strategy indigo, positions blue, degrees violet, awards gold, references teal, profiles pink. No key enters the career file's schema, nothing reads a colour from the file, and the hub's `career.yml` is not edited.
5. **Comments and pages.** The head comments of `Card.svelte`, `RecordCard.svelte`, `CardActions.svelte`, `Career.svelte`, `CareerCard.svelte` and `career-view.ts`, and the comment on `--card-at-rest`, say what is true after the change. `docs/HELP.md:347` says each list of the Career view has its own colour. `docs/HELP.md:301` adds that a card whose row holds nothing at rest draws the strip and "..." over its top right corner.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The hues: strategy indigo, positions blue, degrees violet, awards gold, references teal, profiles pink. Six hues all 29 theme blocks define, far enough apart to tell the lists by, and none of red, amber or green, which oculus's lists read as a state.
2. The rule lives in CSS on `:has`, so no kind declares its row empty. A kind whose `meta` draws an empty wrapper element keeps its row in the flow and so keeps the jump, and bare text in a `meta` would not count as a child; no snippet on the tree does either, and the comment on the rule says so.
3. Controls over a long title cover its corner under the pointer, which the ruling's "over its top right corner" accepts. Reserving the corner at rest would narrow every title.
4. The rule stands only under `@media (hover: hover)`: without hover the controls are drawn at rest, the row never jumps, and laying it over the title would cover words for good.
5. The controls paint solid on a wallpaper too: words read through them would be worse than a small solid box.
6. The empty row's 6px goes at rest as well as under the pointer: a row that holds nothing takes nothing.
