## What to build

When this step is done, every deck has a frame size, 1280 by 720 unless the settings or the deck say otherwise, and everything that draws, measures or prints a slide uses its deck's frame. A slide taller than its frame is a "Slide too tall" finding in the Findings view and is marked on its Overview tile, and a slide with no title is a finding of the deck reader.

1. **The setting.** `config/defaults/present.yml` gains, under `defaults:`, `frame: { width: 1280, height: 720 }` with a comment saying it is the size in px every slide is laid out in, which a deck's `deck.frame` overrides. `config/options/present.yml` describes `defaults.frame.width` and `defaults.frame.height`, each `control: number`, `whole: true`, heading `presentation`, a section of its own (`Slides`), the width from 320 to 3840 and the height from 240 to 2160, and a line for the group `defaults.frame` under `groups:`. The frame is read by a setting reader of its own, in a new file `src/lib/data/sources/config/settings/present.ts` that reads both keys through `settingReader(config, problems, <present.yml's options file>)` and is listed in `PROBLEM_READERS` (`problems.ts`), so `defaults.frame.width: 10` is a finding naming the file and the key, and the default is used. The folder `settings/` is at 10 items, so it is split as the rules file says: files that share a concern move into a folder named for it (for example `latex.ts` and `lint.ts`, the writing tools' settings, into `settings/writing/`), with every importer carried and the rename grep quoted. The merged config's frame reaches the deck reader the way `tagTones` does (`data/index.ts:245,343` and `server/content/deck/read.ts:96`, a hub other than the primary taking its own config's frame), read through that reader and nowhere else. The ranges have one source, the options entries' `min` and `max`: the deck reader's check of `deck.frame` (item 2) uses the same rule, handed to it in its context beside the frame, never a second copy of the numbers. `data/index.ts` stays at or under 880 lines.
2. **The deck's key.** A deck's `present.yml` may hold `deck.frame: { width, height }`, a map of exactly those two keys, each a whole number in the ranges of item 1. The deck reader resolves each deck's frame: the deck's own when it is valid, else the setting's. A `deck.frame` that is present and not such a map (a side missing, a side out of range or not whole or not a number, an extra key, `null`, a text) is a finding of the deck reader on the key `deck.frame` (a new string under `findings.deck` in `config/text/findings.yml`, saying what was wrong and that the setting's frame is used), and the setting's frame is used. `config/schemas/deck-schema.yml` gains `frame` under `deck` (type `raw`, its help saying `{ width, height }` in px, overriding the setting `defaults.frame`). The `Deck` type (`model-deck.ts`) carries `frame: { width: number; height: number }`, and the page's wire shape `deckShape` (`present.ts:459-472`) holds it to two whole numbers greater than zero, so a deck answer with any other frame is refused as malformed and no frame text reaches a style, a window's features or the bridge. A member deck of a group is drawn in the deck that holds it, so its own `deck.frame` is not used for drawing; an invalid member `deck.frame` is still a finding, since the reader reads every member's `present.yml`. The `createReader` context literals of `tests/checks/config/integrity.test.ts:102-109,329-335` and `tests/data/sources/present.test.ts:166-175` gain the frame where the context requires it, and nothing else in those tests changes.
3. **Drawn at the deck's frame.**
   - `FRAME_WIDTH` and `FRAME_HEIGHT` are removed; `fit` takes the frame. `Stage.svelte` takes the frame as a prop and lays out, scales and sizes the frame from it; every caller passes its deck's frame (the pane, both slides of the presenter screen, the Overview tile, the show route).
   - The presenter's next-slide room and the tile's shot take the frame's aspect ratio in place of `16 / 9`.
   - `--slide-reading` (`slide.css:11`) is the frame's width less the frame's side padding, set where the frame is drawn, so a 1024-wide frame's text column fits inside it; `Slide.svelte:5` says where it is set.
   - The browser's audience window opens at the deck's frame: the `window.open` features (`present.svelte.ts:102,367-371`) carry the frame's width and height.
   - The desktop app's audience window takes the deck's frame from those features: the main window's open handler (`windows.ts:233`) hands the `features` text along the route to `show.ts`, the address staying a root and a path (G47); with one display, `audiencePlace` (`place.ts`) opens the window 960 px wide and as high as the frame's aspect gives, rounded, and features without a whole width and height give 960 by 540; with a second display the window fills it as today.
   - The print route sets its page and each `.page` to the deck's frame (the `@page` rule written from the deck, for example in a `<svelte:head>` style).
   - The print bridge takes the frame with the address (`preload.cjs`, `bridge.ts` `Printer` and its handler, `desktop/app.ts`, `stores/desktop.ts`, `export.ts`); the bridge answers `refused` for a frame that is not two whole numbers greater than zero, as it does for an address it will not take, and opens no window; the hidden print window opens at that frame (`desktop/windows/print.ts`).
   - The frame handed to the audience window and to the print bridge is that of the saved deck, the answer `present.deck(ref)` holds, since the show and print routes read the saved file; a draft that changes `deck.frame` reaches them when it is saved.
   - `Present.svelte` stays at or under 520 lines.
4. **Slide too tall.**
   - Each Stage drawn in the workbench measures the slide it draws: the slide is too tall when the frame's content is taller than the frame (its `scrollHeight` above its `clientHeight` by more than 1 px), measured at the frame's own size, so a scaled tile measures the same as the pane. A frame with no height (a hidden tab, an ancestor with `display: none`) is not measured and changes nothing.
   - It is measured again when the content's size or the frame's size changes (an image or a formula that loads late, the text size changing, the deck's frame changing), with a `ResizeObserver` on the frame and its content, disconnected when the Stage goes away.
   - The Stage reports its measure through a prop its caller passes; the show route passes none and measures nothing.
   - The measures go to the Present store (`present.svelte.ts`, one map per client, read by every tab's copy of the store): by deck (root and path) and slide key, the px the slide is over and the frame. A slide measured fitting leaves the map; when a deck's answer arrives again, its slides no longer in it leave the map. A Stage that goes away leaves its measure in the map, so the finding stays until a measure finds the slide fitting, the deck no longer holds it, or the page is reloaded.
   - The store answers its findings, one per deck and slide too tall: `{ file, key, line, message }`, `file` the absolute path of the slide's `source` (the root's `dir` joined with `source.path`, the root found in `roots()`), `line` its `source.line`, `key` the slide's key, and the message a new string under `findings.deck` naming the slide's title, the px it is over and the deck's frame. A summary or a grid is the same, its file the deck's `present.yml` and its line its `order:` entry's. A measure whose root the page does not know gives no finding until the root is known. Two Stages drawing one slide of one deck (two panes, the pane and the presenter screen, the tile and the pane) give one finding; one slide drawn in two decks gives one finding per deck.
   - `Findings.svelte` and `findingsCount` (`rail-badges.svelte.ts:219-224`, the Present store handed to `createRailBadges` in `context.ts`) both read those findings beside `registry.refused()` as the page's own findings, so the count and the list agree; `context.ts` stays at or under 880 lines.
   - The Overview tile of a slide too tall carries a mark, a glyph from `config/catalogs/glyphs.yml` and a short word from the Present view's strings, with the finding's message as its tooltip.
   - `present.svelte.ts` stays at or under 880 lines; no file is added to a full folder.
5. **No title.** The deck reader raises a finding (a new string under `findings.deck`, naming the slide by its file and its place in the file) for a slide with no title of its own: a slide of the kinds `title`, `closing`, `section`, `text`, `image`, `columns`, `equation` or `code` with no level-1 heading; a `project` slide with no `title:` in its front matter whose project is missing or has an empty short name; an `idea` slide with no `title:` and no card name, short name or title from a project. The finding's key is `title`, its file the slide's file (absolute, as every finding's) and its line the slide's line in that file; the reader gains a raise on the slide's file that carries the line, and the deck-level raise keeps its shape.
6. **Every sentence true.** The comments listed under "What is on the tree" say the deck's frame, `Presenter.svelte:185` and `Slide.svelte:5` among them. `docs/HELP.md:331` gains the facts, each true as built: a slide is laid out in its deck's frame, 1280 by 720 px unless the deck's `deck.frame` or the setting `defaults.frame` says otherwise; a slide with no title is a finding of its deck, listed under Findings for the portfolio deck and the projects' decks; a slide the Present view has drawn taller than its frame is listed under Findings and marked on its Overview tile, until it fits, leaves the deck or the page is reloaded. `docs/HELP.md:570` names `defaults.frame` beside `defaults.tag_tones`. The `present.yml` template (`docs/templates.md:1910-1929`) shows `frame` under `deck:`, commented, with a comment saying what it sets.

Each file stays under the size rule of `agents/spec.md`; every control is a `ui/` primitive, every string a catalog line, every hue a token.
