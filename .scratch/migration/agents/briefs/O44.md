## What to build

### Part 1, the header toolbar holds one layout (Step 0)

What must hold: for a given pane width, a header toolbar draws one layout and keeps it. `measure()` run again after its own render gives the same `shown` and the same opener; no frame-to-frame flip in any view that draws one of the 24 files' toolbars (Calendar and Inbox through `AccountActions.svelte`, Leads, Radar and the registries through `SourceToolbar.svelte`, and the rest), at any text size and under any icon set. Axel's "Project" view is the Projects board (`Projects.svelte:251`, a `+` and the expand control in an overflow toolbar); `Project.svelte:107` draws no header toolbar.

- `src/lib/ui/frame/toolbar/Toolbar.svelte`: widths and room are read in one fractional unit (`getBoundingClientRect().width` for the controls, the opener probe and the room), never `offsetWidth` against `clientWidth`.
- The room the arithmetic reads does not depend on whether the opener is drawn: it is the width the toolbar's container allows the toolbar, read from outside the toolbar's own content box, so drawing or removing the opener cannot change it. Inside a Pane header that is the width `.tools` may take (`max-width: calc(100% - 3ch - 8px)` of the header row, less the header's padding and gap); a toolbar outside a Pane header (`DiffBar.svelte`, `SourceToolbar.svelte`, `Controls.svelte` of the Files header and the others of the 24) reads its room the same way from its own container. You choose the mechanism (a CSS change to `.tools` and `.toolbar.overflow` that gives the toolbar a box sized by its container, or a read of the container's available width in `measure()`), and the report quotes the line that reads the room and states why that value cannot change when the opener is drawn. A read of the toolbar's own box (`root.getBoundingClientRect()` or `root.clientWidth` while the root is sized by its content) does not meet this, whatever its unit.
- `fit.ts` stays a pure function of widths; a change of its contract is reflected in its head comment.
- `watch.ts` keeps watching what can change the room; if the room is now read from the container, the container is what it observes.
- The header's look does not change otherwise: the controls stay right-aligned, 4 px apart, in `--header-action` boxes; the title keeps the width the controls leave it (never cut to three characters while the controls leave it more); the popover behaves as its head comment says.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)
