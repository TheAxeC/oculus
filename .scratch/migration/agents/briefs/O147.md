## What to build

1. **The kept projects reach the deck reader.** The deck route takes the projects a filter keeps, and the reader narrows every group to them.
   - `GET /api/deck` takes an optional parameter `keep`, a JSON array of project keys; `POST /api/deck` takes an optional `keep` in its body, the same array. Absent means no filter. A `keep` that is not an array of non-empty strings, or holds more than 1024 of them, is answered 400 with `{ error: 'invalid' }`, the word every 400 of the route answers (`endpoint.ts:46`, `:66`, `:69`), never a silent whole deck. Duplicates and keys no project has keep nothing extra.
   - The keys are the bare project keys of the deck's own hub, `DeckProject.id` as the reader already holds it (`ofHub` at `server/content/deck/read.ts:95` gives the bare key, `hub-names.ts:98-102`). The reader's projects are those of the deck's hub alone, so the bare key is unique there. `DeckProject` gains no field.
   - With `keep` given, a member of a group of project decks is kept when its project's key is in `keep`; a member that is no project's deck is left out while a filter is in force. A group of project decks is a group at least one of whose members or candidates, before any filter, is a project's deck. The narrowing applies in every group the request reads, a member deck's own groups included.
   - Everything built from a group's members follows the kept members only: the slides a `{ slides: <group> }` entry draws, `{{<group>.count}}`, the summary rows, the grid cards, the domain legend and `DeckGroup.count`.
   - The answer's `groups[].members` stay every member, each `DeckMember` gaining `projectId` (the project's bare key, absent for a deck that is no project's; named so, since `project` already names a `DeckProject` on `Doc` and display words on `DeckSummaryRow`) and `kept` (true for every member without a filter). A candidate carries `projectId` and `kept: true`, since candidates are not filtered; `missing` and `conflicts` are not filtered.
   - Groups of decks that are no project's are not narrowed. The write route's reads (`src/lib/server/edit/writes/config/lists/deck/deck.ts:212`, `deck-files.ts:143`) pass no `keep` and read every member, as today.
2. **The pane's filter.** The Present view holds a filter in the pane's saved state and reads its deck through it.
   - `PresentState` gains `filters`, a map from a group id (`stage`, `status`, `tier`, `pillar`, `form`, the boards' facet keys and the Map's `STAGE_FACET`) to the picked values, read by `parsePresentState` with an empty map for anything malformed, and written through `setInstanceState` like `overview`. `leftDeck` drops `filters`, so a pane that opens another deck opens it unfiltered. The file is never written: no write of `present.yml` or of any slide file comes from the filter.
   - The groups are Stage, Status, Tier, Pillar and Form, in that order, with the boards' words and rules: Stage is the Map's `stageGroup`; Status, Tier, Pillar and Form are the board facets `status`, `tier`, `pillar` and `form` through `facets` and `facetValues`, as `collection/bar.ts` builds its Projects section. A value is counted over the member projects the other groups' picks leave, as on a board; a group with no value draws no group, and a picked value no member holds any more is listed at 0 so it can be taken out, as on a board. The group rules are taken from `src/lib/state/boards/` (exporting what `collection/bar.ts` holds, such as `projectGroups`, rather than copying it), where they live; the view holds only the pane's wiring.
   - The values are listed from the member projects of the deck's groups of project decks, unfiltered, made into board items as `Collection.svelte:177-198` makes them, restricted to the board items of the deck's hub (`deck.hub`) and matched to a member by its bare key (`DeckMember.projectId`), so a project of another hub with the same key is never matched.
   - The kept projects are the board items of the deck's hub that every group with a pick keeps, by their bare keys; no pick in any group is no filter, and the pane asks for the whole deck without `keep`. While picks are held and the board items are not yet read (`board()` is empty before the first snapshot), the pane sends no request with an empty `keep`; it asks once the board items are held.
   - The pane's saved deck query and its drafted deck both carry `keep`, and the query key holds the kept list, so a filtered and a whole answer are held apart. Every read of that query uses the same key: the read (`present.svelte.ts:273`), `prune` (`:339`) and `savedFrame` (`:419`), so a filtered pane keeps its measures (O146) and its audience window keeps its frame. The Overview reads the pane's deck through the same filtered query and opens no second whole-deck query (`Overview.svelte:60-67`).
   - Changing a pick, or Clear, does not move the pane off the slide it is on when that slide is still in the deck; a slide the filter removes moves the pane to the first slide, as a deck that loses the slide on screen does today.
3. **The bar.** The pane draws the filter with `views/shared/forms/bar/FilterBar.svelte`.
   - The bar holds the five groups and no search box and no Mine only: `FilterBar.svelte` gains a way to draw neither (its `search`, `placeholder`, `onsearch`, `named`, `mine`, `pressed` and `onmine` made optional, the search and Mine drawn only when given), and every other caller draws as today.
   - The bar is drawn only when the deck holds a group of project decks; a deck opened from Files that holds none shows no bar, and no space is kept for it.
   - While a filter is in force, the toolbar (`Bar.svelte`) shows the line "Filtered: n of m decks" ("Filtered: 1 of 1 deck" when m is 1, a key of its own as the catalogs keep `count_one`) and a Clear button. m is the number of distinct member decks of the deck's groups of project decks, a project's deck counted once by its `projectId` and any other by its path, so a deck in two groups counts once; n is the number of those kept. Clear empties every pick. With no filter, neither the line nor Clear is drawn.
   - Every word is a string of `config/text/views/academic/present.yml`, as the view's other words are.
   - `Present.svelte` is at 520 lines, the component limit, and `src/lib/views/academic/present` holds 10 items: what the filter adds to the pane goes into a component or module of a subfolder with room (`acts` 5, `screen` 4, `arrange` 7, `presenter` 1), and `Present.svelte` ends at or under 520.
4. **Every place that draws the deck draws the filtered deck.**
   - The pane, the Overview and the presenter screen draw the deck the pane reads, filtered.
   - The Overview's group tiles are the kept members (`decksOf`, `src/lib/state/life/arrange.ts:99-106`), and a group tile's count (`Group.svelte:134`) is the kept members'. A move or a removal made while a filter is in force writes the group's whole `slides:` list, the members the filter hides kept in their places: a tile dropped beside a kept tile goes beside it in the file's list, and Move Earlier and Move Later (`deckStepped`, `arrange.ts:288-305`) step a kept tile past the next kept tile, the hidden members between them staying where they are in the file's list. The Add tile lists the group's candidates as today.
   - The audience window and the PDF export draw the deck as the pane filters it. Their addresses stay `?root=&path=` and nothing else, under G47; the kept list reaches them over the deck's `BroadcastChannel` (`presentName(ref)`), in messages added to `messageShape` (`present.ts:526-534`) with these kinds:
     - `keep-asked`, sent by the audience page when it opens and by the print page before it reads the deck;
     - `keep`, `{ kind: 'keep', keep: string[] | null, at: <the export's start, ms> }`, the answer, and sent by the presenting pane whenever its kept list changes; `null` is no filter.
   - The audience: the kept list travels in the presenting store's link (`PresentLink`, `present.svelte.ts:299-303`), computed from the pane's saved state and the selectors as `current` is, so the presenting store answers `keep-asked` even while the pane is not drawn (`Present.svelte:50-51`).
   - The PDF: from the press of Export until the print page has its answer, or for 10 s, the exporting pane holds the deck's export under a Web Lock named for the deck's export and answers `keep-asked` with its kept list and the moment of its press; this holds on the browser-tab path too, where `exportPdf` returns right after opening the tab. A second press of Export on the same deck while the lock is held, from any pane, does nothing and says "An export of this deck is already in progress", a string of the view's text file. The print page takes the first `keep` answer and waits at most 5 s for it, well inside the host's 60 s (`READY_WAIT_MS`, `src/lib/data/file-ops.ts:54`, counted from before the load); on no answer it sets `data-failed`, says so on the page, and prints nothing filtered by guess.
5. **The docs.** The head comments of every changed file and the Present paragraph of `docs/HELP.md` state the filter as built. `docs/templates.md`'s `present.yml` section says the filter is the view's and writes nothing.
6. **Tests.** The reader's narrowing (counts, summary, grid, legend, slides, nested groups, members' `kept` and `projectId`) is a test under G32 (`guarded.md:276-278`), rows of `tests/data/sources/present.test.ts`, in an `it` titled for the narrowing, its failing run on the unchanged tree quoted. No guarded entry covers `parsePresentState` or the route's 400, so no test is written for them; C6 and C10 are runs the report quotes. `tests/server/desktop/window-guard.test.ts` is not changed and passes. The orchestrator names the narrowing's test in G32's Test line at landing.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The filter is applied on the server, by the kept project keys, because the counts, summaries and grids are built there; the facet rules stay on the client, the one place the boards apply them, so the Present bar keeps a project by the same rule a board does.
2. `keep` is a JSON array of bare project keys of the deck's hub on the deck route, internal to oculus's own client and server; absent is no filter; at most 1024 keys; a bad one is the route's `invalid`.
3. The audience and print addresses keep their G47 shape, and the kept list travels over the deck's `BroadcastChannel`, as the audience window and the presenter screen already talk, in the two message kinds `keep-asked` and `keep`.
4. A member that is no project's deck is left out while a filter is in force, as a board keeps only projects that carry a picked value.
5. The answer's members stay whole with `kept` and `projectId` on each, so one answer gives the bar's values and the "n of m" line.
6. Moves made while filtered write the whole `slides:` list with the hidden members in place.
7. The bar has no search box and no Mine only, since the ruling names the five filters and nothing else.
8. A pane that opens another deck drops its picks, since a filter's values are those of one deck's members.
9. An export holds the deck's export for at most 10 s and the print page waits at most 5 s for the kept list; a second export of the same deck during that time is refused with a line, since the print address can name no pane.
