## What to build

When the step is done, the Sessions tab (and a Session tab, for what it shares) reads as one tree in the Files and Repositories look: the list, a line, then the sections, and the headings the step line names (the workspace groups, Terminals, Touched by this session, the repository, Merge, Staged, Changed and Untracked), Customizations' kinds and Details' groups each a group row like Repositories' Changed and Untracked, and Details a set of one-line rows. Nothing it shows now is dropped, and every control it has now is still reachable. Timeline's turn headings are entries of a log, not group headings, and stay as they are; the Agents section has no heading inside it.

1. **The divider.** In `SessionList.svelte`, a 1 px line in `--line` runs across the whole width of the pane between the list's fold and what follows it (the sections, or Customizations alone), with the spacing the tree's sections keep between them. It is drawn in the Sessions tab only.

2. **The list's group heads.** In `SessionWorkspace.svelte` and `TerminalRows.svelte`, the `GroupHead` strip is replaced by a group row of the Repositories look: a `TreeItem` at level 2 and depth 1, with the fold chevron (`expanded`), the group's words in normal case at the row's size (the workspace's name, the workspace-less words, or Terminals), the count as a badge at the right edge, the folder as the tooltip where the group has one, and a press folding it. The rows under it stand one step further in (level 3, depth 2). The fold keys, `listRows` and the arrow and Left and Right keys stay as they are. The heads are no longer sticky, and `.fold`'s `scroll-padding-top` and its comment are fitted to that. The head comments of both files and `session-list.ts`'s are made true. `GroupHead.svelte:57-58`'s list of the views that draw it drops the session list, and `pane-fold.ts:1-6`'s head comment says the session list's groups are tree rows it folds, not GroupHead groups.

3. **The change groups move to `views/shared/`.** Repositories' groups part, `ChangeRow.svelte`, `FolderRow.svelte`, `picking.ts` and `change-menu.ts` move into a folder under `src/lib/views/shared/changes/` named for what it holds, at most ten items per folder. `Changes.svelte` is split: the shared part draws the groups (Merge, Staged, Changed, Untracked) with their group rows, actions, right-click menus, the cut warning, folders in tree mode and `VirtualRows`, at a base level and depth the caller gives (the cut warning included); Repositories keeps its own `TreeSection` headed Changes around it, so the Repositories view draws exactly what it draws now. The keyboard selection of `Repo.svelte:135-175` (Shift+Up, Shift+Down, Cmd+A, Cmd+Enter, and the press selection by Cmd and Shift) moves into the shared folder as one piece both views use, so neither holds a copy. `change-tree.ts` gains the key rows of the groups alone, which `repositoryKeyRows` uses, so both views walk the groups with one function. `ChangeRow` takes an optional mark beside its path, drawn where the session passes one, so the session's rows keep `SharedMark`, and an optional session id that its press, its Enter and its menu's open items pass to `openChange`, so a session's row opens the diff for that session as now. Every import and the two test paths (`open-version.test.ts:9`, `keys.test.ts:444`) follow the move, and the tests keep passing unchanged in what they assert. The orchestrator rewrites G104's Code line at landing.

4. **The session's Changes section.**
   - "Touched by this session" becomes a group row (level 2, depth 1, folding, its count the number of files as a badge, no badge when there is none), its files as rows one step in (level 3, depth 2), the empty message under it when it has none.
   - The repository becomes a group row (level 2, depth 1, folding) headed with the words of `sessions.ch_repo` in normal case, its count the number of files in its change groups as a badge while it is readable and no badge while it is not. Under it: the message of a repository that is not readable (the five shapes as now), or for a readable one the branch line, the new-branch field, the commit box, then the change groups drawn by the shared part of item 3 at level 3, depth 2, then the busy line and the outcome as now.
   - The session's groups keep every control `ChangeGroups` gives now (stage, unstage, discard, mark resolved, the group actions, open, open file), now in the Repositories look, and gain what the Repositories view's groups have: the right-click menus, the selection of item 3 by Cmd and Shift presses and by the keys, acting on the selected rows of a group, list or tree mode, Compare with Workspace in a linked worktree, and virtual rows for a long group.
   - List or tree mode is chosen by a control on the Changes section's head, the one the Repositories view uses for it, with its glyph and words, and kept in the tab's state as decision 6 says.
   - Which group rows and folders are folded is kept in the tab's `folded` key through `usePaneFold`, as decision 5 says, so a reload, a switch of tab and a pick of another session keep them.
   - `SessionSection.svelte` takes key rows with parents and `open`, so the arrows walk the group rows and Left and Right fold them as in Repositories. The Changes section is one Tab stop: the arrows walk Touched, the repository and the groups, and the branch menu, the new-branch field and the commit box are reached by Tab and passed over by the arrows.
   - `ChangeGroups.svelte` is deleted, `change-actions.ts` with it if nothing else reads it, `ch_tree` is removed from `changes.yml`, and every line that names any of them is made true.

5. **Details.** `DetailsBody.svelte` draws rows of the tree in the Files look, under three group rows of item 2's look (level 2, depth 1, folding, no count, kept in the tab's state as item 4 says):
   - **Session:** title, folder (the cwd), branch, kind, status, pid, version, started, hook (its message as the tooltip).
   - **Model:** model, effort, mode.
   - **Usage:** context (the meter drawn inside the row, with "used / window" as now), tokens (in, out, thinking), prompt cache (its ttl, the cached share, the busts).
   - Each fact is one row (level 3, depth 2): its name muted at the left, its value at the right, the value cut with an ellipsis at the pane's width and given whole as the row's tooltip. A fact with no value is not drawn (an ended session's pid, an effort the transcript does not state). The meter is drawn in one of TreeItem's existing snippets (`end` with `trailing`, or `detail`), so `TreeItem.svelte` is not changed; a row that needs a part TreeItem lacks is handed back before it is built.
   - The controls become icon actions on the Details section's head, through `Section.svelte`'s `more`, shown as every section head's controls are: **Rename** opens the title row's value as a `NameField` in place, under the rule of decision 9. **Pin** opens a menu of the choices the select offers now: "(scored)" first, then the areas grouped by hub, the pinned one marked ("(scored)" for an unpinned session). **Resume** for an ended session asks once and runs on the second press, its tooltip the confirm words with the folder while armed; for a running session it is disabled, its tooltip the `sessions.resume_live` sentence. **Open the transcript** opens it as now.
   - The words a pin, a rename or a resume answers (saved, failed, the resume's error) stand in one muted row at the top of the section's body while there are any.
   - The `TextInput`, the `Select`, the Title and Clear Title buttons and the capital labels go. Every string they used that nothing reads any more is removed from `config/text/views/sessions/session-detail.yml`, `sessions.resume_unknown` and `sessions.resume_live` kept (read by `terminal-words.ts`); the three group names Session, Model and Usage are added there, and the action words use the keys that exist (`sessions.pin_label`, `sessions.rename`).

6. **Customizations.** Its kind rows in `Customizations.svelte` become group rows of item 2's look (level 2, depth 1, the count as a badge), their findings and listings one step further in; its fold state, tone and tooltip stay as they are.

7. **The pages.** `docs/HELP.md:340-341` say what the tab and the sections now look like and where Rename, Pin and Resume are; `docs/HELP.md:295` (Groups) is made true for the views whose groups are tree rows that do not stay at the top; `README.md` is read and changed only if a sentence becomes false. Every head comment of a changed file says what is true after the step, with no history.

Behaviour, before and after: before, the list runs into Details with no line; group heads are bold sticky bands; the Changes sub-headings are flat capitals that do not fold; Customizations' kinds sit at the left edge with a plain count; Details is a form. After, a line separates the list from the sections; every heading the step names is a group row with a chevron and a count badge that folds and remembers it; the session's change groups are the Repositories view's own; Details is three folding groups of one-line rows with its controls on its head; an empty title written in either rename field clears the title.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The repository's group row is headed "The repository" (`sessions.ch_repo`) in normal case, not with the folder's name, since the session's workspace is already named in its Details and its list group.
2. Rename opens a field in place of the title row's value in Details, in both the Sessions tab and a Session tab, rather than jumping to the list's row, since a Session tab has no list.
3. A fact with no value is left out of Details rather than drawn empty.
4. The head actions of Details show the way every section head's controls show (under the pointer or the focus), as in the Files and Repositories views.
5.
