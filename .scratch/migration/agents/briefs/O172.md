## What to build

1. `fieldMarkdown(text, math, memo)` in `src/lib/ui/html.ts`: one long field as block Markdown, for cards and record pages.
   - It parses with `gfm` and `breaks: true`, through `UNKEPT_TAGS_AS_TEXT` and, when `math` is given, `mathExtension` as `inlineDrawing` does. It does not use the document rule: no heading ids and no `[TOC]`.
   - The result goes through `sanitise`, with the formulas held apart as in `inlineDrawing`, so nothing reaches a card unsanitised.
   - It is memoised by the page's `HtmlMemo` under the same limit, in a place that keeps a block drawing apart from an inline drawing of the same text.
   - `inlineMarkdown` stays for titles and one-line values. Its head comment is rewritten so that it says what it is for now, since the sentence about a note being one paragraph is false after this step.
2. A new folder `src/lib/views/shared/card/text/` takes `Clamp.svelte`, moved with `mv`, and the new `FieldMarkdown.svelte`, so that `card/` stays at its limit. Every import and comment that names `Clamp.svelte` follows. `FieldMarkdown.svelte` is a component with the prop `text`: it makes its own `cardMath`, reads `useHtmlMemo()` and draws `fieldMarkdown(text, cardMath(text), memo)` in one `<div>`. Its styles, under that one root:
   - The size, weight and colour are those of the box it sits in.
   - Blocks are spaced by `margin-top: var(--field-gap, 0.45em)`, and the first block has none, so a field of one paragraph is as tall as it is on the unchanged tree.
   - A list has `padding-left: 1.25em` and items with no margin of their own.
   - A heading is drawn at `1em` in bold, so a `#` in a note never outgrows the card's title.
   - `code` has the look `CodeText.svelte` gives it, the wallpaper rule included. A link has the colour `LinkedText.svelte` gives it. A blockquote has the left rule of `EntryFields.svelte`'s `.stmt`.
   - A long unbroken word breaks (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`, `min-width: 0`). A `pre` and a `table` scroll inside the card and an `img` is at most as wide as the card, so nothing widens its column.
   - A press on a link inside it opens the link and goes no further, as in `LinkedText.svelte`. The listener is registered in an action or an attachment, with no `svelte-ignore`.
3. `Clamp.svelte`: `.text.cut` sets `--field-gap: 0`, so a cut text is whole lines and the last line is never sliced by a paragraph gap. The measure reads the text's height with the gap off in both states (the property set on the box and removed in the same read), so a text that fits is not called long when it is open. The control and the cut height stay as they are. Its comment on the children is rewritten: the text may hold blocks.
4. Every field listed under "What is on the tree" is drawn through `FieldMarkdown`, on the card and on the record page:

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The field rule is block Markdown with `gfm` and `breaks: true`, without the document rule's heading ids and `[TOC]`, since a card has no outline to link to. Five real cases from the hub, run on Marked 18.0.12 (a task note's words are withheld, as they are confidential):
   - `research/data.yml` notes, in: ``Rebuilt by `sbatch hpc/ward_waygal_prep.slurm` in ward-bttr-crosssubject; delete freely.`` Out: `<p>Rebuilt by <code>sbatch hpc/ward_waygal_prep.slurm</code> in ward-bttr-crosssubject; delete freely.</p>`.
   - `research/venues.yml` wants, in: `Methods + applications in biomedical & clinical informatics, EHR, clinical NLP, decision support, data standards, reproducible benchmarks.` Out: the same words in one `<p>`, with `&amp;`.
   - `pillars/domains.yml` blurb, in: `Core ML / tensor methodology not tied to one clinical field, operator learning, structured-tensor theory, federated/DP methods, evaluation.` Out: the same words in one `<p>`.
   - `academic/funding.yml` note, in: ``A ZonMw-funded project I contribute to, not my own grant. Acknowledgment text for HCC/HCCNet manuscripts is in `statement:` above.`` Out: one `<p>` with `<code>statement:</code>`.
   - `academic/funding.yml` about, in: `Role: Scientific coordinator (UHasselt) and AI Researcher` newline `Team: approx. 10 researchers across 4 institutions`. Out: `<p>Role: Scientific coordinator (UHasselt) and AI Researcher<br>Team: approx. 10 researchers across 4 institutions</p>`.
2. The drawing is one component, so that the markup, the spacing and the press on a link are written once rather than at seventeen sites.
3. The gap between blocks is `0.45em`, and none while a clamp cuts the text, so the cut falls on whole lines; a `pre` or a `table` inside the cut may be sliced by its own padding, which costs nothing.
4. `firstSentence` reads the first line and drops a leading block mark, so a one-line cut of a field written as a list does not show its `-`.
5. `radar.threatens` becomes a label without a placeholder, since a block cannot stand inside a sentence.
6. A focus holding a line break ends its own block before `fit`.
7. The fields under "Not changed" stay inline.
