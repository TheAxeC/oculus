## The rule the step ends

The Live HPC poll publishes every failed poll as `unreachable`, and the HPC view, the status bar and the page's error box then say "The cluster did not answer over ssh. Is this Mac on the UT network or on EduVPN?". That sentence is false when ssh connects and the cluster's Slurm does not answer: on 2026-10-26 the cluster's primary Slurm controller was down, ssh with the poll's own options connected in 0.2 s, `squeue --me` took 118 s and ended "slurm_load_jobs error: Unable to contact slurm controller (connect failure)", and the poll's 40 s timeout fired first. Ruling 428 (a) has the poll tell three failures apart, each with its own sentence, the last good poll kept on screen; Z1026 names the second one's status word `slurm_down`. Both are in `plan.md` Rulings.

## What to build

1. **Three failures told apart (ruling 428 (a), Z1026).** A failed poll is published with one of three statuses:
    The checks run in this order:
    - `unreachable`: ssh did not connect: the connected marker (item 2) is not in stdout and the runner resolved with exit code 255 or rejected with its own timeout; or there is no host (`:220-224`, unchanged). The hint is ssh's stderr line, or for a timeout the word the runner gives, in the `unreachable_detail` form as today.
    - `slurm_down`: the marker is in stdout and a Slurm command did not answer: `SEC_RC` is present and not 0 (its own error, or the remote `timeout`'s 124), or the runner timed out after the marker and before the Slurm commands' last section (`SEC_SINFO`) was printed. The hint is Slurm's own message, read from `squeue`'s stderr captured on its own (redirected into its own section, so an ssh warning or a login banner on the merged stderr is never the hint), or, for 124 or a runner timeout, a sentence saying Slurm did not answer within the limit.
    - `error`: anything else: a spawn error, a resolved answer the parser cannot read, `SEC_RC` 0 with no `SEC_SACCT`, the marker with exit 255 and `SEC_RC` 0 (the connection dropped mid-poll), or a runner timeout after `SEC_SINFO` (Slurm answered and the rest of the batch, Recover's section, ran past the limit), whose hint says the poll ran past its limit after Slurm answered.
    A resolved answer with code 0 and the Slurm sections is read as today, with or without the marker; the marker is read only to tell the failures apart.
    The last good rows and `lastOk` stay on every one, as `degraded` keeps them today; the retry cadence (`RETRY_MIN`) applies to all three.
2. **The poll ends before its own timeout when Slurm hangs.** Each Slurm command of `batchCommand` and `recoverCommand` runs under the remote `timeout`: `squeue` 12 s, `sacct` 8 s, `sinfo` 5 s, 25 s together, which leaves 15 s of the poll's 40 s runner timeout for the connect (ssh's `ConnectTimeout=8`) and Recover's section. The `echo "${SEC_RC}$?"` follows the timed `squeue` directly, with no pipe or subshell around it, so it records `squeue`'s or `timeout`'s code. The remote command prints a connected marker first. The runner's timeout rejection carries the stdout it held (in `ssh.ts`), its message still containing `timeout` so `actions.ts:73` reads it as today, and the poll reads that stdout. No time of a healthy `squeue` on the cluster is on file; the report states the limits and says so.
3. **`recover()` and the quota read** keep their behaviour: `recover()` runs only after a good poll, and a failed quota read keeps the last meters.
4. **The status word.** `HpcStatus` gains `slurm_down`; `docs/apps.md:421` lists it with one clause saying what each failure word means.
5. **The words.** In `config/text/views/system/machine/hpc.yml`: a short word for `slurm_down` beside `unreachable` and `error`, and its sentence, which says ssh reached the cluster and Slurm did not answer, with Slurm's message as the hint in the `unreachable_detail` form. `unreachable_msg` is said only for `unreachable`. `error` gets a sentence of its own in the error box. Every word through `t()`.
6. **The readers.** `Head.svelte`, `Hpc.svelte` and `hpcWords` in `status.ts` show the three: the pill's word, the error box's sentence with its hint, the Login node section's reason (`Hpc.svelte:111`), and the status bar's short word and its long sentence, each failure with a sentence of its own (the long text of `error` is a sentence, not the bare word); `slurm_down` counts as failed wherever `unreachable` does (the `bad` class).
7. **Tests.** The report also says, in one sentence, that a `Permission denied` or `Host key verification failed` answer is `unreachable`, whose sentence then asks about the network. No entry of `agents/guarded.md` covers the poll's status, and a wrong status word loses no work, breaks no installation and accepts no wrong configuration, so the cases are runs the report quotes, not tests. The G115 rows of `runs.test.ts` keep their meaning: the good fake (`:479`, no marker) still reads `ok`, and the down fake (`:503`, `code: 255`, no marker) still reads `unreachable`; neither fake is changed.
