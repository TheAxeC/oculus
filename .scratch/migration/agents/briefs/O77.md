## What to build

1. **A board card opens no more in place** (ruling 352). `Projects.svelte` drops `expanded`, `toggleCard` and the `open`/`ontoggle` it passes; a card on a board is always drawn closed. The `expanded` key is no longer read or written: a tab's saved state that still holds it is read as if it were not there, with no code that reads the old key (the rules file's rule against migration code). The doc of `storedState` (`instance.ts:109-111`) stops naming cards drawn open.
2. **Open project page** (ruling 352). On a board, the card's Open project page action, its title and Enter on the keyboard cursor open the project page beside the board as a preview tab. `openBeside` gains an optional fifth parameter, `OpenOptions` (`preview`), passed on through `layout.svelte.ts` and from `openInto` into `openOpened`, with the meaning `openView` gives it; its other callers are unchanged. So the page lands where `landingFor` places a view the board asked for (never the board's pane, a new pane split right of the board when no pane takes it), and the next project opened from the board replaces it in that pane's preview tab. A press on the project already shown activates it and keeps its tab, as a second click in Files does. Typing in the project page (a subtask, a follow-up or any form of the card) keeps its tab, so a half-typed form is never replaced. On the project page the card has no Open project page action, and its title does what it does today. The Focus lane cards keep `openView` as today (ruling 352 names the project boards).
3. **Add Subtask and Add Follow-up** stay on the board card and open their forms on the card (ruling 352), offered whether or not the project has subtasks or notes, since a board card no longer shows the blocks.
4. **The card's actions** (ruling 353 as 352 renamed it).
   - Under the pointer the card draws three icons, Ask, Open Workbench (where the hub is served) and Open project page (on a board, with the glyph `page`), then a "..." opener drawn with a new key `project.card.menu_glyph` whose value is `more`.
   - The "..." menu, and the same menu on a right-click anywhere on the card (and on the context menu key or Shift+F10 on the focused card), lists every action with the glyph it has today, in runs: a run headed Open with the rows Project Page (on a board), Folder, Show in Files, Workbench (where served) and Presentation (where the card has a deck); a run headed Add with Subtask and Follow-up; then, after a rule and with no heading, Ask, Edit Manifest, Pin to Top (Unpin while pinned), Copy Context, Deposit (where the card has it), and Clone or the Cloned-in entry (where the card has them). The row words are new keys of the text catalog in the words ruling 353 gives; the icons' tips keep today's words.
   - `CardActions.svelte` is not changed, so the nine other cards that use it keep their controls. `ContextMenu.svelte` gains the `groups` that `Menu.svelte` has, drawn by `MenuList.svelte` the same way, a group with an empty heading drawing none; its other users pass none and draw as before.
5. **The closed card** (rulings 356 and 356 (2), redone).
   - Next is drawn as the ELI5 is: a NEXT label in the ELI5 label's style and the sentence at the ELI5's size and colour, cut to its first sentence as today.
   - The review line ("under review at ...") is not drawn.
   - WAITING ON is a labelled line above FUNDING, in the style of the other labelled lines. Each value of `waiting-on:` is named by `waitingLabel` (`waiting.ts`), the one rule every surface names a waited-on value by: a person as a tag that opens the Person view, as the TEAM line draws one; a project as a tag that opens it, as the RELATED line draws one; a value that names neither as a tag of its key with no press. The tags are amber. The head comment of `waiting.ts` names the card's WAITING ON line in place of the card helper line.
   - BLOCKED is a labelled line above FUNDING, in red, with the words `project.helper.blocked_external` gives today, drawn while a chip carries the flag `blocked`; WAITING ON and BLOCKED are each drawn when each holds.
   - The facts row draws a blocked chip red.
   - The facts row ends with the folder's name and the glyph `folder`, the whole path in its tip, a press opening the folder as the Folder action does; a project with no folder (`folder` empty and no `workspaceFolder`) draws none. The folder path line is not drawn.
   - Lead, the lane, the stage chip, the VENUE line and the other labelled lines are drawn as now.
   - `waitingLine` and its re-export go, its parts moved to where the two lines read them; no code keeps the one-line form.
6. **The project page's card** (rulings 352 and 381 (a)). The card on the project page sets its blocks in two columns: left STATUS, LATEST, NEXT (the whole `next`), RESULTS, OUTCOME, IDEAS / AVENUES; right REPRODUCED, DISSEMINATED IN, FILES, SUBTASKS, FOLLOW-UPS. On the project page the first-sentence Next line under the ELI5 is not drawn, so NEXT is said once. In a pane too narrow for two columns of readable width the right column goes under the left.
7. **The Focus lane card** (rulings 378 and 382 (a)). It draws three more facts as marks after the status marks and before the name, in the order role, tier, venue: the role only where it is not Lead (`roleOf` of the project: Supervisor, Collaborator or Other), and only while the workspace names a `self` person; the tier only where it is not the first word of `tiers:` of the project's hub (`research` in oculus's `vocab.yml`); the venue's short name (the registry's `short`, else its name) on a project whose stage carries `placed`. No domain and nothing else. The lane card draws no review wait line ("Under review <venue>"); its people and blocked wait lines stay. HELP's Focus line says so.
8. **The Focus filter** (ruling 379).
   - The Focus pane's header holds one Filter action, a glyph button (`sliders`, the glyph of the board's Filter) with words in its tip, drawn pressed while anything is hidden. It opens a list that stays open on a pick, as the board's group lists do (`PickMenu.svelte`, or the same bits-ui Popover and Command), holding:
   - Mine only, a ticked row with the words `roles.mine` and tip `roles.mine_tip`, the rule `isMine` over the record's role fields, on at the start; drawn disabled when no record on the page would be hidden by it, with a new tip `focus.mine_all`, "Everything here is yours", and then not applied;
   - Role, a checklist of the roles of `roles:` that have a field, all ticked at the start; unticking a role hides the records whose role it is; the rows without a field stand under Mine only;
   - Tier, a checklist of the words of `tiers:`, all ticked, applied to project rows only;
   - Hub (item 9).
   - Mine only and Role are not drawn, and not applied, while the workspace names no `self` person, as the project boards do (`offersMine`, `state.ts:100-120`).
   - The choice is kept in the tab's instance state beside the folds, under one key `filter` holding `mine` (a boolean) and the unticked `roles`, `tiers` and `hubs` (lists of words), so a reload and a move of the tab keep it and a role, tier or hub added later starts ticked.
   - It applies to the whole page: the command center, the tasks, the Awaiting box, the band, the lanes and the Idea lane, each counting over the records the filter keeps. The board computes the filter once (a pure module under `src/lib/state/boards/`) and hands each section its records, so the sections take them from the board instead of each reading `portfolio()`.
   - A row whose record names no role field (a calendar or feed line, a lead, the career banner, a collection entry whose `schema:` declares no role field) is always shown under Mine only and Role; Tier applies to project rows alone.
   - Each section with rows hidden says how many on its heading line ("3 hidden"), drawn by `FoldHead` (the command center, the tasks) and `LaneHead.svelte` (each lane and band column). A filter that hides every project leaves the lanes drawn with their counts; the empty message `focus.none` stays for a portfolio with no projects.
9. **The Hub list** (ruling 380). One entry per hub of the workspace (`roots()` with `hub: true`), with its name, drawn only while the workspace has more than one hub; a record's hub is its `root`.
   - In the Focus filter, a checklist all ticked at the start, applied to every row that carries a hub (`root`); the career banner and the feed lines carry none and are always shown.
   - On a project board, a group of `FACETS` (`hub`) on line 1 of the bar after Venue, counted like the others (each value counted under the other filters in force) and, like every group, with nothing picked at the start, which keeps every hub; a pick keeps the picked hubs' projects. 11. **Size.** `Collection.svelte` is at 505 lines of 520 and `ProjectCard.svelte` at 473: what the step adds goes into partials or modules of their own where a file would pass its limit (the menu's runs as a pure builder beside the card, the Focus filter's rule as a pure module under `src/lib/state/boards/`, the Hub chips as a partial of the collection view), each with its head comment. ## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. On a board the title and Enter open the page beside as Open project page does, since ruling 352 rejected the page as a tab in the board's pane, which is what the title opens today.
2. "Replacing it there" is the preview tab of the pane `landingFor` gives the board's request, the mechanism a single click in Files uses: a page made kept (by typing, a double click on its tab, or a second press on its card) stays, and the next project takes a new preview tab in that pane.
3. Add Subtask and Add Follow-up are always offered, since a board card no longer shows the blocks that carried their own add control.
4. The menu's Open and Add runs carry headings, as `Menu.svelte` draws its groups; the last run has none. Ask and Open project page are in the menu too, and so are Clone and the Cloned-in entry, since the menu lists every action.
5. The "..." opener reads a new key `project.card.menu_glyph`, so the shared `project.card.more_glyph` of the nine other cards is not touched.
6. A waited-on value is named by `waitingLabel` on the card as on the strip and the lane cards, so one rule names it everywhere.
7. WAITING ON and BLOCKED are both drawn when both hold; the one-line rule of people over blocked was for one line.
8. The lane card's tier mark is shown for a tier other than the first word of `tiers:` of the project's hub, oculus's first being `research`, so a hub that lists its tiers in another order is read in its own words.
9. The Focus filter's rule is a pure module the board computes once, giving each section the records it keeps and the count it hid.
10. The career banner and the feed lines carry no hub and are always shown by the Hub list, as by Mine only and Role.
11. The board's Hub group follows the form of every group (nothing picked keeps every hub), which is the board's way of having every hub ticked; on Focus and the collection views the list is a checklist all ticked.
12. The Hub group sits on line 1 after Venue and folds into More by `fit()` like the others; its tone is `grey-d`.
13. The saved shapes: the Focus tab's `filter: { mine, roles, tiers, hubs }`, a collection's `hubs`, and the board's `hub` facet in its existing `filters`; each list holds the unticked words, so a new value starts ticked.
14. The Filter list on Focus stays open on a pick, as the board's group lists do.
