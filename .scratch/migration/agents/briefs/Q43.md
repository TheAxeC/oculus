- The pane mounts only its active tab, so component state is lost on a switch.

## What to build

1. **The open form in the instance state.** Which form is open (`root`, `workspace`, `bookmark` or none) and the bookmark being edited are fields of `FilesView`, written through the view's instance state, so a tab switch and a reload bring the same form back open.
2. **The line forms' text.** The text typed into the root and workspace line forms is kept in the instance state too, and cleared when that form closes or its action succeeds.
3. **Closing forgets.** Closing the bookmark form by its toggle, by another bookmark row's edit or by Cancel forgets its draft, through `toggleAdd` and `toggleEdit` of the form drafts store (the collection key the bookmark form uses), as the collection views do; a successful save and a delete behave as the entry form already makes them.
