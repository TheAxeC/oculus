## What to build

1. **Help (`docs/HELP.md`), written again from the code.** Help has two parts: the views, and the keys table under the heading "The keys". The views part has one entry per view a user can open: each component's own registration, listed or not, that a reader reaches (the Project, Person and Entry pages among them); the collection view and the project board once each, as the kind every `collections:` entry and every `boards:` row is drawn by; the apps' contributed views left out, since they come from apps and not from oculus. The shell's parts that are no registered view (the rail, pages and panes, workbenches, the palette, the status bar) each get one entry of the same form. Each entry says what the view is for in one sentence and then only what its screen does not show by itself: a behaviour a reader cannot see by looking (what a press does that its label does not say, where a write goes, what a key does in that view, what a setting changes). What the screen shows is judged from the view's markup and its strings under `config/text/views/`, read by the builder: nothing the screen already shows (a label, a column, a button's own words) is written. The keys table holds every key oculus binds whose meaning a reader needs, each row true of a binding in the code: a row `rows.ts` builds, or a key a view binds on its own box (the Present view's `screen/box.ts` and `screen/keys.ts`); the rows of ruling 706 are kept.
2. **The data map (`docs/data-map.md`), written again from the code.** For each hub file oculus reads (the registries, collections and feeds of `config/defaults/sources.yml`, the `project.yml` manifests its `scan:` finds, the settings files under a hub's `.oculus/`, and a repository's own `.oculus/` files: `project.yml`, `settings.yml`, `commands.yml`, `launch.yml`, `cpp.yml`, `app-data/<name>.json`): its path, its keys each with what it holds, and what links it to the other files (which key names an entry of which file). A second hub, the primary hub and the repositories below a hub stay only as far as they change which file is read, the nested-repos block among them.
3. **The decks guide (`docs/decks.md`), written again from the code.** A deck's files (`present.yml`, the slide files, the images), the keys of `present.yml` and of a slide's front matter, each with what it holds and its value when unset, and the slide kinds the reader accepts, each with one example of its file. The groups, the order, the filter and the project facts strip stay as keys and their behaviour.
4. **Every sentence true and needed.** Each sentence of the three pages is checked against the code that does what it says, and the report gives, per paragraph, list item or table row, the code read for it (`file:line`). A sentence of today's pages that is true and needed may stay; every other one goes. No behaviour the code does not have is described. A sentence that tells the reader nothing they can act on, repeats the screen, narrates the code, or says what something is not, is not written. Plain prose under the rules file's prose rule, ASCII, no hard-wrapping, one name for each thing.
5. **Every line that describes or cites the pages stays true.** The lines of "Words elsewhere that describe the pages" and "Comments that cite the pages" are each left true of the new pages: a line the rewrite makes false is changed to say what the page now holds, or its citation is dropped with the comment's own statement kept. `README.md:95` and `:97` describe scopes ruling 711 changes ("every view, key, setting and file"; "the views that show them"), and `README.md:99` and `docs/templates.md:1924` say "each with an example" of the keys as well as the kinds, where ruling 711 gives an example per slide kind only; all four are rewritten to the ruled scope.
6. **The draft goes to Axel before it lands.** The report gives, for each page, its headings and its line and word counts before and after. The orchestrator runs the writing review over the three pages and sends its findings in the repair round; Axel reads the pages and approves them before the step lands.
