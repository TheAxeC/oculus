## What to build

The plan's ruling "Open item 749": "(b). The Files view's tree measures become the default of every tree (`TreeItem.svelte`), so each level is 8px deep everywhere". The step line adds that the indent token `--tree-indent` stays as it is.

1. The Files tree's two measures become the theme's tokens: `--tree-guide-gap: 0px` and `--tree-guide-shift: calc(var(--icon-box) / 2)` in `tokens.css` beside `--tree-indent`. The comment there says what each is and that the gap is the one tree measure a row does not multiply by the text scale. `TreeItem.svelte` reads them with no fallback value in `var()`; its comment at `:365-370` says each level is the indent and twice the gap, which is the indent alone at the default gap of 0; its comment at `:259-261` names the two tokens among those an ancestor may set.
2. `Tree.svelte:491-496` sets neither measure; it keeps `--tree-end-inset` and its other rules, and its comment says what it sets.
3. The three Graph indents (`CommitFiles.svelte:250`, `CommitRow.svelte:239`, `GraphHead.svelte:80`) take the level formula `calc(var(--tree-indent) * var(--text-scale, 1) + 2 * var(--tree-guide-gap))`, as `ChangesRepo.svelte` does, and `ChangesRepo.svelte:139-142` reads the gap with no fallback. So a commit row and the graph head stand one level in from the section head, their twistie where a depth-1 row's twistie is, moved right by the lanes, and a commit's file row one level under its commit. Their comments say what is true.
4. Session rows and terminal rows of the Sessions list start where their level puts them, as in the Files tree: the transparent 2px border goes from `.row` in `SessionWorkspace.svelte` and `TerminalRows.svelte`, and the amber mark of a session waiting for input is drawn without taking width, at the row's left edge, 2px wide, and seen over the row's hover and selection tint. The comment at `TerminalRows.svelte:147` goes with its rule or says what is true.
5. Both components over 520 lines are split by concern, as `spec.md:48` says, so each ends at or under 520: the guide columns of `TreeItem.svelte` (the `guides` derived, the `each` that draws them and the `.guide` styles with their hover and focus rule) into `src/lib/ui/tree/row/Guides.svelte`, which TreeItem draws in the same place; and the `Props` type of `Tree.svelte` into `src/lib/views/shared/explorer/tree/control/tree-props.ts`, as `ui/tree/item-props.ts` holds TreeItem's. Each new file opens with the head comment `spec.md` asks for, and the head comments of `TreeItem.svelte` and `Tree.svelte` name where the moved part lives.

No comment the step writes carries a word of `spec.md:44`'s list ("still", "as before", "no longer" and the rest). Nothing else changes: the row height, the name's size, the twistie, the icons and `--tree-indent` stay as they are.
