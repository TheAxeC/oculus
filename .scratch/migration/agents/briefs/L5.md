## Axel's rulings (plan.md, L5 and its Step 0)

- The button: icon only, about 48 px wide, muted until hovered, the page on screen drawn in full colour with a 2 px accent bar on its left edge, the page's name as the tooltip, as VS Code draws its activity bar.
- The badge: one per button, the count of the first view of the page (in tab order, or the arrangement's order before the page is made) that has one, drawn as a small filled count on the icon's lower right corner, inside the button, as VS Code draws it; the page's other counts stay on their tabs.
- The bookmarks: one Bookmarks icon at the rail's foot, beside Search and Update, opening a menu of the bookmarks by name; a choice opens the bookmark as today.

## What to build

1. The rail is about 48 px wide under every theme. Each page button is an icon of the activity bar's size, with no box or border, muted (`--muted`) and brighter on hover; the button of the page on screen draws its icon in the text colour with a 2 px accent bar on its left edge. The name is the tooltip, with the digit that reaches it as today ("Focus, key 1"), and stays the button's accessible name.
2. One badge per button as ruled, in the badge's tone, inside the button's box over the icon's lower right corner, never outside it, with its hover words the view's badge title.
3. The foot: Search, Update and a new Bookmarks icon, each icon only with its name as the tooltip. Bookmarks opens a menu of every bookmark by name, through the existing menu primitive; a choice opens it as the rail's bookmark buttons did. With no bookmarks the icon is still drawn and its menu says there are none. The BOOKMARKS group is removed from the list.
4. The terminal (`rice`) themes keep the rail readable in their own way: whatever they draw, the width and the one-badge rule hold.
5. Tests, each red under its revert: the rail's width; a button's accessible name and tooltip; the active bar on the page on screen and not on another; one badge per button, the first counted view's, drawn inside the button's box (a browser case compares the badge's box with its button's); the Bookmarks menu listing the bookmarks by name and opening one; the old BOOKMARKS group gone; the keyboard ring still walking pages and the foot.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The rail width becomes the token `--rail-width: 48px`.
2. The Bookmarks icon uses the glyph the bookmark control already draws (`bookmark` or the document glyph, whichever the vocabulary names for a bookmark), and its menu entries carry each bookmark's label.
