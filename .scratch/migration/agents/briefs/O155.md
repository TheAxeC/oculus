## What to build

1. **One order rule for boards and decks, in layer 1.** The words of a category's order and how two projects compare under them (`stageRank`, `BOARD_SORT`, `byWord`) move from `presets.ts` to one file of `src/lib/data/shapes/boards/` (beside `categories.ts`, or in it while it stays under the size limit), exported as one function that compares two projects, each `{ stage?, year?, title }`, under a list of words and a stage order, with no pin. `compareCards` in `presets.ts` keeps the pin and calls it; `items.ts`, `facets.ts` and `collection/card-order.ts` import from the new place; nothing is re-exported from `presets.ts` for them. The boards order exactly as on the base (G124's tests pass unchanged).
2. **`deck_order` in `vocab.yml`.** `config/catalogs/vocab.yml` gets `deck_order: [publications, other-outputs, in-review, in-flight, released, idea, dead-ends]` after `categories:`, with a comment in the file's own form: the order a deck places the categories of `categories:` in, the decks of a category ordered by its row's `sort:` as on the boards, a category the list leaves out after the listed ones in the order of `categories:`, a key no row of `categories:` holds a finding. It is described in `config/options/catalogs/vocab.yml` (heading `boards`, section `Vocabulary`, `control: list`, its help saying the words are keys of `categories:` in the order a deck draws them), shaped in `catalog-files/vocab.ts` (a list of texts), given the merge shape `{ kind: 'whole' }` in `VOCAB_SHAPES` (a plain list replaced whole, since its order is its meaning), named in `HUB_LIST` and in `OWN_HUB_KEYS` so every hub may set its own, in the expected keys of `config-folder.test.ts`, in `docs/data-map.md` (the count and the key list), and in every list of the keys replaced whole: `vocab.yml:4`, `options/catalogs/vocab.yml:4`, `docs/templates.md:469-472` (with a commented example), `docs/HELP.md:586` and G126 (`guarded.md:192`). It follows the whole-list rule those places state: a hub's `deck_order` that is not a list of texts, or that names one key twice, is a finding naming the hub's file and oculus's list stands. A key of a hub's own `deck_order` that no row of the hub's merged `categories:` holds is one finding naming the hub's file and the key, raised for the primary hub and for every other hub (in `readOtherHub` too, whether or not the hub's file sets `categories`), and the key is not read. A key of oculus's own `deck_order` that the merged `categories:` lacks (a category a hub takes out with `{ key: <key>, removed: true }`) is left out silently, as the other word lists lose a word the hub takes out; no new source of `SOURCES` is added for it.
3. **The deck reader orders by it.** In `resolveGroups`, the decks `discover: true` adds are ordered thus, replacing `sorted`: first the decks whose project is in a category, the categories in the order of `deck_order` and then the categories it leaves out in the order of `categories:`; inside a category, by the row's `sort:` words and then `BOARD_SORT`, through item 1's function, under the hub's stage order; then the decks in no category (a deck of no project, or a project no row holds). The words compare the project's own `stage`, `year` and `title` (`DeckProject.title`, as the boards compare the project's title). A tie, and the order of the decks in no category (a deck of no project, a project no row holds, a project with no stage, since every row has a `stages` block), goes by the deck's folder path. A project's category is read by `categoryOf` over the hub's merged config, as the boards file it. No pin is read. The decks `slides:` names keep their place and their order. The candidates of the Add tile keep the finder's order. `DeckProject` gains `year?: number` (from `Project.year`, in `deckProject`), and `DeckContext` gains what the order needs (the categories, the resolved order of their keys, the stage order, the config the blocks are held against), built once in `deckReader` from `hub.config.merged`; the tests' own `createReader` inputs give it too.
4. **The group key `sort:` goes.** `GroupSpec.sort`, its read and `findings.deck.sort_unknown` go from `spec.ts` and `findings.yml`; `DeckKeys`' `sort` (`model-deck.ts:296`, `:301`, and `readKeys` in `spec.ts`), the head comment of `server/edit/deck-edit.ts:20-21`, the write's `sort` path (`deck-keys.ts`: `KEY_FIELDS`, `GROUP_KEYS`, `takes`, `takenWords`, the head comment) and `server.deck_slides.value_sort` go; `groupKey` takes `'find' | 'discover'`; the Edit Rule form loses its Sort field; `History.svelte`'s `DECK_KEYS` loses `'sort'`; the texts `present.field_sort`, `field_sort_folder`, `field_sort_help`, `field_sort_none`, `field_sort_of`, `field_sort_title` go; the deck schema loses the `sort` field and the `groups` help its "its sort"; `docs/templates.md:1927` goes; the demo fixture's `sort: folder` goes; the `deck-slides` test rows that write `groups.<group>.sort` go or become rows of another key where the row tests something else (each named in the report). No code reads or refuses a leftover `sort:` key in a group beyond what the schema check already does for any key the deck schema does not declare (`findings.schema.not_declared`); the hub's line goes at landing, as one change of the data.
5. **The rule sentence and the tip.** `ruleSentence` says whether the group adds what it finds, with no sort; its doc comment says so. The texts read, word for word:
   - `rule_adds: '{0}, and adds what it finds.'`
   - `rule_edit_tip: 'Set which decks {0} finds and whether it adds them'`
6. **The docs and comments.** `docs/decks.md` loses the `sort` key (`:119`) and its example line (`:88`), `:100` loses "and what `sort: title` orders a group's decks by", `:130` says the discovered decks follow the deck order (the categories by `deck_order`, inside a category by the row's `sort:` as on the boards, then the decks in no category, by folder), and `:139` names `find:` and `discover:`. `docs/HELP.md:337` says Edit Rule sets the group's Find and Discover; the Present line (`:331`) or the arranging lines say once how a group orders the decks it discovers, pointing at the boards' order of `:308`; `:308` names `deck_order` where it says the boards order a section's cards, so a reader of either finds the other. Every head comment and doc comment that names the group's sort, `sorted`, `byWord`'s place or `BOARD_SORT`'s place (`groups.ts`, `spec.ts`, `arrange.ts`, `deck-keys.ts`, `presets.ts:13`, `items.ts:8`, `facets.ts:13`, `categories.ts:1-8`) says what is true after the step. G124's Code line names the new file of item 1; G32's Test line names the order rows of item 7.
7. **The tests and their guarded entries.** The guarded entries take the deck order in their statements (the wording, taken in automode): G32 (`guarded.md:276`) says a deck's discovering group orders the decks it adds by `deck_order` and the rows' `sort:`, and its Test line names the order rows; G124 (`:186`) says a `deck_order` key no category holds and a key listed twice are findings, and its Test line names those rows; G126 (`:192`) names `deck_order` among the lists replaced whole.
