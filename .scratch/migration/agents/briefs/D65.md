## What to build

When the step is done, every project is one kind of record, read by one reader, written by one writer and drawn by one board component. A personal project is a project of the tier `personal`, with the manifest, the status, the card, the Focus lines, the search, Cmd-K and the relations of any project. A folder's place decides nothing: a manifest's own `tier` and `type` say which board and stage it is on. No code reads an older form of any stored data.

1. **The scan.** A `scan:` entry is `{ dir, optional? }`. Each `dir` is walked to any depth; a folder holding `project.yml` is a project and its subfolders are not walked, the scan folder itself included; dot folders, `node_modules` and symbolic links are not entered, and no other folder name is named in code. A missing `dir` is a finding unless `optional: true`. Two scan entries that reach one manifest (`projects` and `projects/ideas`) read it once, with no finding. A folder with no manifest is a filing folder, so a ghost record is no longer made: the `ghost` field leaves the model, and every reader of it (the premises list them) loses its branch. A project's `category` is the first folder under its scan folder when the project lies deeper than one level, and none otherwise. A scan entry carrying `type`, `tier` or `nested`, or any other key, is a finding naming the key; the entry is read for its `dir` and `optional`. `ScanRule` holds `dir` and `optional` only, and `isSideTier`, `SIDE_TIER` and every reader that picks a rule by its folder name go: the docs reader (`server/content/docs.ts`) walks every scan folder as the projects reader does, and the Projects tab's split (`projectTypeOrder`, `rail-badges.svelte.ts`) is the `type_order` words that the board's projects carry, in that order.
2. **Tier and type from the manifest.** A manifest's `tier` is one of `projects`, `coauthor`, `archive`, `personal` and `software` (the `Tier` type is these five words); its `type` is one word of `type_order`. A missing or unknown `tier` is a finding on the manifest's `tier` key, and the project is read with no tier: it is on no board and still reached by its page, Cmd-K, search and the Map. A missing `type`, or one outside `type_order`, is a finding on `type` (G15: a manifest with a broken field still shows its project). The manifest's own `tier` and `type` are the record's, and `typeWritten` goes. The form's `tier` row becomes a select of the five words and `type` a select of `type_order`, both written by the manifest write like any field; `id` stays read-only and refused by the write. The help lines of `id`, `tier` and `type` in `config/schemas/manifest-schema.yml` say what each now is.
3. **The stages.** `active` and `manuscript` are one stage, `active`: `manuscript` leaves `type_order`, `tab_split` and every list of item "Tier and stage words in code" above; the Map's legend draws no manuscript entry; the Projects board's rollup has `in progress` over `[active]` and `idea`; the Projects tab splits into `idea` and `active`. Every reader of a tier word in code reads the new words: the Projects board is the tier `projects`, a stage is a `type`, and `live` and `onBoard` of `focus.ts` are replaced as item 9 says.
4. **Repositories.** A `roots:` entry has `key`, `dir` and `hub?` only. Every root that is not a hub and whose `<dir>/.oculus/project.yml` is there is a project (a root without one, or whose directory is not on this machine, is no project and no finding, so `repository_no_manifest` goes), read by the reader of item 1's records with the same checks as a hub manifest, under the size bound and file checks `readPersonalFile` gives today (renamed for what it reads), with the root's key for its id, `repository: { root, dir }`, and the `tier` and `type` its manifest names, of any of the five tiers. D60's refusals stay, now of any repository project: the key holding a colon, a slash, whitespace or a reserved word; the dir being, lying inside or holding the first hub or a hub root; a second key of one real directory. A `tier` on a roots entry is a finding naming the key, and the entry is read without it. `readRoots`, `RepositoryRoot`, `repositoriesOf` and `hubManifests` (`server/files/roots.ts`), the roots write (`writes/config/roots.ts`), the Settings `roots` fields in `options.yml` and the `findings.roots.tier_*` lines lose `tier`, and `repositoryRule` and the `repository_*` findings that only a root's `tier` could give go.
5. **Ids.** A project's id is its folder's basename, or its root's key for a repository. Two projects with one id (two folders of one name under different scan folders, or a folder and a root key) are one finding naming both files; the first is read and the second is not, the scan folders in their listed order and, inside one, folders in name order, before every root. A manifest whose written `id` differs from its folder's name stays the finding it is today. No `side:` or `root:` id is made or read anywhere; a saved tab naming one draws the unknown project, with no code for the old form (ruling 48 (1)).
