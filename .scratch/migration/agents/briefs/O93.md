## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The three people fields hold person keys, a key or a list, read as a list; a write sends a list, and an empty list drops the field. This is how a collection entry's `lead`, `supervisors` and `collaborators` are read (`entryPeople`), so one rule reads both. `renderFlowList` run on six key lists (`npx tsx` over `src/lib/server/edit/entry-edit/lines.ts`) writes `["axel-faes"]` as `[axel-faes]`, `["maryam-amir-haeri","stephanie-van-den-berg"]` as `[maryam-amir-haeri, stephanie-van-den-berg]`, `["maureen-guichelaar"]` as `[maureen-guichelaar]`, `["o'brien"]` as `[o'brien]`, `["lab:ann"]` as `[lab:ann]` and `["two words"]` as `[two words]`. Run again on keys that need quotes (`npx tsx` over the same function): `["a,b"]` is written `["a,b"]`, `["x[1]"]` as `["x[1]"]`, `["{y}"]` as `["{y}"]`, and `["ann","ann"]` as `[ann, ann]`, so the writer drops a key given twice before it renders the list.
2. A line's people are drawn as their registry names, lead first, then supervisors, then collaborators, each name once, joined by a catalog word of `project.yml` holding ", ", in muted small text after the line's text on the card and after the label on the Focus row; a key no hub resolves is drawn as the key. The name of a key is resolved as `recordChoices` resolves it: the record's hub first, then the one other hub that holds it.
3. A line with its own people is judged by them for Mine only and the Role list only; its record's hub, tier and pillars still decide the other rules, since those are facts of the record and not of the line.
4. The Pillar rows are the pillars of every hub by id, labelled by title, with the hub's name after it while the workspace has more than one hub; a funding scheme's pillars are the ones its projects name (`fundingPillar`), the snapshot's pillars of a scheme.
5. The waiting glyph is `clock` (`(w)` in the ASCII theme), and it replaces the team and related glyphs on the waiting tags, so the waiting kind reads by one glyph; the lane card's line takes it whether it names people or says Blocked, since it is one line.
6. The people picks offer no Add person: the line form takes the people the registry holds.
