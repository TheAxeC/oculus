## What to build

When Q126 is done, each fact about oculus has one home: DESIGN.md says what was chosen and why, the guides under `tools/oculus/docs/` say how each control behaves, README.md says how to run and check it, and a head comment says what its file does. A reader of DESIGN.md finds each decision and its reason in a few sentences, and someone who changes a behaviour edits one place.

1. **Each item of the list keeps its decision and its reason and loses every fact another place already says.** For each item above, read it beside the place or places it is said to repeat, and rewrite it so that it states: what was chosen, what that rules out, and the file or folder that holds it, in a few sentences. Every fact that a guide, HELP.md, README.md, a head comment or another DESIGN.md item already says is removed, in whatever words DESIGN.md said it: a paraphrase is a second home too. A reason lives in DESIGN.md and the behaviour in the guide: where a guide line holds a reason (for example `docs/source-control.md:55`'s "so one press of Stage All Changes cannot stage a file nobody looked at", `docs/files-and-search.md:73`'s "so the version that answers is the one the lockfile pins"), the reason is said once, in DESIGN.md, and the guide keeps the behaviour and drops that clause or keeps it as a short pointer to DESIGN.md's item. A repeat of HELP.md is handled the same way: HELP.md keeps its short form for the user, and DESIGN.md keeps only the decision. An item that holds no decision of its own once its repeats are gone (the review says so of :145, "with no reason of its own", and of :546, which restates :165) is removed, or merged into the item it repeats when that item lacks a reason only it gives. A further repeat of the same kind that the builder finds in an item outside Apps and off the list is reduced the same way in this step and named in the table. The judgment is per item and is the builder's, made by reading; the report's table (Report) names for each item what stayed, what went and where that fact is said.
2. **Control behaviour no guide holds moves to the guide it belongs in.** Where an item states how a control behaves (which press does what, a refusal's words, an edge case) and no guide says it, that sentence moves to the guide that covers that part of oculus (`window.md`, `views.md`, `files-and-search.md`, `source-control.md`, `terminals.md`, `configuration.md`), in that guide's form and section, and leaves DESIGN.md; a guide's headings are not renamed, split or removed, since HELP.md links to them by anchor and DESIGN.md by name (for example `:245`'s wallpaper toggle rule goes to the Settings section of `views.md`, and `:465`'s keyboard equivalents of docking to `window.md`). Nothing a reader needs is lost: each moved sentence is named in the report with its new place.
3. **Every cite names a file that holds what its sentence says.** Each file path, line or section that a listed item, or any item this step changes, cites is checked by reading the cited file: a cite that does not hold its sentence is corrected or dropped. Every name a code comment cites (the list under "What is on the tree") stays in DESIGN.md as a heading or a bold label at the start of its paragraph, and the deck item (`:479`) begins `- Decks:` in the section's form (every item of "Decisions taken" opens `- <Label>:`), so that `utils/migrations/convert-decks.mjs:4`'s cite holds; `src/lib/state/workbench/selection.svelte.ts:7`'s "sessions are six views, not one" keeps its item.
