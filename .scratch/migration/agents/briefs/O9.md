# Brief: O9, the primitives

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Then `tools/oculus/DESIGN.md`, "Layer 3, view" (primitives, boundaries), "Theming", "Configuration", and the decision on order. O8 gave the workbench with two views in it; this package gives the vocabulary every view is composed from: the primitives under `ui/`, the tone lookups a view needs to colour them from the config, a gallery view that shows every primitive with sample data, and the gate that keeps every colour a primitive paints inside the contrast test's measured pairs.

## The colour rule this package makes enforceable

O5's contrast test measures every `--<token>-txt` on `--bg`, `--panel` and `--chip`, every `--c-<hue>-txt` on `--c-<hue>-bg`, and every `--on-c-<hue>` on `--c-<hue>`, under all fifteen themes (1950 pairs, none below 4.5:1). A primitive that only ever paints text with a `-txt` token on one of the three surfaces or on its hue's tint, or with an `--on-` token on the matching fill, cannot produce a pair the test did not measure. That is the rule: in `src/lib/ui`, `src/lib/shell` and `src/lib/views`, a `color` declaration is `var(--<x>-txt, ...)`, `var(--on-<x>, ...)`, `inherit` or `currentColor`; a `background` or `background-color` is `var(--bg)`, `var(--panel)`, `var(--chip)`, `var(--line)`, `var(--accent)`, `var(--c-<hue>)`, `var(--c-<hue>-bg)`, `none`, `transparent`, or a `var()` of a custom property the component itself sets from one of those; and no declaration anywhere in those folders carries a raw colour (`#`, `rgb(`, `hsl(`, a named colour). `tests/ui/colours.test.ts` reads every `<style>` block under the three folders and fails on anything else, with the file, the selector and the declaration in the message. O7's `+layout.svelte` and O8's components are brought under the rule in this package if they are not already.

## What to build

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/state/`:

- `tones.ts` (no runes): the lookups from the config payload to a tone, where a tone is the hue part of a palette token (`teal` for `--c-teal`). `setVocab(config)` installs the `palette` (hex to token name), `status_palette`, `type_color`, `status_legend` and `themes` maps from the merged config, fed the same way as the catalog and the glyphs (page data at first load, the `config` channel afterwards; `+page.server.ts` and `state/socket.svelte.ts` gain one call each). `toneOf(hex)` (`palette` lookup, `#7c5cff` to `violet`, unknown hex to `grey`), `statusTone(status)` (a collection entry status to its tone through `status_palette`), `typeTone(type)` (a project type through `type_color`), `chipGlyph(chip)` and `chipLabel(chip)` (through `status_legend`, the icon character and the label; an unknown chip gives the chip word itself and no glyph), `themeTone(theme)` (the research theme colour of `themes`). Every function is pure over the installed maps; `tests/state/tones.test.ts` covers each with a synthetic config and the unknown cases.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/ui/`, each a Svelte 5 component with typed `$props()`, snippets for content, tokens only, and the colour rule above:

- `Card.svelte`: a panel-coloured box with `--radius`, `--line` border and `--shadow`; props `title`, `tone?` (a 3 px left bar in `--c-<tone>`), `pinned?` (an inset `--accent` ring); snippets `meta` (the row under the title, where chips go), `children` (the body), `footer?`. The title is a heading whose level is a prop (`level`, default 3).
- `Chip.svelte`: an inline label; props `label`, `tone?`, `glyph?` (a glyph name, through `Glyph`), `filled?`. Outline (default): text `--c-<tone>-txt` on `--chip`, `--line` border, `--radius-pill`. Filled: `--c-<tone>` background, `--on-c-<tone>` text. No tone: `--muted-txt` on `--chip`. Under `body.rice` (the terminal shape the O5 report lists): no background, no border, `[` and `]` around the label from `::before` and `::after`, and the colour unchanged.
- `Meter.svelte`: a horizontal bar; props `value` (0 to 1, clamped), `tone?`, `label` (the accessible name), optional `text` shown at the right. `role="meter"`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-label`. The track is `--chip`, the fill `--c-<tone>` or `--accent`.
- `Table.svelte`: props `columns: { key, label, align?: 'left' | 'right', width?: string, numeric?: boolean }[]`, `rows: Record<string, unknown>[]`, `rowKey` (the key whose value identifies a row), optional `cell` snippet `(row, column)` for a custom cell, `sortable?`. Clicking a sortable header sorts by that column (strings by locale, numbers by value, a second click reverses) and the header carries `aria-sort`; the sort is the component's own state. Sticky header, `--line` row separators, numeric columns in `--font-num` right-aligned, the row hover in `--chip`.
- `Pane.svelte`: the container a view fills: a header row with the title (through a `title` prop) and a `toolbar` snippet at the right, then a body that scrolls (`overflow: auto`, `min-height: 0`), `--panel` background. This is what a view renders at its root so the workbench's pane box is filled and scrolling happens inside the view.
- `Empty.svelte`: one line for an empty state: a glyph (`glyph?` name) and a `message` the view has already translated. `--muted-txt`. Under `body.rice`, a `# ` prefix from `::before`.
- `Toolbar.svelte` and `ToolButton.svelte`: a row of controls (`role="toolbar"`, `gap`), and a button with `label`, `glyph?`, `active?`, `onclick`, `title?`; active is `--accent` background with `--on-accent` text, inactive is `--panel` with `--muted-txt` and a `--line` border. `Segment.svelte`: an exclusive group of `ToolButton`s from `items: { id, label, glyph? }[]`, `value`, `onchange(id)`, `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-checked` on each.
- `Search.svelte`: a text input with `value`, `placeholder`, `oninput(value)`, Escape clearing it, `type="search"`, `aria-label` from `placeholder`.
- The primitives O7 and O8 already made (`Sash`, `Tabs`, `Glyph`, `Menu`) stay and are brought under the colour rule.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/views/`:

- `Gallery.svelte` (`id: 'gallery'`, registered in `views/index.ts`): every primitive above with sample data: three cards (one with a tone bar, one pinned), a row of chips in the outline and filled forms across at least six tones with glyphs from `status_legend` through `chipGlyph`, three meters, a sortable table of eight rows with a numeric column, a toolbar with a segment, a search box, and an empty state. Every string through `t()` under a `text.gallery` group. This view is the living style sheet: the orchestrator opens it under several themes after landing, and it stays in the tool.

Under `tools/oculus/tests/`:

- `ui/colours.test.ts`: the colour gate above, with a self-check on synthetic `.svelte` fixtures in a temporary directory showing a raw hex, a `color: var(--c-teal)` (a hue that is not `-txt`) and a `background: var(--nonesuch)` each refused, the pattern `keys.test.ts` and `boundaries.test.ts` use.
- `ui/primitives.test.ts`: `render` from `svelte/server` of every primitive with props: the roles and ARIA attributes named above are present, a chip's tone lands in its class or style, a meter clamps its value, a table renders one row per input row with the numeric class on the numeric column, an empty state shows its message.
- `state/tones.test.ts`: as above.
- `tests/contrast.test.ts` is not edited: the gate is that primitives cannot leave its measured set.

## Conventions

Four-space indentation (`npm run format`); every file under 1000 lines; ASCII in every authored file (a glyph reaches the DOM only through `Glyph` or a catalog string; `[`, `]` and `#` in a `::before` are ASCII and allowed); no new dependency; `tests/boundaries.test.ts`, `tests/keys.test.ts` and the new `tests/ui/colours.test.ts` must pass; no literal user-facing string in a component.
