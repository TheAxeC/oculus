## What to build

1. **isRecord.** Every copy goes, and every use calls `isMap` imported from `data/helpers/guards.ts`. `rows.ts` drops its `as isRecord` alias, and `layout/index.ts` stops re-exporting the name. Afterwards `grep -rn isRecord src tests` prints nothing.
2. **Server paths.** A new `src/lib/server/core/paths.ts` exports three functions:
   - `inside(path, dir)`: true when the resolved path is the resolved folder or below it.
   - `below(path, dir)`: true when it is strictly below.
   - `realPath(path)`: `realpathSync`, or null when it throws.

   Every copy and inline check listed above calls one of the three:
   - Folder-or-below sites call `inside`; that includes `customizations.ts` `within` and the bodies of `pathInside`, `permitted` and `underMirrors`.
   - Strictly-below sites call `below`; that includes `compose.ts`.
   - The five `real` copies call `realPath`.

   `group.ts` keeps its empty-string guard before calling `inside`. `insideRepo` goes, and `git-write.ts` and its callers use `core/git.ts`'s `pathInside`. `git-routes.ts` calls `permitted` directly. The allow-list lines of `allow-duplication.txt` that paired these copies are removed; the duplication check then passes without them.
3. **Porcelain.** `core/git-parse.ts` exports `statusRecords(output)`: the records of `status --porcelain=v1 -z`, each `{ code, path, from }`, where `code` is the two letters, `path` the path the record names, and `from` the old path of a rename or copy (the next field), else null.
   - `core/git.ts` `porcelain` reads through it.
   - So do `git-write.ts` `tracked` and `untrackedIn`, `git-refs.ts` `holds` and `git-index.ts` `pairOf`.
   - `deposit-git.ts` `porcelain` runs `status --porcelain=v1 -z`, and `deposit.ts` `changesIn` reads through `statusRecords`. A deposit's changed paths are then unquoted, and a rename is listed by its new path.
4. **Client paths.** A new `src/lib/data/helpers/paths.ts`, safe on both sides, exports four functions:
   - `nameOf(path)`: the last non-empty segment, moved from `file-type.ts`, which keeps exporting it through a re-export so its importers are unchanged.
   - `trimSlash(dir)`: the folder without its trailing slashes.
   - `pathUnder(folder, target)`: `''` for the folder itself, the relative path below it, or null outside it.
   - `deepestHolding(items, dirOf, target)`: the item whose folder holds the target most deeply, with the path under it, or null.

   The copies go:
   - `format.ts` `basename` and the local one in `TerminalRows.svelte`: their callers (`format.ts` `elide`, `Usage.svelte`, `Timeline.svelte`, `TerminalRows.svelte`) call `nameOf`.
   - `rootHolding`, `repoOfFile` (its `bare`, `under`, `wholePath` included) and `repoStatus` build on `pathUnder` and `deepestHolding`. `repoOfFile` keeps excluding a file that is the repository's own folder.
   - `selectors.svelte.ts` `inside` becomes `pathUnder(folder, path) !== null` behind its null and empty guard.
5. **ago.** `data/helpers/dates.ts` exports `ago(iso, now, largest)`:
   - It returns `{ unit, count }` with unit `'now' | 'minute' | 'hour' | 'day' | 'week' | 'month' | 'year'`, the largest unit that is at least one and not above `largest`.
   - `'now'` covers under a minute, including up to a minute in the future.
   - It returns null for a stamp that cannot be read or lies more than a minute in the future.
   - Weeks are 7 days, months 30 and years 365, as `file-git.ts` counts them.

   `folds.ts` `ago` goes. `SessionWorkspace.svelte` calls the new one with `'day'` and maps `minute`, `hour` and `day` to its existing keys `sessions.ago_m`, `ago_h` and `ago_d`. `file-git.ts` `ago` keeps its words and reads the count from the new one with `'year'`, answering `''` for null.
6. **Local date.** `data/helpers/dates.ts` `localDate` takes `number | Date`. `calendar.ts` `localDay` goes; its callers there and in `Calendar.svelte` call `localDate` from `data/helpers/dates.ts`. `transcripts.ts` `localDay` goes. `notes.ts` `stamp` takes its day from `localDate`.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. **Where the helpers live.**
   - The server helpers go in `server/core/paths.ts`, beside the other server-wide helpers. They import `node:path` and `node:fs`, so the client cannot import them.
   - The client-safe helpers go in `data/helpers/paths.ts` and `data/helpers/dates.ts`, which `state/` may import (`guards.ts`'s head comment names that row of the boundary table).
2. **Names.**
   - `inside`, `below` and `realPath` for the server.
   - `nameOf`, `trimSlash`, `pathUnder` and `deepestHolding` for the client.
   - `statusRecords` for the parser.
   - `ago` and `localDate` for the dates.

   These are internal names, not a user-visible shape.
3. **Tried on real cases from the tree.** "Strictly below" and "folder or below" stay two functions, since the tree uses both. On main:
   - `entries.ts:71` refuses the hub folder itself (`below`).
   - `feeds.ts:54` admits it (`inside`).
   - `compose.ts:115` refuses the folder itself (`below`).
   - `images.ts:284` admits it (`inside`).
   - `customizations.ts:66` admits it (`inside`).
4. **The trailing slash.** `nameOf`'s rule wins over `basename`'s: `'a/b/'` names `b` where `basename` gave `''`. A terminal or session folder never ends in a slash on the payloads read (`cwd` from tmux and the registry). The report states the change.
5. **A future moment.** One rule for both `ago` callers: more than a minute ahead is null. The Repositories blame then shows no age for a commit dated more than a minute in the future, where it showed "now". The report states it.
6. **The deposit's porcelain moves to `-z`.** The plan's rows lose git's quoting, and a rename is listed by its new path. The report states it, with the before and after.
