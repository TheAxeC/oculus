## What to build

1. **One shared part.** Every record page in scope (a project's; a person's; the entry page of a collection entry of every registry: pillar, domain, venue, dataset, funding scheme, compute resource, organisation, and of every other collection) is drawn through `RecordPage.svelte`, extended to draw, in this order: the outcome line, the record's card, the row of count buttons (item 2), then the page's blocks (item 6). The views hand the frame the card, the row's groups and the blocks; no view draws its own row or its own bars. A lead's and a Lit Watch paper's page go through the same frame with no row: a lead names no project, and a Lit Watch paper keeps its card as today, its strip of the projects it names included, since it is not a record of ruling 710. Every prop the frame gains is optional, so `Listing.svelte`, `Career.svelte` and `Post.svelte` draw as they do today.
2. **The row of count buttons.** Under the card, one button per row of `categories:` that holds one of the record's projects, in the list's order, "other" last for the projects in no row, each `<n> <row label>` in the row's colour as a board section heading is, then the total, worded by `projectCount` ("1 project", "<n> projects"). A row with `archival: true` (`dead-ends`) gets its button too when the record has a project in it, so a project an entry page names today through "Referred to by" stays reachable from the page. A press on a category's button opens the record's board (item 3) with every slice of that category picked (`filters.slice` holding each `sliceValue(category, word)` of the record's projects in that category); a press on the total opens it with nothing picked. The press sets the tab's whole board state: that pick, no search, no other facet, and Mine only off as Focus sets it for the Awaiting board, so the board shows exactly the projects the button counts. A record with no project draws no row. The projects counted are the record's projects as its card's foot strip counts them today: a project's related projects; a person's team projects (`personJoin().projects`); a pillar's, domain's, venue's and dataset's members as their card reads them (`lineMembers`, `domainMembers`, `venueUsers`, `datasetUsers`); a funding scheme's, an organisation's and a resource's projects as `cross-links.ts` joins them. On a page that draws the row the card no longer draws that foot strip, so the projects are drawn once; on a board, a view's grid and a Lit Watch paper's page the card is unchanged.
3. **The record's board.** A view of `Projects.svelte` of its own, registered beside `AWAITING_VIEW` with `listed: false`, built as the Awaiting board is: its tab state names the record (its kind and its key, with the collection and hub for an entry) and the title, its projects are computed from the live portfolio each time it is drawn, so a project that stops naming the record leaves it, and its preset is `awaitingPreset`'s shape over those ids (a shared function both call, not a copy). It has the full board bar, Cards and List, and offers no New project. One tab per record and board kind: the tab's id carries the kind of board (item 5), the record's kind and its full hub-qualified id, so a person's two boards, two records of one key in two hubs and two records of different kinds with one key are separate tabs, and a second press on the same record's buttons reuses that record's tab with the new pick. The tab's title is "Projects of <record name>". A tab opened with no record (from the palette), for a record that is gone, or for an entry whose key changed since the tab was saved, says the record is gone and holds no project. The preset skips its own view id as `awaitingPreset` does (`presets.ts:577`), so a hub `boards:` row keyed with that id does not take its place.
4. **"Referred to by" removed.** `Referrers.svelte` is deleted and no page draws a referrers block. What names the record and is not a project (a person or a funding scheme naming an organisation; the `slurm.resource` setting naming a resource: the `person`, `funding` and `setting` kinds of `referrersOf`) is drawn on the card as one line of tags, under a small label in the card's line style, each tag opening its page: a person's page; a funding scheme's entry page; a funding scheme with no key, which has no page, as plain words as today; and the `slurm.resource` setting opens Settings at its row. For that, Settings' rail ask is widened to name any row: `revealShape` and `settingsReveal` (`typed.ts`) take a row id, `{ reveal: 'layout.yml:rail' }` or the setting's row, the rail's choice (`ActivityRail.svelte:164`) passes `RAIL_ROW` through the same shape, and `Settings.svelte` shows the asked row as it shows the rail's today (clear a query that hides it, unfold it, scroll it into view, end the ask). `referrersOf` stays for `DeleteAsk.svelte`; `listsReferrers` and any other export the change leaves unused are removed; the words `entry_form.referrers`, `no_referrers` and `referrer_open` that no code reads any more are removed, `referrer_setting` kept if the tag line uses it.
5. **Waiting on.** The person page draws no Waiting on block. Its row ends with one more button, "<n> waiting on <name>", when `personJoin().waitingOn` is not empty, opening the record's board of those projects (a second board kind of item 3's view, its title "Waiting on <name>").
6. **The blocks.** No record page draws a `GroupHead` bar. Each block is drawn under a small label as `CardDetails.svelte` draws its labels (`.label`): on the project page LaTeX, the documents and the sessions; on an entry page whose `link:` names a folder its documents, files and sessions (`EntryFolder.svelte`); on a student's page the supervision folder (`FolderFiles.svelte`). The files, the sessions and the supervision folder are plain lists; the documents block keeps its document viewer (`Segment` with `Markdown`, `Project.svelte:171-181`, `EntryFolder.svelte:104-114`) under its small label, since a document is read there, and LaTeX keeps what `Latex.svelte` draws inside its bar today. The blocks no longer fold, and the folds kept under `folded` of these tabs' state are no longer written or read by these pages.
7. **The project page's layout for every card.** On its page, every record card draws its body in the project page's layout: the closed card, then its fields in `CardDetails.svelte`'s two columns, each field under its small label, the right column under the left in a narrow pane. `EntryCard` (`page`), `VenueCard`, `DatasetCard`, `DomainCard` and `LineCard` (`page`) take that layout through one shared part (the two-column body and its label), not five copies. Nothing a card shows today is lost: every field, tag and action stays, moved into the layout.
8. **Comments and words.** Every head comment of a file the step changes is rewritten to what the file does now, `GroupHead.svelte:64-82`'s list of users included, and `config/text/views/shared/entry-form.yml:1`'s head line. Words no code reads are removed from `config/text`.
