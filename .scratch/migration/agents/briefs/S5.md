"S5 the Settings view's controls and headings: every option and every field of a family takes the control its value needs (a list to pick from for a word of a short list or a key of another list, a checkbox for true or false, a number box, a glyph picker drawing the glyphs, a colour picker drawing the palette hues, a multi-pick, a sub-editor for a nested list of records in place of one line of YAML text), free text kept only for free words (labels, titles, paragraphs, placeholders, column headings); every setting shows its current value; the headings by subject in place of file names: This browser, Appearance, Editor, Terminal, Sessions and usage, Cluster, Boards and audit, Rail and pages, Presentation, Project sources, Calendar and mail (calendar.* and mail.* of behavior.yml), This machine (the keys of local.yml, values never shown); after S4 (ruling Z333) (ruling Z426)"

## What to build

### 1. The headings

1. The Settings view's groups are, in this order: This browser, Appearance, Editor, Terminal, Sessions and usage, Cluster, Boards and audit, Rail and pages, Presentation, Project sources, Calendar and mail, This machine, then the Catalogs and Texts groups as today. The heading words are new lines of `config/text/views/system/workbench.yml`, each read by a literal `t('workbench.settings_heading_<id>')` call, so `keys.test.ts`'s computed prefixes are not touched.
2. Each catalog entry outside `local.yml` names its heading with a new key `heading:`, one of the ids `appearance`, `editor`, `terminal`, `sessions`, `cluster`, `boards`, `rail`, `presentation`, `sources`, `calendar`, exported as one list `HEADINGS` from `options.ts` and read by the state. Every `local.yml` key is under This machine and carries no `heading:`. The group ids are the heading ids, `this-browser`, `machine`, `catalogs` and `texts`.
3. Inside a heading, a row's place follows the file order of `FILES`, then the catalog's order; rows gather under their `section` in the order each section's first row appears. Every row's section is the table's (item 6.4); a `local.yml` entry gains a `section:` (item 6.5). `Section.heading` in `groups.ts` is renamed `Section.title`, so "heading" names the subject heading alone.
4. Each heading shows, at its top, one line per file it draws from, in `FILES` order: the file's name, its description (`describedAs`), the `local` Tag for `local.yml`, and the control that opens the file in a source tab (`views.open_source`, as `List.svelte` draws it today). A file that is loading, unreadable, cut or unparsed says so on its line and its rows are absent from every heading; the other files' rows are drawn. `Group` holds a list of these file lines in place of its one `file`, `description`, `local`, `status` and `said`. Appearance, Sessions and usage, Cluster, Rail and pages, Presentation, Project sources, Calendar and mail and This machine draw from one file each; Editor from `behavior.yml` and `sources.yml`, Terminal from `appearance.yml` and `behavior.yml`, Boards and audit from `behavior.yml` and `layout.yml`.
5. A search word matches a row when it occurs in the row's title, key path, section, description, or its heading's words. Under an empty query a heading whose files all say why they hold no row is kept with its lines; under a typed query a heading holding no row is dropped, as the table of contents drops it.
6. The table of contents lists the headings and their sections. A closed-groups list a tab saved under the file names is read as no closed group, so every heading opens.
7. An entry with no `heading:` or an unknown heading id is a finding (item 2.4) and its row is drawn under a heading "Without a heading" (id `unheaded`, words in workbench.yml), placed after Calendar and mail and before This machine, which exists only while such a row does.

### 2. The catalog vocabulary and its reader

1. A leaf option's `control` is one of: `toggle`, `number`, `text`, `long` (a TextArea), `choice`, `multi` (a MultiSelect), `list` (one text box per item, Add, Remove, Move), `glyph` (GlyphSelect over `catalogs/glyphs.yml`), `hue` (HueSelect, writing the hue's name), `colour` (HueSelect in its colour form, writing a palette hex), `folder` and `file` (a hub path box with suggestions), `path` (an absolute or `~/` path box), `parked` (a list each item of which has Remove and no Add). A family field may carry any of these and `records`.
2. The keys an entry or a field may carry beside `control`:
   - `values` (a fixed list) or `values_from` (one source, or a list of sources whose items are offered together) on a `choice` or `multi`; `values_by: { field, sources: { <word>: <source> } }` on a `choice` field whose source is named by another field of its record (`deck.order.ref` by `slide`); a word the map does not name gives no items;
   - `suggest_from` (one source) on a `text` field or option: the box offers the source's items, and any text is accepted;
   - `empty: true` on a `choice` or `path`: an empty value is offered first, with words saying what empty does, and writes `''`;
   - `min` and `max` on a `number`, inclusive;
   - `slots` (the names of a fixed number of items) on a `list`: one text box per slot, labelled by the slot, no Add, Remove or Move, and a list of another length refused;
   - `items: number` on a `list`: each item a number box;
   - `joined: true` on a `multi`: the picked words written as one text separated by one space, in the order held and then picked;
   - `allow_missing: true`, or the name of a toggle field of the same record, on a `folder` or `file`: a path that is not there is accepted (always, or while that field is true).
3. A family's `fields:` entry is either a string (its description; the field is `text`) or a map `{ description, control, ... }` with the keys above, where `control: records` takes `fields` describing the records of a nested list, drawn as a sub-editor of cards inside the card (Move up, Move down, Remove, Add), never as one line of YAML.
4. `options.ts` reads every key above into a typed entry (a field string read as `{ description, control: 'text' }`) and a new reader `optionFindings(engine, config)` answers a finding naming `config/catalogs/options.yml` and the file and key path (with the field, for a field) of each of: an unknown control word, an unknown `values_from`, `values_by` or `suggest_from` source, a `choice` or `multi` with neither `values` nor `values_from` nor `values_by`, a field map without `description`, an entry outside `local.yml` with no `heading:` or an unknown heading id, a `min` above its `max`, a `slots` that is not a list of words. The entry is then read as `text` (an unknown or incomplete control) or under `unheaded` (a heading). The sentences are a new `findings.options` group of `config/text/findings.yml`, read by literals.
5. `optionFindings` is gathered where the other catalog readers' findings are: `server/root/context.ts:303-310`, `server/root/watch/portfolio.ts:513-519`, `config-checks.ts` `FINDERS`, and re-exported through `data/sources/config.ts`.
6. The head comment of `options.yml` (lines 6-10) and of `options.ts` (lines 1-15, 26-34) say the new shape.
