# Brief: F1, the feeds on the socket

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Axel ruled on 2026-09-12 at 21:45 that the feeds' pull path is a defect against DESIGN.md's live-data goal (live data pushed over one WebSocket with channels rather than polled) and is fixed before Q9, which writes to the feeds.

## How the feeds reach the page today (checked 2026-09-12 at 22:05 on main at 1720baab)

- The portfolio build reads the three feed files (`data/sources/feeds.ts` `readFeeds`, line 166) into the snapshot's `feeds` summary (run dates and counts the Focus board and the badges read; `data/model-collections.ts` lines 70 to 88), not the entries.
- The four feed views (Radar, Leads, Inbox, LinkedIn), the palette (`state/palette-source.svelte.ts`) and the badges (`state/rail-badges.svelte.ts`) read `state/feeds.svelte.ts`: `feed(kind)` fetches `GET /api/feeds?kind=` (`server/feeds.ts` `handleFeeds`, line 371; the route `src/routes/api/feeds/+server.ts`, 6 lines) the first time a kind is asked for, and again whenever the `portfolio` channel's version changes (the store subscribes to the portfolio version, lines 22 to 24, and refetches every kind already fetched); `peekFeed(kind)` reads what is held without fetching (the palette and the badges), `refetch(kind)` is called after a write (Linkedin.svelte, PostCard.svelte).
- `server/watch/portfolio.ts` `watchedDirectories` (line 67) watches the folders of the inbox, leads and radar files, so an edit to one of those republishes `portfolio` and the open views refetch; the LinkedIn folder (`config/sources.yml` `linkedin_dir`, `theaxec.github.io/_linkedin`) is not watched, so an edit there reaches nothing until the view is reopened.
- So: the entries travel by request after a version push; a view opened later fetches; the palette and the badges know a feed only after some view fetched it (Q4a's recorded edge); the LinkedIn feed is not live at all.

## What to build

1. **A `feeds` channel.** The server publishes one message on a new channel `feeds` whose payload is `{ radar, leads, inbox, linkedin }`, each the `FeedData` the route answers today (`meta`, `entries`, `cadenceDays`, `schema`, `missing`, or `error` when the file is unreadable), read by `server/feeds.ts`'s readers; published at start and on every change: the watcher gains the LinkedIn folder in `watchedDirectories`, and a change under a feed's folder republishes `feeds` (the whole payload; the four feeds together are small) beside `portfolio`. The channel joins the socket's subscription the way `hpc` and `sessions` did (read `server/socket.ts` and `server/channels.ts`, and where the page subscribes on boot, `state/socket.svelte.ts` or its equivalent; say where). `data/model.ts` gains the payload type (`FeedsPayload`).
2. **The client reads the channel.** `state/entities.svelte.ts` gains `feeds()` beside `hpc()` and `sessions()`; `state/feeds.svelte.ts` becomes a thin reader over it: `feed(kind)` returns the channel's `FeedState` for the kind (null until the first message), `peekFeed` and `refetch` are deleted with every caller updated, the version subscription and the fetch are deleted; the four views, the palette and the badges read the channel, so the palette indexes every feed from the first message and the badges never wait for a view. `GET /api/feeds` is deleted (`handleFeeds` and the route); the write actions of the feeds (`feed-field`, `feed-delete`, `lead-add`, `linkedin-idea`, `linkedin-post` in `server/writes.ts`) stay and reach the page through the watcher, with no refetch after a write anywhere.
3. **The snapshot's feed summary stays** as the Focus board's source (the run dates and counts), computed by the build as today; the report says whether anything reads both and could read one.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. One channel for the four feeds, not four channels: they change rarely and together they are a few hundred entries; a channel per feed would be four subscriptions for nothing.
2. The LinkedIn folder is watched non-recursively (its files sit flat), the same debounce as the others.
3. Nothing user-visible changes; the wire format gains one channel, named here.
