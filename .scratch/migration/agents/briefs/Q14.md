## How each missing binding lives in oculus (the three layers)

Everything here is view and shell code over state that exists; no data or wire change. One config shape: the chord letter of a rail flyout group.

1. **Digits.** `1` to `9` open the Nth direct view button of the rail in display order (`shell/ActivityRail.svelte`'s `sections`: the small groups' buttons, the flyout openers skipped, as the cockpit numbered its inline tabs and not its menus), in the main area (`openView(id, 'main')`, which activates an open instance rather than opening a second: check `state/layout.svelte.ts` `openView` and say so).
2. **Chords.** A flyout group (`config/layout.yml` `nav_groups`, a group of more than four views) gains an optional key `chord:` (one lowercase letter); the brief sets `chord: c` on Academic life (the cockpit's Collections menu held the same collections) and `chord: m` on Sessions (the cockpit's other menu). `c` or `m` then a digit within 1.5 s opens the Nth entry of that group's list; the chord lapses as the cockpit's does; the shell reads the letter from the config, not from a literal.
3. **Tab cycling.** `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` when not typing open the next or previous view in the rail's display order, flyout entries included, from the focused pane's active view (the cockpit walked every tab). This takes Tab from focus navigation while nothing is being typed, as the cockpit did; the report names that cost.
4. **The card cursor.** The shell keeps a cursor (an index into the visible cards of the focused pane: `focused()` of `state/layout.svelte.ts` line 426 names the pane; the pane's element is `PaneView.svelte`'s; the cards are the elements `ui/Card.svelte` renders, marked `data-card`, filtered to those laid out, as the cockpit's `cards()` line 292 filtered by `offsetParent`). `j`/`k`/arrows move it, `i`/`o`/arrows move it geometrically as the cockpit's `kbRow` (line 305), the card under the cursor carries `data-cursor` and `Card.svelte` draws the cockpit's `.kbsel` ring from it (an accent outline, tokens only), scrolled into view centred. `Enter` activates the card's head: the Card primitive's title control when the card has `ontitle`, else the control marked `data-card-toggle` (the project card's open control: find it in `views/shared/ProjectCard.svelte` and mark it); `Escape` clears the cursor first, before the existing Escape meanings. Selection is DOM state on the focused pane (the cockpit's approach); a view change or a pane focus change clears it.
5. **Search.** `/` focuses the focused pane's `ui/Search.svelte` input (mark the input `data-search`), selecting its text.
6. **Scroll.** `g g` and `G` scroll the focused pane's scroll container (the element of `PaneView.svelte` that scrolls; name it) to the top and the bottom, smooth.
7. **The sheet.** `?` toggles a keyboard sheet over the workbench: a `ui/` primitive (a new `KeySheet.svelte` or the `Confirm`'s ground pattern), rows from `js.keys.rows` split on `;;` and `|` as the cockpit's `buildSheet` (line 621), heading `js.keys.title`, hint `js.keys.hint`; closed by `?`, `Escape` or a click. The rows string in `config/text.yml` line 62 is extended, in place, with oculus's own keys after the cockpit's: `Ctrl+1-9 | switch page;;Ctrl+Shift+Left / Right | move the page`.
8. **The guards.** Every new binding bails when typing (the cockpit's `typing()`, which covers xterm's textarea), when a modifier is held, when the palette or the tray is open, and when a `Confirm` is up.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Digits address the rail's direct views, not the pane's tabs: the cockpit's numbered tabs were its views.
2. The chord letters are config (`chord:` on a flyout group), set to the cockpit's `c` and `m`.
3. Tab cycling is ported as the cockpit had it, with the accessibility cost named.
4. The cursor is DOM state on the focused pane, as the cockpit's, not layout state.
