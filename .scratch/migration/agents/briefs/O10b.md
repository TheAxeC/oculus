# Brief: O10b, docking

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Then `tools/oculus/DESIGN.md`, "Layout as data", "Layer 3, view" (shell, boundaries), and the two decisions on layout and docking of 2026-09-10. O7 built every operation on the tree and O8 the workbench over two trees; this package is the interaction: a tab dragged to another strip moves, dropped on a pane's edge splits, dragged across the main and panel trees docks and undocks; a pane closes whole and maximises; every one of those has a keyboard path. Nothing here adds a model: the saved workbench is the same envelope, extended only where a new fact needs a home.

## What to build

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/state/`:

- `layout.ts`, extended with pure operations, each tested: `closePane(layout, path)` (every tab of the pane goes, the pane collapses as `closeInstance` collapses an emptied pane, the root pane stays and empties); `transfer(workbench, id, target, path, index)` (an instance moved from whichever tree holds it to the pane at `path` in `target`, the source cleaned as `moveInstance` cleans; within one tree it is `moveInstance`); `splitWith(workbench, id, target, path, edge)` (the instance removed from its tree and the pane at `path` in `target` split on that `edge`: `left` and `right` make a row split with the new pane before or after, `top` and `bottom` a column split; the `splitPane` of O7 does the split, with its same-direction insertion rule); `maximise(workbench, target, path)` and `restore(workbench)` on a new optional field `focus?: { target: Target; path: number[] }` of `Workbench`, which `checkWorkbench` requires to name an existing pane and `parseLayout` drops when it does not (the envelope stays version 2; a value without the field is a workbench with none). A workbench operation that names no pane returns its argument unchanged.
- `layout.svelte.ts`, extended: `closePaneIn(target, path)`, `transferInstance(id, target, path, index)`, `splitInstance(id, target, path, edge)`, `maximisePane(target, path)`, `restorePanes()`, `focused()`; every one through `apply`, so it is saved. Opening a view while a pane is maximised opens into the maximised pane.
- `drag.svelte.ts`: the one drag in progress, shared by every strip and pane: `start(id, target, label, point)`, `move(point)`, `hover(drop: Drop | null)`, `end()` (performs the hovered drop through the store), `cancel()`; `dragging()` returns null or `{ id, label, point, drop }`. A `Drop` is `{ kind: 'strip'; target; path; index }` or `{ kind: 'edge'; target; path; edge }` or `{ kind: 'centre'; target; path }` (the centre of a pane body means the end of its strip). The drag starts only after the pointer has moved 6 px from where it went down, so a click on a tab is still a click.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/ui/`:

- `Tabs.svelte`, extended: each tab face reports `onpointerdown(id, point)` and the strip reports `onhoverindex(index | null)` from the pointer's x within it during a drag (the insertion index is before the tab whose midpoint the pointer has not passed); a `dropIndex` prop draws a 2 px `--accent` insertion mark at that index; a `menu` snippet prop, if given, renders a small opener at the strip's right for the pane menu. `Tabs` stays domain-blind: ids, labels and callbacks.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/shell/`:

- `PaneView.svelte`, extended: wires the strip's callbacks to `drag.svelte.ts`; the body computes edge drops from its own rect while a drag is over it (an edge zone is the outer 25 % of the body's width or height, the rest is centre) and reports them through `hover`; the pane menu (a `Menu` from O8) with `ui.pane_close` (close pane), `ui.pane_maximise` / `ui.pane_restore`, `ui.move_to_panel` / `ui.move_to_main` for the active tab, and `ui.split_right`, `ui.split_down` for the active tab (splitting its own pane, the new pane after it). Those menu items are the keyboard path: the opener is focusable and the menu already handles arrows and Escape.
- `DropLayer.svelte`: rendered once by `Workbench`, `position: fixed`, `pointer-events: none`, above everything; while a drag is in progress it draws the ghost (the tab's label beside the pointer, `--panel` on `--line`) and the indicator for the hovered drop: for an edge, a translucent `--accent` rectangle over the half of the pane body the new pane would take (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 25%, transparent)` is allowed here as the one translucent fill, since it is not text and the colour gate checks `color` and `background` of text-bearing rules); for a centre, the rectangle over the whole body; for a strip index, nothing beyond the strip's own mark. The document's `pointermove`, `pointerup` and `keydown` (Escape) listeners exist only while a drag is in progress, installed by the drag store's `start` and removed by `end` and `cancel`; `user-select` is none on the body for the duration.
- `Workbench.svelte`, extended: when `focused()` names a pane, that tree renders only the pane's subtree and the other tree and the sash are hidden; a `ui.pane_restore` item in the pane menu and Escape (when no drag is in progress) restore.

Under `tools/oculus/config/text.yml`, under `text.ui`: `pane_menu`, `pane_close`, `pane_maximise`, `pane_restore`, `move_to_panel`, `move_to_main`, `split_right`, `split_down`, `drag_hint` (the ghost's accessible text).

Under `tools/oculus/tests/`:

- `state/layout.test.ts`, extended: `closePane` on a leaf, on the last pane of a split (collapse), on the root; `transfer` within a tree, across trees, to an index, with the source collapsing; `splitWith` on each of the four edges and the same-direction insertion rule; `maximise` and `restore`, a `focus` naming a pane that a later close removed being dropped by `parseLayout`, the envelope round trip with and without `focus`.
- `state/drag.test.ts`: the drag store with a fake store (the operations it calls recorded): the 6 px threshold, `hover` then `end` performing exactly one operation of the right kind, `cancel` performing none, `end` with no drop performing none.
- `shell/workbench.test.ts`, extended: `render` of a workbench with `focus` set draws only the focused pane; `Tabs` with `dropIndex` draws the mark.
- The drag itself has no DOM in the suite; the orchestrator drives it in Chrome after landing (a tab dragged onto the other pane's strip, onto each edge, from main to panel and back, Escape mid-drag, close pane, maximise, restore, and a reload after each).

## Facts checked on the tree, 2026-09-10

- `layout.ts` exports `openInstance`, `closeInstance`, `activate`, `moveInstance`, `splitPane(layout, path, dir, instance)`, `resize`, `equalSizes`, `paneAt`, `panes`, `findInstance`, `checkLayout`, `checkWorkbench`, `serialiseLayout`, `parseLayout`; `Workbench` is `{ main, panel, dock, panelSize }` at `LAYOUT_VERSION` 2; `MAX_CHILDREN` is 10 and `splitPane` nests past it.
- `layout.svelte.ts` exports `layout(target)`, `workbench()`, `apply`, `change(target, edit)`, `dock`, `resizePanel`, `activateInstance`, `closeInstanceIn`, `openView`; every change goes through `apply`, which saves.
- `Tabs.svelte` takes `tabs`, `active`, `onselect`, `onclose`; a tab is a face button and a close button in a presentation wrapper. `Sash.svelte` is the model for pointer capture and the `user-select` toggle.
- `Menu.svelte` closes on Escape and outside pointer down, moves with arrows, and takes `glyph` and `compact`.
- `tests/ui/colours.test.ts` checks `color` and `background` declarations in `ui/`, `shell/` and `views/`; the one translucent indicator fill above is the only `color-mix` and the report says whether the gate accepted it as written or needed the rule's stated allowance for a non-text fill made explicit in the test (an allowance that names `color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) N%, transparent)` and nothing wider).
