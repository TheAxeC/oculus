2. **A test keeps it so.** `tests/history.test.ts` (new, in the shape of the other source audits): the pattern above over `src`, `tests` and `bin` (the test's own file excepted by its content, not by name), printing every hit as the failure message; the plan's id vocabulary is data at the top of the test with the reason it exists.
