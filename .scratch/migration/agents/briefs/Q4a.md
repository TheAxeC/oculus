# Brief: Q4a, the rail: counts and badges, the groups, the foot controls, the tray's place

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Four items Axel raised on 2026-09-12 after using the O25 landings, all on the rail (`shell/ActivityRail.svelte`, 424 lines) and the Update tray; each is a user-visible shape and is checked in Chrome on the TEST page at landing. The cockpit page each item is measured against is named in the item.

## What to build

1. **Counts and badges on the rail.** The cockpit drew a count on every tab (`tools/cockpit/src/cockpit/page.py` lines 486 to 500: for a collection, the entries whose status is in that tab's live set, the badge coloured by that status's colour; for the boards, the entries of the board) and four urgency badges (`render/command_center.py` `tab_badges`, lines 40 to 60: the inbox's untriaged threads in red, the leads due within `leads_badge_days` of `config/behavior.yml` (line 53, 14) in amber, a red mark when LinkedIn is overdue by `linkedin_cadence_days`, the active applications in green). The rail draws them the same way: the registry's `badge?: () => string` hook (`views/registry.ts` line 30, drawn by the rail at lines 197 to 200 as a small pill on the glyph, used by Live HPC through `state/hpc.svelte.ts` `badgeText`) gains a companion `badgeTone?: () => string` (a hue name from `state/tones.ts`, drawn as the chip's `--c-<tone>` pair; absent means the accent as now), and every view whose cockpit tab carried a number registers one in `routes/views.ts` from the client stores: Projects, Coauthor and Archive the count of their board (`state/selectors.svelte.ts` `board(boardPreset(view))`), every collection view the count of its live entries (the statuses `config/vocab.yml` calls live for that collection, the way the cockpit's `NAV_LIVE` did; read `page.py` around line 470 for that table and put the same table in `config/vocab.yml` or `layout.yml` as data, named here as a config shape), the Inbox the untriaged count in red, Position Leads the count due within `leads_badge_days` in amber, LinkedIn a red mark when overdue, Applications the active count in green, Live HPC as it is; the numbers come from the snapshot (`portfolio()`), the feed summary of the snapshot (`snapshot.entities.feeds`, what `state/feeds.ts` `attention()` and `refresh()` read) and the fetched feeds (`state/feeds.svelte.ts` `peekFeed`), never from a fetch the rail starts. A count of zero draws nothing. Tests: `tests/shell/rail.test.ts` (a badge with a tone drawn, none at zero), `tests/views/registry.test.ts` (the hooks registered), `tests/state/` for any new pure count.

2. **The rail groups.** Programs, Domains and Map sit under Boards in `config/layout.yml` `nav_groups` (line 15, `boards: [focus, projects, coauthor, archive, programs, domains, map]`), which Axel read as wrong ("Boards contains domains, pillars, map for some reason"). They move to a group of their own, `structure`, labelled "Structure" with the `pillar` glyph if the glyph catalog has one (else `programs`' own glyph), placed after Boards; the boards group keeps `[focus, projects, coauthor, archive]`. A `layout.yml` change, reversible by editing the file; `tests/config.test.ts` and the rail test follow.

3. **The foot controls aligned.** The rail's view buttons are `.view` (34 px high, the full rail column, the glyph at `--fs-xl`, lines 359 to 372); the group openers are a `Menu compact` inside `.flyout` (lines 172 to 180, margin-top 6 px), and the foot holds `ToolButton compact` controls centred in `.controls > div` (lines 218 to 240) plus the theme menu, the three toggles (`.toggle`, lines 384 on) and the panel dock. Those are four box shapes in one 52 px column, which is what Axel sees as misaligned. Every control in the rail takes the one box of `.view` (34 px, the column's width, the glyph centred), the openers and the foot controls included: give `Menu` and `ToolButton` a `rail` variant or wrap them in the `.view` box, whichever keeps the primitives' own tests green, and the three text toggles become glyph buttons in the same box with their words in the tooltip. Tests: `tests/shell/rail.test.ts` asserts every rail control carries the one class.

4. **The Update tray's place.** `shell/UpdateTray.svelte` is fixed at `top: 12px; left: calc(var(--rail-width) + 8px)` (lines 102 to 115), which reads as random. It opens the way the palette does (`shell/Palette.svelte`: a full-window ground, the dialog centred, 10vh from the top, `min(42rem, 100%)` wide, the ground's click closing it), with the same ground rule; the Escape handling stays in the workbench. Tests: `tests/shell/palette.test.ts`'s tray case asserts the ground and the dialog role.
