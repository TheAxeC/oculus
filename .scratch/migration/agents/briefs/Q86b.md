## What to build

1. **The drag physics** (`graph.ts`). `Physics` becomes `{ linkLength, linkStrength, damping, gap }` and `DEFAULT_PHYSICS` `{ linkLength: 107, linkStrength: 0.042, damping: 0.88, gap: 4 }`; `repulse`, `collide`, `center`, `cool` and `DRAG_PHYSICS` are removed with every use. `DragHold` gains `apart`: for every pair of a mover and any other node, the pair's distance at the press (a map keyed by the two ids in a fixed order), taken by `holdFor`. `physics(nodes, edges, hold, params = DEFAULT_PHYSICS, bounds = DEFAULT_BOUNDS)` runs, for the hold's movers only: the springs at the hold's lengths (an edge with no recorded length rests at `params.linkLength`), damping, a move of `DRAG_STEP` (0.4) times the velocity, then the push, then the bounds clamp. The push moves each mover off every other node closer than `min(a.radius + b.radius + params.gap, apart at the press)`, along the line between them, by the whole shortfall against a node that does not move and by half of it against another mover. A pair whose limit is 0 (coincident at the press) is never pushed. A pair apart at the press that is coincident in a frame is separated along x, with finite positions. The dragged node is not a mover and never moves under the tick. `dragTick(nodes, edges, hold)` returns `{ nodes, moved }`, `moved` the largest distance any node moved in that frame. `settle(nodes, edges, hold)` runs `dragTick` until `moved < REST` (0.01) or `MAX_FRAMES` (2000) frames, and returns `{ nodes, frames }`. `pressHold(nodes, edges, id, running)` answers the positions and the hold a new press starts from: when `running` holds the hold of a settle still going, it first finishes that settle with `settle(nodes, edges, running)` and takes the new hold from its positions.
2. **The drag loop** (`Graph.svelte`). `alpha`, `reheat`'s alpha and the cooling go. `step()` runs one `dragTick` and asks for the next frame while `moved >= REST` and fewer than `MAX_FRAMES` frames have run since the loop started; a pointer move while dragging starts the loop when none runs. `nodeDown` cancels a pending frame and takes its positions and hold from `pressHold`, so a press never inherits the last drag's movement. The `DragHold` literal at 84 gains `apart`. Any new `$effect` goes below the measuring effect, or `map.test.ts:501`'s regex is updated to find the measuring effect by its content.
3. **The pointer mapping** (a new module `src/lib/views/structure/map/pointer.ts`, used by `Graph.svelte`). Two pure functions. `toBox(client, rect)` maps a client point to the graph's box under `xMidYMid meet`: `scale = min(rect.width / WIDTH, rect.height / HEIGHT)`, the picture's left at `rect.left + (rect.width - WIDTH * scale) / 2` and its top at `rect.top + (rect.height - HEIGHT * scale) / 2`, the point `((x - left) / scale, (y - top) / scale)`; a rect of no size (scale 0, a hidden tab) answers `(0, 0)`. `toGraph(boxPoint, pan, zoom)` answers `((x - pan.x) / zoom, (y - pan.y) / zoom)`. `viewport()` is `toBox`, and `local()` is `toGraph` of it. The drag, the link pointer, the pan and the wheel zoom all go through them.
4. **The labels** (`labels.ts`). `shownLabels(boxes, first, nodes)` takes the nodes as `{ id, x, y, radius, kind }`. Order: the leading labels, drawn whatever they overlap; then the pillar labels (`kind === 'line'`) in the graph's order; then every other label in the graph's order. A label after the leading ones is left out when it overlaps a kept label or any node's shape other than its own node's. A project is a circle (`x`, `y`, `radius`); a pillar or a collaborator is the square `x - radius .. x + radius` by `y - radius .. y + radius`; a box overlaps a circle when the box's nearest point to the centre is closer than the radius. A label of zero width (not measured) overlaps nothing: that is checked first, against labels and nodes alike. `Graph.svelte` hands `positions` in.
5. **Tests.** In `graph.test.ts`, every call of `physics` and `dragTick` takes the new signature, and the repulsion case at 297, the `DRAG_PHYSICS` spread at 365, the collision case at 376 and the fields case at 395-400 are replaced by the cases below. The 44-node fixture lives in a new module `tests/state/boards/structure/map-fixture.ts` with `layout.test.ts`'s `node()` and `edge()` moved into it; `layout.test.ts` and `graph.test.ts` import them from there, never a copy, and `graph.test.ts` places the fixture with `createGraphviz().ready()` in a `beforeAll`. Every new or renamed case in `map-labels.test.ts` and every case of the new `tests/views/structure/map-pointer.test.ts` draws nothing, so each is named "audits ..."; a renamed case's old entry leaves `allow-audits.txt`, and no entry is added. Each new or changed test names the revert that turns it red.
6. **Documents and comments.** `DESIGN.md:291`'s drag sentences, `HELP.md:103`'s label sentence, `Graph.svelte`'s head comment, the `physics`, `holdFor`, `dragTick`, `settle` and `pressHold` docstrings in `graph.ts`, and `labels.ts`'s head comment say what the code now does: the springs at the recorded lengths with the push at a fixed step until rest; the pointer mapped through the letterbox; a label kept off every node but its own, a pillar's label placed right after the leading ones.
