## The mapping (the table for Axel; built as written)

Venue words to domain keys: `core-ml` and `method` to `method`; `theory` to `theory`; `cs` and `systems` to `cs`; `neuro` to `brain` and `brainbci`; `signal` to `heart`, `brain` and `brainbci`; `imaging` to `liver` and `liverfl`; `clinical` to `heart`, `liver` and `liversurv`; `informatics` to `privacy`, `liversurv` and `meta`; `federated` to `liverfl`, `immune` and `privacy`; `bio` to `immune`; `high-end` to nothing: it leaves the field, since the `tier` field already carries a venue's standing. A venue's new list is the union over its old words, in the order of the domain registry, without repeats.

Dataset words to domain keys: `single-cell` to `immune`; `eeg` to `brain`; `bci-ecog` to `brainbci`; `ecg` to `heart`; `mri-imaging` and `ct-imaging` to `liver`; `survival-tabular` to `liversurv`; `pde-operator` to `method`; `clinical-ehr` to `privacy` and `heart` (its four datasets serve the privacy and the cardiology projects); `mammography` to `method` (its one dataset, `ddsm`, has no project yet); `simulated-copula` to `method` (its one dataset serves `linfoot-estimator`, a method project). `theme_labels` goes: a domain has its title.

## The shapes this brief sets

1. The field keeps its position in each entry and takes the name `domains:` (a list of domain keys) in both registries, so the field says what its values are; the schema fields follow (`domains`, type `tags`, help "domains.yml keys"). A `themes:` still present on an entry after the migration is a finding naming the new key, as M1 does for `program:` (`RENAMED_FIELDS` in `data/validate.ts` grows a second pair, `themes: 'domains'`, for the two registries), and a `domains:` value that is no key of the domain registry is a finding.
2. The migration is one script under `tools/oculus/bin/` (`rename-themes-to-domains.ts` or the tool's own naming), run once by you over the worktree's hub and the demo, kept and idempotent, carrying the two tables above as data and mapping the demo's own words by the same rule (the demo's words that no row names are listed in the run's output and mapped by you by their meaning, in the script's demo table, so the demo stays a fixture that validates). It edits the field through `renameKey` and a value rewrite in `server/yaml-edit.ts`'s way, so comments, ordering and quoting of the hand-written files survive; the run prints per file what it wrote and which entries carried a word the table did not name.
3. Both tools' readers follow: oculus reads `domains` into `Venue` and `Dataset` (`domainIds: Id[]`, the relation tables `venueDomain` and `datasetDomain`, a domain's `venueIds` and `datasetIds`), draws the facets and the card words from the domain registry's titles and tones (`domainLabel`, `domainTone`), and the Domains view lists a domain's venues and datasets beside its projects; the cockpit's facets and Domains tab read `domains` and the domain titles, its selftest checks the keys, and `theme_labels` is read nowhere.
4. HELP.md and DESIGN.md (a decision line) say the field, the finding and the rule.
