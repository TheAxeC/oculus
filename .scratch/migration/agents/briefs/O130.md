## What to build

1. `readOtherHub` runs `categoriesFindings` over the other hub's merged config when that hub's settings file sets `categories` (`fromHubFile(from.categories)`), with `fileOf` giving that file (`join(root.dir, HUB_FILE)`, the `sources` path the `sourcesFindings` call beside it already uses) and the catalog `textCatalog(config)`, and adds its findings after `loaded.findings` and `sourcesFindings`. So a wrong `stages` or `forms` word and a row with no `color` in that hub's `categories:` are findings there, worded and keyed as the primary's run words and keys them. A hub that writes no `categories` gets no run: its rows are oculus's own, which the primary's run and the shipped-config test already check, and a run would say each of their findings once per hub. The word a finding names is not read, as for the primary (a word no project carries matches nothing).
2. The signature of `readOtherHub` and its callers stay as they are.
3. The doc comments of `readOtherHub` and of `OtherHub` say its findings are its settings file's, the folder readers' and the category check's over its own merged config. The head comment of `boards.ts` (lines 1-3) and of `boards.test.ts` (lines 1-4) say a hub's `categories` are checked against its merged config and name its settings file. Every other comment and document sentence the change makes false is rewritten, found by a grep of `readOtherHub`, `categoriesFindings` and `OtherHub` across `src`, `docs`, `config` and `tests`.
4. `docs/HELP.md` says, in the section "Hubs and projects" (after line 591), that a hub's `categories` rows are checked against that hub's own words and each finding names its `.oculus/config.yml`. One bullet, worded by the builder.
5. One test, a table, in `tests/data/sources/config/boards.test.ts`: a new describe 'the category rows of a hub that is not the primary', one row per case C1 to C7 below, each planting a scratch hub, reading it with `readOtherHub({ key, dir }, loadConfig(engineDir(), { hub: dir, other: key }))` and comparing every finding whose key starts with `categories` as `[file, key, message]` with the expected. It is G124's test; it is run on the unchanged tree first and the report quotes that run. C1, C2, C3 and C5 fail there, which is their proof; C4, C6 and C7 pass there and after, and are audits of what the change preserves (no run for a hub that writes no rows, the hub's own words, the hub layer's findings said once).
