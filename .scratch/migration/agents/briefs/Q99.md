## What to build

1. **A terminal's input sent as its bytes.** The page's input reaches the pane as the bytes it is, whole, through `send-keys -H`. In `tmux.ts`, `splitKeys`, `keyName`, `SEQUENCES` and `SINGLES` are removed with their tests, and a pure `inputChunks(bytes: Buffer, most = 4096): Buffer[]` cuts the input into pieces of at most `most` bytes, never inside a UTF-8 character (a cut never falls before a byte `0x80`-`0xBF`) and never inside an escape sequence: from an `ESC` (`0x1b`) a sequence is open until its end, a CSI (`ESC [`) at its final byte `0x40`-`0x7E` after parameter bytes `0x30`-`0x3F` and intermediate bytes `0x20`-`0x2F`, an SS3 (`ESC O`) at the byte after `O`, an OSC (`ESC ]`), DCS (`ESC P`), APC (`ESC _`) or PM (`ESC ^`) at `BEL` (`0x07`) or `ESC \`, and any other `ESC x` at `x`. When no cut point exists within `most` bytes (one sequence longer than that), the piece ends at the last UTF-8 boundary within `most`. In `sessions.ts`, `sendBytesArgs(id, bytes)` gives `['send-keys', '-t', <session>, '-H', ...hex]`, two lowercase hex digits per byte. `#input` in `channel.ts` sends `inputChunks(Buffer.from(encoded, 'base64'))` one piece per command, in order, stopping at the first failure as now. The page side (`sendInput`) is unchanged. `sendKeysArgs`, `steerArgs` and `interruptTerminal` stay as they are.
2. **A tab closes when its terminal ends.** The terminals store is handed, in `createClientState`, the Terminal tabs of every page (`layout.pages()`, `tabsOf`, the view `COMPOSER_VIEW`, each tab's id, page and instance state), `closeInstance`, and the selection's `selectedTerminal` and `select`. On each `sessions` message (`entities.afterReceive`), a terminal has ended when the message lists terminals and that id is not among them, and either an earlier `sessions` message this page received listed it, or this is the first `sessions` message the page received (a terminal that ended while the page was away). A tab pinned to an ended terminal closes, on whichever page it is. When the selected terminal has ended, the selection is cleared and every Terminal tab that follows the selection closes. A terminal started from this page and not yet listed is not ended, since no message listed it and the first message came before it. The decision "which ids have ended" is a pure function in `terminals.svelte.ts` (the ids listed before, the ids listed now, whether this is the first message) with its own tests; the store holds the set of ids it has seen listed.
3. **A new shell's prompt where tmux has its cursor.** `#attach` reads the cursor with a new `cursorArgs(id)` (`display-message -p -t <session> '#{cursor_x} #{cursor_y} #{pane_height}'`) and the capture as now, and a pure `screenAt(capture, cursor)` in `sessions.ts` keeps the capture's lines up to the cursor's row (the row index in the capture is its line count less `pane_height` plus `cursor_y`, with the capture's final newline not counted as a line) and ends with `ESC [ <cursor_x + 1> G`, so the page's cursor lands on tmux's row and column. The CR LF rewrite stays. A cursor read that fails or does not parse sends the whole capture as now, and says so on the console like the capture's own failure.
4. **The Pages view as list rows.** Each page is a `TreeItem` row in a `TreeView` (as the Branches view draws its rows): the page's name as the label at the tree's font size, `current` on the page on screen (bold with its check, in place of the "shown" tag), the rail key as the row's muted `detail` (`workbench.pages_on_rail`, in place of the tag), the unsaved mark as the row's `end` (the dirty glyph, muted, with `workbench.pages_unsaved` as its accessible name and tooltip), the rename box as the row's `field`, and Rename, Reset (rail pages only) and Close as the row's `actions`, shown on hover and focus. The row's press shows the page (`switchPage`), replacing the Show control; the drag from the row keeps working (a press held until it crosses `THRESHOLD`, then `startPage`), started by a `pointerdown` on the row. New page stays above the list. `workbench.pages_current` and `workbench.pages_open` leave the catalog when nothing reads them.
5. **Docs.** Every sentence of `DESIGN.md` and `HELP.md` the change makes false is corrected (grep for `send-keys`, `scrollback`, `Pages view`, `Show`, `exit`); a terminal's input as bytes and the tab closing on a terminal's end are stated where the terminal is described. `npm run diagrams` rerun.
