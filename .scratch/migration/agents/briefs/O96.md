## What to build

1. **The strip is grouped by `categories:`.** `groupProjects` gives one group per row of `categories:` that holds a project of the strip, in the list's order, each project in its category as `categoryOf` files it under its own hub's words; a row carrying `archival: true` (dead-ends) draws no group, its projects left out; a project in no row is drawn in one last group, "other", as a project board's "other" section is. Members stay sorted as now and nested by their own stage inside a group. A group is flagged with the warning when one of its projects carries a chip whose word carries `blocked`, in whatever category. No category key, label or colour is named in code: they are read from the rows.
   - Every strip `groupProjects` draws changes with it: a collection entry's card, a person's per-role strips, a record card, the project card's related strip; the Lit Watch card's id set is unchanged in what it collects apart from the archival and no-row rule above.
   - `CrossLinks.svelte` heads a category's group with the row's label in the row's colour and its count, as a board's section heading draws the category, and keeps the warning glyph and the stage nesting; the Archival card's groups per stage are drawn as now. The heading's colour is given to `Disclosure.svelte` as an optional tone prop (a `toneOf` tone, as a board's section heading takes it), its other users unchanged. The text words `xlink_*` of `project.yml` that no code then uses are removed, with their glyph words, so a category's group head carries no glyph (the rows carry none), the Archival card's stage heads keeping theirs. With the entries of several hubs on one strip, the groups stand in the order of every hub's rows as a board's sections do (`BoardPreset.categories`), each project in its own hub's category.
2. **A Projects section in a collection's More.** A collection view in which at least one entry's card draws a project strip (the projects of `crossLinks`, or of `roleLinks` for a person) gets, in More of its bar and only there, a section headed by the word "Projects" holding six groups, in this order: Category (the rows of `categories:` by label and colour, archival rows left out), Stage (the words of `stages:` by label), Form, Tier, Role (drawn only while a person is marked as the user, as on a board) and Pillar, each value read and labelled as the project boards read and label it: the project items are built by `boardItemFor` over `mapPreset` and their Form, Tier, Role and Pillar groups by `facets` and `filterGroups`, as the Map's bar builds them; the Category group lists the board's categories (every hub's rows, archival ones left out, with "other" for projects in no row) by `item.category`; the Stage group lists the stages as the Map's `stageGroup` lists them, exported from `map-bar.ts` for this, with no keep-live rule (nothing picked keeps every project). `FilterBar` and `PickMenu` gain one optional prop that heads a run of More's groups with one heading, their other callers unchanged. The picks are saved under the group ids `project_category`, `project_stage`, `project_form`, `project_tier`, `project_role` and `project_pillar`, apart from line 1's `role`. A view whose entries draw no strip gets no section. The list mode draws no strip, so the section is offered in list mode too and a pick hides the rows it would hide as cards.
   - A value's count is how many distinct projects of the strips of the entries the bar keeps (by the search, Mine only and the entry groups) carry that value, under the other project groups' picks; a picked value no project carries is listed at 0, as an entry group lists one, and can be taken out. The entry groups' counts and line 2's status counts are taken over the entries the project picks leave, as the bar's rule takes every group's count under the other groups' picks. A pick saved while its group is not drawn (a Role pick after the self is unmarked) narrows nothing, as the bar's rule says (`bar.ts:204-205`).
   - A pick narrows each card's strip to the projects that carry a picked value of every project group with a pick (any of the picked values within one group), its group counts and the person card's per-role strips with it. While a project group has a pick, an entry whose strip is left empty is hidden, and is counted out of line 2's "5 of 12 shown"; with no project pick, an entry with no strip is shown as now.
   - The picks are kept in the tab's state with the other picks; Clear all takes them out too. The entry's own page draws the whole strip, unfiltered. The No funding card is a card of the Funding view: its strip narrows and it hides when emptied like every card.
3. **The words and the page.** `docs/HELP.md:296` and `:301` say the strip's groups as item 1 gives them; `:277` says the Projects section of More, its groups, its counts and that a pick hides an entry left with no project. The head comments of `cross-links.ts`, `CrossLinks.svelte`, `bar.ts`, `Collection.svelte`, `EntryCard.svelte`, `FilterBar.svelte`, `PickMenu.svelte`, `Disclosure.svelte` and `map-bar.ts` (where they change) say what they now do, and each sentence listed under "What is on the tree" that the step makes false (the `vocab.yml`, options catalog, `vocab.ts` and `CardLinks.svelte` lines) says what is now true: the strip groups by the rows of `categories:` and leaves out a row carrying `archival`.
