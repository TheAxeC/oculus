## What to build

1. **The flat rows.** A pure function in `turn-rows.ts` turns `TurnRow[]` and the runs' open state into the rows the window draws: a said turn is one row, a closed run or a single call is one row, and an open run is its press as one row plus one row per call, so no size escapes the window (ruling 634). Each flat row has a string key that holds across reads: a call's row is keyed by that call's own turn index, which a read never changes, and a run's press by a key that cannot equal a call's. `TurnRun.svelte` draws one flat row, the press or one call, and keeps today's look, its rows standing together with no gap between them.
2. **A windowed list.** `TurnList.svelte` draws only the flat rows in view and `OVERSCAN` rows past each edge, through `@tanstack/virtual-core`, driven from a new `turn-window.svelte.ts` made in `TranscriptBody.svelte`, so heights survive `held.error` unmounting the list. It uses `rowWindow`, `keptRange` and `offsetIn` from `virtual.ts` rather than copies; `virtual.ts`'s line 1 stops saying explorer and says what is true. The height of the rows not drawn stands before each drawn row and after the last (`DrawnRow.gap`, `RowWindow.after`), including between a kept row and the window.
3. **Heights.** ResizeObserver stays the trigger, and the value comes from `getBoundingClientRect` through the `measureElement` option, since a ResizeObserver entry's size leaves CSS zoom out and the default rounds. `style:zoom` moves from `.turns` onto each row's box, and the spacers carry no zoom, so a spacer's px are the column's px and `scrollHeight` equals the rows' sum within a pixel. `.turns` loses its `gap`. Each row's box holds the space above it as padding from data, so the sum of heights is the column's height. The look of a first row comes from data (index 0 of the whole list), never from `:first-child`. A row not yet measured takes an estimate from its own kind and length, above 0, taken once for its key and never changed by later measurements, so measuring one row moves no other row's height. `measure()` is not how an estimate is refreshed. `data-index` is written from the current index at every draw, so a read of older turns hands no height to another key.
4. **Dropping heights.** Heights, estimates and held open state are dropped with the target, where `docs.clear()` runs, and when a read from the start replaces the list. After a width or zoom change the drawn rows measure again, and undrawn heights stand as estimates until they draw. `retakeZoom` asks for that measure itself, since a pure zoom change may fire no observer.
5. **One owner of `scrollTop`.** `reader-place.ts` owns every correction. The core's own is off (`shouldAdjustScrollPositionOnItemSizeChange` returns false) and `.chat` sets `overflow-anchor: none`, so three writers do not double a correction. `reader-place.ts` finds a row by its key among the rows drawn; each drawn row carries its key as a data attribute. `take` runs before a draw and on scroll, and `hold` runs after every draw and after every height change, so a row above the view whose measured height differs from its estimate moves no words. When no row stands at the view's top (a fast drag leaves it in a spacer), nothing is taken and the words do not jump on release. The place and the foot stay hand-written rather than `anchorTo` and `followOnAppend`, because the rule that a read of older turns holds by the row past the top (`Anchor.past`) has no form in those options. The comment at `reader-place.ts:12-13` is corrected.
6. **The foot.** While `stuck`, every height change goes to the foot again, so `toFoot`, a first draw and following the newest turn reach the true foot however far the estimates were. `.chat` carries a `data-windowed` mark, and `scrollRegion` in `key-dom.ts` scrolls such a box at once rather than smoothly, since `hold` writing `scrollTop` would cancel a smooth scroll; `G` and `gg` then reach the true foot and the head.
7. **Older turns and a hidden tab.** `askOlder` reads as now. The scroll margin (`offsetIn`) is read again when `held.older` or `olderError` changes. A column with no height keeps the last window, measures nothing and overwrites no height, since the core gives no rows there. A list replaced by a shorter one reads no row past its end, and a window change settles after one draw, as `rowWindow`'s held window gives.
8. **Selection.** A pure function in `turn-window.svelte.ts` answers which rows a selection keeps, from the two ends' row keys and the rows: both keys give every row between them, whichever way round; a collapsed selection gives none; a key gone from the rows gives the nearest row that stands; an end with no key, before or after the list, reads as the list's first or last row; a selection covering the list gives every held row, which is Cmd+A (ruling 634). `selectionchange` drives it, so Copy gives the whole text as today, with a slow moment accepted on a very long list. The row holding the focus stays drawn too.
9. **A row that leaves the page.** An open `details`, a code block's or table's sideways scroll are held by the row's key and restored when it draws again (ruling 634). A fragment link to a heading in an undrawn row draws that row and then scrolls to it through `scrollToFragment`, with the press heard in `TranscriptBody.svelte`; `jump.ts` changes only to export what that needs. Tab moves on through rows as they draw: from the last focusable drawn, Tab and Shift+Tab reach the next row that holds one, drawing it first, and past the last such row Tab leaves the list as today.
10. **Menu, runs and keyboard.** The Transcript's one menu, including a right-click on a spacer with a selection standing, a run's press, links in answers, and PageUp, PageDown, Home and End on the column work as they do now.
11. **Comments and pages.** The head comments of every file changed say what is true after the change. `docs/HELP.md` after line 459 says that Transcript draws only the turns near the view, and that the browser's Find therefore reaches only those turns.

## Cases

The look at landing proves these, on a scratch server, with a session of 960 turns, the same with a run of 500 calls, and a session of 20000 turns of mixed lengths that the session generates into the scratch HOME with a throwaway generator in its scratch folder (ruling 634). Rows are of mixed heights, far from the estimate.

- Any size, scrolled to the head: at every scroll position the page holds at most the rows in view plus the margin, counted with `querySelectorAll` on the drawn rows; a run of 500 calls open or a failed run by itself stays inside that count.
- 20000 turns: no long task over 100 ms while scrolling turns already held, and the count holds.
- Scrolling up slowly through unmeasured rows of mixed heights: the top of the row at the view's top stays within 1 px of where the scroll put it.
- Dragging the bar fast to the head: rows draw, older turns load, the words do not jump on release, and the foot flag is right.
- A read of older turns with the reader near the head, the top row losing its head and the top run extended: the words stay within 1 px after the measure.
- An append much taller than the estimate, reader at the foot: at the true foot. Reader above: nothing moves.
- A result that opens a run inside the margin, and one outside it, above the view: the words stay.
- A press on a run in view, then closed: the choice stands, also after it scrolls out and back.
- An image loading in a row above the view while scrolling up: no words move.
- A selection from a turn at the head to a turn 300 rows on, by a drag and by a shift-click over rows never drawn: Copy equals those turns' text. It stands while scrolling.
- Cmd+A then Copy: every held turn's text. A selection cleared: the rows kept go back to the window.
- A right-click on selected text, on nothing, and on a spacer with a selection standing: the same menu as now.
- Focus on a Copy button in a row, then scrolling away: focus stays and the row stays drawn.
- Tab through a stretch past the margin, forward and back: focus reaches each row that holds a focusable, in order.
- An open `details` and a sideways-scrolled code block that leave and return: as they were.
- A fragment link to a heading in an undrawn row: it scrolls there. In the same answer: as now.
- The tab hidden with a turn arriving meanwhile, then shown, at the foot and in the middle: back at the foot, or at the same words.
- A session switched away and back: opens at the foot, a run's press stands, and the other session's foot is right too.
- A pane resized narrower while reading in the middle: the words at the view's top stay, and scrolling on past old-width heights moves none.
- The text size changed: `scrollHeight` matches the rows within a pixel at a zoom other than 1.
- A wallpaper theme: no surface painted where a spacer stands.
- A session of one turn and of none, and a failed read of older turns with its line and Try again: as now.
- A transient `held.error`, then the list back: heights stand.
- End, the tab's Jump to the last turn, and `G`, with rows far from the estimate: the true foot. `gg`: the head.
- PageUp, PageDown and Home on the column, and a link in an answer: as now.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Rows leave the page, as the ruling says. `content-visibility: auto` keeps every row in the page and so does not end the cause.
2. The browser's Find reaches only the rows drawn. It follows from the ruling, the Help page says so, and the report to the user names it.
3. A new file beside the view drives the virtualizer, importing `virtual.ts`'s rules. `VirtualRows.svelte` draws `li` elements and probes `--tree-row`, and a Transcript's rows are neither; a rule of `virtual.ts` is reused, never copied.
4. The store keeps its turns. Dropping them is a different concern from what the page draws, and a turn's text is capped.
5. `zoom` moves from the list to each row's box, so a spacer needs no division by zoom.
6. `G` and `gg` scroll at once on a windowed column.
