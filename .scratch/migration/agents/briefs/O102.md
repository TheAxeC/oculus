## What to build

The rulings this step carries are open items 539, 540, 542, 543, 544, 549, 557, 558, 559 (as corrected above), 560, 561 and 564 to 568 of `plan.md`'s Rulings; read each whole.

1. **The publication record.** A manifest of a paper on the website gains `publication:`, one record (540 (1)). Typed in it (543 (1), 544): `authors` (one free-text field as the website prints it), `book`, `program` (a list of words of `website.yml`'s `programs:`), `url`, `links` (a list of rows `{ name, url }` in the order the website prints them, without `Code`), `abstract`, `pub_last` and `hide`. Generated from the manifest: `title` (its `title`), `pub_date` (its `year`), the section, `order`, `pub_pre`, `pub_post` and the `Code` link. The record's fields are entered in `config/schemas/manifest-schema.yml` with the project form's sections and help. A `program` word `website.yml` lacks is a finding of the data check naming the manifest and the word; the check reads the words from the website folder's `website.yml`.
2. **The generated publication fields** (G139).
   - The section: the first row of `website.kinds` whose `stages` and `forms` hold the manifest's, with the catch-all `audit.website.kinds` applies today kept as it is (a published paper with no matching row goes to `journal` or `conference` by its form as today; read `expectedSections`). A row's `kind` is a list, as today.
   - `order`, for the four paper sections only (560 (1)): `first` when `self` is in `lead` (co-first authors included), `senior` when in `supervisors`, `coauthor` when in `collaborators`. A paper whose manifest names `self` in none of the three gives no entry and a finding naming the manifest.

   - A manifest at a stage that gives no section, or at a stage with the `negative` or `dead_end` flag, that carries `publication:` gives no entry and a finding naming the manifest. A project of a tier `research_audit.skip_tiers` names is handled as any other manifest carrying `publication:`.
