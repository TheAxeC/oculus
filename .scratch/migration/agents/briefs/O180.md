## What to build

The rulings are `plan.md` Rulings "Open item 744" and "Open item 745". When this step is done, no field name, list key or word of the website's publications file stands in `audit/website/`; the only record keys named in the reader and the writer are the three oculus itself works with, `program`, `links` and `rank`.

1. **The rows.** `website.paper_fields`, read by `websiteSettings` into `paperFields`, rows in the file's order. A row is `{ key, from }`. `key` is required and matches `/^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]*$/`, as a CV section's key. `from` is one of `project_title`, `project_year`, `author_place`, `kind_pre`, `kind_post`, `links_with_code`, or left out or the empty text for the paper's own field of that name. A row its shape refuses is left out with a `shape` problem at the row, and a row whose key an earlier row holds with a `repeat` problem, the earlier one standing, as `cvSections()` does.
2. **The three words.** `website.paper_list` (text), `website.code_link_name` (text) and `website.author_places.lead`, `.supervisor`, `.collaborator` (texts), read by `websiteSettings` into `paperList`, `codeLinkName` and `authorPlaces`.
3. **Default, Settings, hub list.** `paper_fields: []`, `paper_list: ''`, `code_link_name: ''` and `author_places: { lead: '', supervisor: '', collaborator: '' }` in `config/defaults/behavior.yml`; entries for each in `config/options/behavior.yml`, `website.paper_fields` with fields `key` (text) and `from` (choice over the six words, `empty: true`), each `help` saying what it is; each key on its own line in `HUB_LIST` beside `website.kinds`, so each is a key of the primary hub.
4. **The reader.** `publication()` keeps every key of the map whose value is not null, copied with `Object.fromEntries`, so a key `__proto__` is an own key. `program`, `links` and `rank` are read as they are on the tree. Any other key is held as the file gives it. `Publication` says that: the three typed fields and any other key as `unknown`.
5. **The writer.** `PapersInput` gains `paperFields`, `paperList`, `codeLinkName` and `authorPlaces`. `entryOf` writes one key per row, in the rows' order: a row with no `from` takes the record's own field of its key, an own property only; a row with a `from` takes that word's value: `project_title` the manifest's title, `project_year` its year, `author_place` the word of `authorPlaces` for the user's place where the kind says so, `kind_pre` and `kind_post` the words of the row of `website.kinds`, `links_with_code` the links with the code link last under `codeLinkName`. A value that is undefined writes no key. Each section's list goes under `paperList`. `differences` in `website/files.ts` counts the order of a map under the key of the row whose `from` is `links_with_code` and no other. `websiteFiles` and its callers (`audit/publications.ts`, `runs/website.ts`) pass the settings on.
6. **A word not set.** A deposited project whose link needs `code_link_name`, or an entry whose place needs a word of `author_places`, that the setting does not hold: the link or the key is left out and there is one problem `audit.msg_site_no_word` per setting key per build, about nothing, naming the whole key, in the form of `noWord`, with its sentence in `audit.yml` and its key in `ProblemKey`.
7. **Empty.** While `paperFields` is empty or `paperList` is the empty text, a press of Write the website is refused with a new word `website-no-paper-fields`, checked right after `website-no-cv-sections`, its sentence in `career.yml` beside `website_no_cv_sections` in the same form and naming both settings; the button is disabled in `off()` with it; the audit offers no press while `cvSections` or `paperFields` is empty or `paperList` is the empty text, and the two sentences of `audit.yml:61-62` name the three settings. Their keys are not renamed.
8. **Comments, the template page, tests.** Every head and doc comment the change makes false says what the code does now: `papers.ts`, `publication.ts`, `publications.ts:4`, `runs/website.ts:9-12`, `website.svelte.ts:6-7`, `settings/files.ts:321-327`, `WebsiteSettings`, `website/files.ts:10-12`, `WEBSITE_REFUSALS`, `config/defaults/behavior.yml:34`, `config/options/behavior.yml:1636`, and any other your own grep finds. `docs/templates.md` lists the new keys in the commented defaults and each in the example, and its sentence on `website:` says what they are. The test hub's settings in `tests/fixtures/hubs/website-hub.ts` hold the lines of item 9. Every test that builds a website setting or a `website:` block for a press holds what it needs (`tests/routes/docs/audit.test.ts`, `tests/routes/sessions/website.test.ts`, `tests/server/root/watch/website-watch.test.ts`, `tests/state/sessions/source/website-store.test.ts`). The new rows go in the new file `tests/data/website/paper-fields.test.ts`, and any row that must move to keep `website-files.test.ts` at or under 880 lines goes there too.
9. **The hub's lines.** These lines write today's file unchanged, and the test hub's settings hold them (item 8).
