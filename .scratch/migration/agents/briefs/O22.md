# Brief: O22, the Focus board

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Then `tools/oculus/DESIGN.md` lines 168 to 187 (Layer 3: a view reads selectors, composes primitives, writes its own instance state; the boundary table) and line 245 (step 7: the remaining views, Focus first). Focus is the landing board: what is being worked on now, grouped into lanes by each manifest's `focus:`, under a ticker of everything with a date. The cockpit's `render/focus.py` and `render/command_center.py` are the cautionary reference for which facts the board has to show; every choice below is justified from `DESIGN.md`, and the cockpit's drag, click-to-cycle and quick-add controls are not carried as they were.

## What this package is, and what it is not

In: the data fields the board reads that the model does not carry yet; the ticker ("This week": overdue, deadlines by category, follow-ups, subtask deadlines and open subtasks, venue load, snoozed); the lanes in three clusters; the ideas backlog; the "awaiting a response" strip; the funding, applications and teaching band; two writes through the manifest writer (a card's lane, and its priority). Out, each booked in the plan by the orchestrator: the career panel (a new package O22b, since `CAREER.yml` has no source module yet); the refresh nudges, the inbox and LinkedIn counts and the position-lead deadlines (O25, with the feeds they read); the "manifests behind their docs" line (O28's Audit, which is where file times are read). The Data view's recovery command is O25's.

## What to build

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/data/`:

- `model.ts`, `Project` extended (lines 170 to 222): `priority?: string` (`manifest-schema.yml` line 39: `high`, `med`, `low` from `vocab.yml`'s `priority_levels`), `waitingOn: Id[]` (schema line 52, `waiting-on`: collaborator keys or free names), `followups: Followup[]` (schema lines 56 to 57, `followup`: a string, a `{ do, after }` map, or a list of either; `Followup = { do: string; after?: string }`), `snooze?: string` (schema line 58, an ISO date), `subtasks: Subtask[]` (schema line 74, `subtasks`: a map or list of `{ do, deadline, done, snooze }`; `Subtask = { key: string; do: string; deadline?: string; done: boolean; snooze?: string }`). `Venue` extended (lines 222 to 240): `deadline?: string` and `deadlineMonth?: string` (`registries/venues.yml` lines 30 to 31, a conference's next call and its usual month). `normalise.ts` (`project()` at lines 130 to 175 and the venue mapper) carries them, each from the manifest key of the same meaning and nothing else; a malformed value is dropped, not a finding, the way `str()` and `strings()` already behave. `tests/data/normalise.test.ts` gains one case per field, and `tests/integrity.test.ts` stays at zero findings on the real hub (run it; it reads the hub read-only).

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/state/`:

- `focus.ts`, pure functions over the snapshot, config and `now`, tested without a DOM: `lanes(config)` (the `focus_lanes` of `vocab.yml` lines 172 to 181, with `cluster` `now`, `parked`, `incubate`, and the `holding_color` of line 186); `laneItems(projects, lanes)` (every `active` or `manuscript` project by its `focus`, sorted deadline first then title; `holding` and unset are not lanes); `backlog(projects, lanes)` (the ideas: `focus` `holding`, or type `idea` with a focus that is no lane); `unplaced(projects)` (active or manuscript with no `focus`); `waiting(projects, venues, collaborators)` (the strip: people from `waitingOn` resolved to names through `collaborators`, papers under review by venue from the `submitted` chip, `blocked` from that chip; the cockpit's `helpers.py` lines 102 to 118 is the precedence); `ticker(snapshot, config, now)` answering `{ overdue, deadlines, followups, subtasks, venueLoad, snoozed }` with every item `{ kind, glyph, label, days, id | entry, snoozed }`: overdue is any project deadline in the past on a project whose chips are not terminal, any funding entry in prep with a past `deadline`, any events, education, tasks, applications or collaborations entry with a past `deadline` and a status the palette does not mark `terminal` (`vocab.yml` lines 116 to 141, `status_palette`, and `tones.ts`); deadlines is the coming ones inside `behavior.yml`'s `deadline_horizon_days`, grouped by category in this order: papers (a project's `deadline`, with its venue's short name folded in when `venueId` names a conference, whose own call line is then dropped), funding, talks and events, teaching, collaborations, tasks, conference calls (a venue with `deadline` inside the window, or a `deadlineMonth` within `conf_horizon_months`); followups is every project or entry `followup` whose `after` is unset or past, oldest due first; subtasks is every pending subtask of a project or entry (a parent with a terminal status takes its subtasks off the board unless the palette marks it `live_subtasks`), dated ones with their days and undated ones grouped by the parent's `kind` field; venueLoad is a warning when `behavior.yml`'s `venue_conc_warn` or more in-flight projects name one venue; a project or entry whose `snooze` is a future date goes to `snoozed` instead of any group. `band(collections, config)`: the funding entries stacked by `fund_stages` (`vocab.yml` lines 199 to 205), the applications entries awaiting an outcome, the education entries of this academic year, each with its deadline days. Dates are compared through `daysUntil` of `state/board.ts` lines 176 to 230, and the `DEADLINE_SOON` there is the tone break.

## Facts checked on the tree, 2026-09-11

- `Project` carries `focus`, `deadline`, `next`, `chips`, `venueId` and `collaborators` (`model.ts` lines 170 to 222) and not `priority`, `waiting-on`, `followup`, `snooze` or `subtasks`, which the schema names (`manifest-schema.yml` lines 39, 52 to 58, 74). `Venue` carries no `deadline`. Collection entries are raw `fields` maps (`model.ts` line 310), so an entry's `deadline`, `status`, `followup`, `subtasks`, `snooze` and `kind` are read from `fields` and need no model change.
- `vocab.yml` holds `focus_lanes` (nine lanes in three clusters), `holding_color`, `priority_levels` (three, with `flames`) and `fund_stages`; `status_palette` marks terminal statuses with `terminal: true` and `ongoing` with `live_subtasks: true`.
- `CAREER.yml` exists at the hub root and `sources.yml` names it as `career_file`, but no source module reads it: the career panel is O22b.
- No feed (inbox, leads, radar) is in the snapshot: `data/sources/` has no feed module. The refresh nudges and the inbox, LinkedIn and position-lead lines are O25's.
- `text.yml` is at 866 lines; the `focus` and `cc` groups already hold the words.
