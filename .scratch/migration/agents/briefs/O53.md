## What to build

1. **`lead:` is a list of person keys (ruling 205).** In `manifest-schema.yml`, `lead` is `type: chips, from: people`, in the Connections section beside `collaborators`, its label "Lead" and its help saying it names the person or people who lead the project, several for co-first authors, picked from the people list. The model's `lead` is `Id[]` (no longer optional text), read with `strings(manifest.lead)`, joined through the relation tables as `collaborators` is (`links.ts`, its own table or the `projectPerson` table read for both, a key the registry does not hold dropped as a collaborator's is), resolved own hub first in `hubs/merge.ts` as `collaborators` is (`at('people', 'lead')`), and listed in `NOT_BARE` as a list. A person referred to by a project's `lead` is a referrer in `referrers.ts`, as a collaborator is. No code reads a text `lead:`: a manifest that still writes `lead: Faes` gets G64's one type finding for a bare or non-list relation field, and its project has no lead. The form's value is `lead: project.lead.map(keyOf)`, as `collaborators` is, so opening a project's form and saving another field leaves `lead:` as written. The doc comments `model-entities.ts:769,801` and `index.ts:197` name the lead beside the collaborators, and `Person.projectIds` holds the projects whose lead or collaborators name the person.
2. **`authors:` leaves the manifest (ruling 251).** `authors` is removed from `manifest-schema.yml`, the model (`authors?`), `normalise.ts` (`authorsShape`, the field), `presets.ts` (`authorsShape`) and the form's values (`schema.ts:337`). A manifest that still writes `authors:` gets the schema's finding for a field no schema declares (G56). No code reads it.
3. **The team line (rulings 205, 251).** `authorLine` becomes the team line: the names of `lead:` then `collaborators:`, each drawn from the people registry (the person's `name`, its key where the registry holds no name), a person on both lists once, in that order, and without the person the workspace's `self` names (the person whose `self` is true). Its name and doc comment say what it draws, and the re-export in `src/lib/state/boards/board/index.ts` follows its name. The card's authors line (`ProjectCard.svelte`) and the Authors column (`Projects.svelte`, the cell `authors`) draw it; a project whose team is only `self` draws no line.
4. **Every view of a project's people reads the team (ruling 205).** The board item gains the team's tags (lead first) for the card's people tags: `CardLinks.svelte` draws the team without `self`, under the label of a new text key that names the team, in place of the collaborators-only tags (`card.ts`'s group for the new tags gives `person`, and the key `project.card.collaborators` goes when nothing reads it); the search haystack holds the whole team's tags, `self` included, so a search finds a project by any of its people; a joint work's projects (`collection.ts`) are those whose team holds its person; the Person page's projects (`person.ts`, `Person.svelte`) are those whose `lead:` or `collaborators:` name the person; the Map's people option (`graph.ts`, `Map.svelte`, `Controls.svelte`) draws a node and an edge for each person of the team other than `self`. Every head comment and doc comment of a changed reader says it reads the team, and the catalog lines `person.yml` `no_projects` and `map.yml` `collaborators` name the team.
5. **The person picker adds a person (ruling 251).** In a field whose options come from `people` alone (`lead`, `collaborators`, every other schema field `from: people` drawn by `MultiSelect.svelte`, and a `type: select, from: people` field such as the joint-work `person`, drawn by `Select.svelte`), when the typed text, compared without case, is the name or the key of no person of the registry, those already chosen included, the picker offers one more choice, "Add person <typed text>", its words a new text key. Choosing it writes a new entry to the people registry of the hub the edited record is in, through the existing `new-entry` write of the collection `people`, with `key` the typed name as `addTask` makes a key (lowercase ASCII words joined by hyphens, accents off, `-2`, `-3` while taken in that registry) and `name` the typed text trimmed; on success the new key is picked into the field, and a refusal of the write is said where the form says its errors (`EntryForm.svelte` `notesOf`), with nothing picked; the handler and the record's hub reach the field from `Editor.svelte` and `EntryEditor.svelte` through `EntryForm.svelte` and `FieldControl.svelte`. While the write is in flight the Add choice is not offered again; a form closed or discarded after the write was sent leaves the new person in the registry. Typed text that gives no key (no ASCII letter or digit) offers no "Add person" choice. The key maker, cut at `TASK_KEY_MAX` as a task's key is, lives beside `taskFields` in `src/lib/state/forms/new-entry.ts` or in a file beside it, shared with it rather than copied. A field that is not `from: people` alone gets no such choice (`waiting-on`, `from: [people, projects]`, keeps its free text). `MultiSelect.svelte` and `Select.svelte` take the extra choice and its handler from their caller; they learn nothing of people.
