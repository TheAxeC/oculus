## What to build

The ruling "Submission date" in `plan.md` Rulings decides what is built: a manifest key `submitted:`, type date, in the Dates section, holding the day of the latest submission to the venue that holds the paper now; oculus draws it, with the days since, on a project's card and on its Focus lane card while its stage is submitted (`pauses_countdown`); the Review lane orders cards of equal deadline by it, the oldest first.

1. **The key.** `config/schemas/manifest-schema.yml` gets a field `submitted` beside `deadline`: `label: "Submitted"`, `type: date`, `section: Dates`, and help words that say it is the day the paper was last submitted to the venue that holds it now, written `YYYY-MM-DD`, drawn with the days since it on the project's card and its Focus lane card while the stage is submitted. `Project` gets `submitted?: string`, documented as the manifest's `submitted`; `normalise.ts` fills it with `str(manifest.submitted)` beside `deadline`; `fieldValues` in `src/lib/state/forms/schema.ts` gives the form `submitted: project.submitted`, so the editor opens with the manifest's date and a save that leaves it untouched writes it back as it was.
2. **The fact.** One function in `src/lib/state/boards/focus.ts`, beside `cardYear` and written like it, gives the fact a card draws: the date as written and the whole days from it to today (`daysUntil` negated), only when the project's stage carries `pauses_countdown` in the `stages` of the project's own hub (read with `hasWord`, as `cardYear` reads its flags, never by the stage's name) and the value is a day written `YYYY-MM-DD` and nothing else, the same test that keeps `not_a_date` quiet. It gives none with no `submitted`, with any other value (`soon`, `2026-10-24T10:00:00+02:00`), or at another stage. A date after today gives the date with no days; today gives 0 days.
3. **The words, once.** One formatter in `src/lib/views/shared/card/card.ts` turns that fact into its label and its tip, from strings under `project.card` in `config/text/views/boards/project.yml`, so the board card and the lane card write it alike.
4. **Drawn on a board card.** `CardFacts.svelte` draws the fact as a tag before the year's, with the label, glyph and tip of item 3; it takes `now` as a prop, which `ProjectCard.svelte:390` passes from its own `now`. ProjectCard's head comment lists the new fact among the row's.
5. **Drawn on a lane card.** `laneFacts` takes the time it counts days from as a parameter, which `Lanes.svelte` passes from its `now`, and puts the fact first among a lane card's facts, as a kind of its own of `LaneFact` carrying what item 3 needs. `LaneCard.svelte` gives that kind its glyph in `FACT_GLYPHS` and its tip in `factTip` through item 3.
6. **The lane order.** `laneOrder` orders two projects of the same deadline, or with none, by `submitted` before the title: the older date first, a project with a date (as item 2 reads a date, at any stage) before one without, and the title between two of the same date. A lane of a cluster with `sort: title` is unchanged (`titleOrder`). The five sentences that state the lane order (`focus.ts:86` and `:421`, `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:176`, `config/options/catalogs/vocab.yml:163`, `vocab.ts:126`) say "by deadline, then by the day submitted, oldest first, then title" in their own wording.
7. **The flag's descriptions.** The three descriptions of `pauses_countdown` (`config/catalogs/vocab.yml:60`, `config/options/catalogs/vocab.yml:58`, `vocab.ts:178`) also say that a card at such a stage draws its `submitted` day with the days since.
8. **The template.** The project.yml block of `docs/templates.md` shows `# submitted: YYYY-MM-DD` beside `# deadline:` with a comment in the block's style, so G83's test holds.
9. **G32's test.** The fixture and the expected project of "fills a project from the manifest keys of the same meaning" (`tests/data/snapshot/normalise.test.ts:307`) carry `submitted`, so the entry's parser row reads the new key.
10. **The docs no step writes.** The report's section "Doc text" gives the row of `docs/data-map.md`'s manifest table (line 383) and any line of `docs/HELP.md` the key needs, each as `grep -n` prints the current line and its replacement.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The label is the word, the date and the days in the catalog's way of writing days on a card, following the ruled example "submitted 2026-10-24 | 32 d" in the catalog's `{0}d` form; the days part is left out when the date is after today. The tip has the form of every other card tip, "Kind: value", with the days in words in the catalog's plural forms: "Submitted: 2026-10-24, 32 days ago", and "Submitted: 2026-11-26" for a date after today. Five dates, today 2026-11-25: 2026-10-24 (four of the hub's manifests) gives `submitted 2026-10-24 | 32d`; 2026-11-19 (two) gives `submitted 2026-11-19 | 6d`; 2026-11-04 gives `submitted 2026-11-04 | 21d`; 2026-10-22 gives `submitted 2026-10-22 | 34d`; 2026-11-26 (flkit, from the ruling) gives `submitted 2026-11-26`.
2. The glyph is `stale`, the hourglass the Review lane itself carries; `waiting` is left to the WAITING ON line, so one card never draws one glyph with two meanings.
3. The fact comes before the year on both cards, so a stage that draws both draws them in one order.
4. Days are counted with `daysUntil`, so a card and the deadline badges count from the same local midnight. An impossible day such as `2026-02-30`, which `ISO_DATE` passes, is counted as `Date.parse` reads it, as a deadline is today.
5. The lane order reads `submitted` at any stage, since a card on the Review lane and a card that left it are ordered by one rule, and only a submitted card draws the fact.
