# K1, the digit keys

Read `../spec.md` first, its section "The principles every package is judged on" above all. Axel ruled on 2026-09-14 at 20:29 (plan.md Rulings): a bare digit switches the page, Ctrl plus a digit switches the tab within the pane that has focus, and `v`, `c` and `m` followed by a digit open a view. Today a bare digit opens the rail's direct view of that number (`src/lib/shell/keys.ts` line 127 reads `order.direct`), `c` and `m` are the two chords `config/layout.yml`'s `nav_groups` name (lines 13 and 14), and Ctrl plus a digit switches the page in `src/lib/shell/workbench-keys.svelte.ts` line 106 before the resolver runs. The key sheet (`config/text/app.yml` line 12) lists both, and the page strip's tab hint (`config/text/ui.yml` line 31, `ui.page_shortcut`, drawn by `src/lib/shell/PageStrip.svelte` line 90) names Ctrl. A fix of delivered work, alone, before R17.

## What to build

1. `src/lib/shell/keys.ts`: two new actions in `KeyAction`, `{ kind: 'page'; index: number }` and `{ kind: 'tab'; index: number }` (index 1 to 9 as pressed). `resolveKey` answers Ctrl plus a digit (ctrlKey, no metaKey) with `tab` before the blocking-modifier guard and regardless of `typing`, since a modifier chord is not typing, as the page shortcut behaved; a bare digit with no chord pending answers `page`, under the same `typing`, palette, tray and confirm guards as every other bare key; a bare digit after `v`, `c` or `m` within `CHORD_MS` answers `open` from that chord's list, as the `c` and `m` chords do today. `order.direct` stays what it is (the rail's numbering) and becomes the `v` chord's list; nothing reads it as a bare-digit target any more.
2. The `v` chord is named in `config/layout.yml` as a top-level key `direct_chord: v` beside `nav_groups`, with a comment in the file's own voice (the one-letter prefix that reaches the rail's directly drawn views by number), read in `src/routes/+page.svelte` beside `nav_groups` and handed to `railOrder` (`src/lib/shell/rail.ts` line 136), which puts `direct` under that letter in `chords`. A missing or malformed key means no `v` chord and nothing else changes.
3. `src/lib/shell/workbench-keys.svelte.ts`: the Ctrl-digit branch at line 106 goes; `perform` handles `page` through `pageShortcut` and `tab` through the focused pane: `src/lib/shell/PaneView.svelte` line 210 gains `data-pane-path` (the pane's `path` prop joined with a dot; an empty path is the empty string) beside `data-pane-target`, and `perform` reads the pane's tree with `layout(target)`, its node with `paneAt` from `src/lib/state/layout/tree.ts`, the tab with `item` from `data/guards.ts` at `index - 1`, and calls `activateInstance(target, tab.id)`; a digit past the pane's last tab, or no focused pane, does nothing and the key is not swallowed. The same pane-finding rule as the cursor keys (`focusedPaneElement`).
4. The words: `config/text/app.yml` line 12's rows become, in this order, `1-9 | switch page`, `Ctrl+1-9 | switch tab in the focused pane`, `v1-v9 | open a view`, `c1-c9 | Collections menu`, `m1-m9 | More menu`, then the rest unchanged with the `Ctrl+1-9 | switch page` row removed; `ui.page_shortcut` names the bare digit (`'{0} - {1}'` or a form of your choosing that reads as a key); the `SHORTCUTS` comment in `PageStrip.svelte` line 39 says the bare digits; DESIGN.md line 268 names the new bindings where it names Ctrl. `grep -rn "Ctrl+" README.md DESIGN.md config src` prints only the search tip and the page-move shortcut after this.
5. Tests: `tests/shell/keys.test.ts`: a bare digit always answers `page` with its number (the store decides whether a page exists), `v` then a digit answers `open` of the direct view and `v4` past the list answers null, `c` and `m` unchanged, a bare digit while `typing` answers null, Ctrl plus a digit answers `tab` while typing and while not, Cmd plus a digit answers null (the browser's own). `tests/browser/pages.spec.ts` lines 42 and 46 press `1` and `2` with focus on the strip's tab (not in a text box). A new case in `tests/browser/keyboard.spec.ts`: with two tabs in the focused main pane, Ctrl+2 activates the second (`data-pane-active`), Ctrl+9 leaves it, and `v` then `1` opens the rail's first direct view. `tests/shell/paneview.test.ts` or `tests/shell/workbench.test.ts`: a rendered pane carries `data-pane-path` with its path.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. `direct_chord` in `config/layout.yml` rather than a constant, since the other two chords live there.
2. The pane's path on its element, since the key handler finds the focused pane by the DOM and the tree by the path.
3. Ctrl plus a digit works while typing, as the page shortcut did.
