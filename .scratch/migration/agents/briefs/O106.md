## What to build

1. **A card leaves out the Website section.** A field whose schema row has `section: Website` is not drawn on a card in a collection or on the entry page's fields. The name of the section is one constant beside `drawnFields`, which only selects rows. A row of the same name in another section (`role` is Core in `events.yml`) draws as today. A file with no schema draws as today. A field the entry holds that its schema does not name draws as today.
2. **The page has a Website group.** A card handed `page`, after the entry's fields, draws a group headed by the rows' own `section` word, holding each filled Website field: its `label`, then its value, a textarea as the body kind draws it. A checkbox the entry holds is drawn with its label and a catalog word for yes or no, both states, since `min: false` is the state that acts. An entry with none has no group. `EntryCard` hands `page` to `EntryFields`. People schemas have no Website row, so the Person page draws no group.
3. **A person's rows are drawn by name.** A `type: records` field, not a `map`, whose rows have a field with a `foreign_key` naming the people collection is a new field kind. The field names no field by word, so `students` is not spelled in the code.
   - On a card: its label, then one person control per row.
   - On a page: its label over one line per row, the person control, then the row's other text fields in schema order, joined by `JOIN`.
   - One map as the value is one row. A row with no person, or a value that is neither a list nor a map, is left out. An unknown key draws as the key does on the existing person control. With two person sub-fields, the first in schema order is the person.
   - A `foreign_key` of two targets is not the new kind, as the existing check reads it.
4. **Plain labels.** The hub patch adds `label:` to the 27 Website rows and to `students`, with these words: `cv_section` "CV section", `cv_rank` "CV place", `cv_title` "CV title", `cv_url` "CV address", `cv_status` "CV status", `role` "CV role", `location` "Location", `duration` "CV period", `about` "CV text", `min` "On the short CV", `hide` "Hidden on the CV", `students` "Students". They are the manifest's words where it has one. The help of `min` in `funding.yml` and `docs/templates.md`, and the label and help of `cv.min` in `config/schemas/manifest-schema.yml:118`, are corrected to what the templates do: ticked keeps the row on the short CV. `docs/templates.md` prints the same rows with the same labels. No new schema key: the step reads `section`, `label`, `foreign_key`, `fields` and `map`, and adds none.
5. **Docs.** `docs/HELP.md`, the head comments of `EntryFields.svelte`, `EntryCard.svelte` and `collection/index.ts`. `docs/data-map.md:267` says `section` also decides what a card draws, and `docs/templates.md:1088` says the label also names the field in a column and on a page. `docs/HELP.md` says search still reads every field, and that a Website word finds an entry whose card does not show it.
