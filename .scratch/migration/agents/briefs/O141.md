## What to build

1. The layouts. In the three-column layout the comment describes (a pane wide enough for three columns in both grids), the headers stand in rows: the band row (those of Funding, Jobs and Teaching that are drawn, in that order), then the `now`, `parked` and `incubate` rows in the order of `focus_clusters`, then the full-width Idea lane. A cluster row splits the width into three equal columns; a drawn band of k columns splits it into k equal columns (k is 1, 2 or 3, `auto-fit` collapsing the empty tracks). Two headers touch when they stand side by side in one row, or when they stand in two consecutive drawn rows and their horizontal spans overlap (a shared edge alone is no overlap, since the grids keep an 8 px gap). The Idea lane spans the width, so it touches every header of the last drawn row above it, the band row included when every cluster is folded. The open layout has every cluster open and all three band columns drawn. The other layouts are every combination of folded clusters and drawn band columns (each band column drawn or not, a band of none not drawn): a folded cluster's row is not drawn. Read `Lanes.svelte`, `Band.svelte` and `Focus.svelte` to confirm this model, and hand it back with the code that contradicts it before measuring if it does not hold.
2. The case-run program measures, for every theme of the 49, every pair of headers that touches in the open layout and every pair that touches only in another layout, on the `-lane` surfaces, and prints each pair under 10 with its theme and distance, and the counts: open pairs under 10, and collapsed-only pairs under 10 in at least one theme. It runs with `npx tsx` from the worktree root and writes no file of the worktree (spec.md's case-run program rule). The report quotes its source and every run.
3. Where an open-layout pair is under 10 in any theme, give one of its two headers a new colour in `config/catalogs/vocab.yml`: a lane's `color` in `focus_lanes` is a hex that `palette:` maps to a `c-` tone (never one it maps to `muted`, which draws grey, `tones.ts:199,217`); a column's value in `focus_lane_tones` is a tone name whose `c-<name>` the palette holds (a hex there draws grey, `tones.ts:34,207-212`). After the change:
   - no open-layout pair is under 10 in any of the 49 themes;
   - every other pair, in each theme: one at 10 or more on the base is at 10 or more after, and one under 10 on the base is at or above its base distance after;
   - the count of collapsed-only pairs under 10 does not grow.
   With no open-layout pair under 10 on the base, no colour changes. If no palette tone meets the three bars for a pair, hand the pair back with the run before writing anything.
4. Rewrite the last two sentences of the `vocab.yml:181` comment and the doc of `LANE_SHARE` (`derive.ts:83-89`) to what the final run measured: that the open-layout pairs are 10 or more apart in every theme (only when the run shows it), and the count of collapsed-only pairs under 10 in at least one theme with each such pair named and the themes it falls in, in plain prose. Rewrite the second sentence of the `vocab.yml:213` comment the same way: the band's three tones stand above the lanes, and the sentence says what the run measured of them against each other and the headers they touch. The rest of the three comments stays as it is; the `vocab.yml` comments stay one line each, the `derive.ts` doc is filled at 100 columns.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The layout model of item 1 (three columns; the band row above the cluster rows, split by its drawn columns; a folded cluster or an undrawn band column taken out) is read from `Band.svelte:141,160,199` and `Lanes.svelte:267-313`; the builder confirms it against the code.
2. Distances are measured on the `-lane` surfaces, the colour a header paints (`LaneHead.svelte:67`).
3. A colour change takes a tone `palette:` already maps, so no theme needs a new token.
4. "Every other pair kept at or above where it was" is read per pair against the 10 line: a pair already under 10 may not get closer, and a pair at 10 or more may move but stays at 10 or more. A recolour that moves a header away from one neighbour moves it toward another, so a bar that let no distance fall at all would forbid every recolour; the 10 line is the rule both comments state.
