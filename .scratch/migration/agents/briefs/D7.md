## The shapes, all ruled (Axel, 2026-09-16)

1. `registries/people.yml`: one entry per human, `key`, `name`, `affiliation` (an organisation key), `role`, and optional `orcid`, `email`, `links`, `folder` (a student's supervision folder under `education/student-supervision/`), `self: true` on exactly one entry, Axel's own. The five students are entries. Agreed 00:30 and 00:40.
2. `registries/organisations.yml`: `key`, `name`, `kind` (`university | hospital | funder | company | other`), `country`, optional `links`. A person's `affiliation:` and a funding scheme's `funder:` are keys into it; a free string in either is a finding. Agreed 00:40.
3. `network/collaborations.yml` becomes `network/joint-work.yml`, the collection key `joint-work`, the view "Joint work"; an entry is a piece of joint work with a person: it keeps `key`, `status`, `tags`, `note`, `deadline`, `followup`, gains `person:` (a `people.yml` key, required) and a `title` that names the work rather than the person; the person's facts leave the entry. Agreed 00:40 (the word `joint-work` at 00:40 after `partnerships` and `network` were weighed).
4. A manifest's `collaborators:` names persons: its values are `people.yml` keys, a finding otherwise; the key keeps its name because it names a role, and the same rule (role as key, person key as value) is what a later `lead:` or `students:` field follows. No manifest is edited, since every collaboration key is also its person's key. `waiting-on:` accepts a person key or a project id from now on. Agreed 00:25 to 00:35.
5. A Person view (`person`, unlisted, one instance per key, opened from a collaborator tag on a card, from a joint-work entry and from the Map's node): the entry, the organisation, the joint work whose `person:` is the key, the projects whose `collaborators:` name the key, and for a student the folder's files through the file route. Agreed 00:25.

## How it lives in oculus (the three layers, the data live)

- Data: `data/sources/registries.ts` reads the two new registries into `Person` and `Organisation` entities (`data/model-entities.ts`), with relations `projectPerson` (from `collaborators:`), `jointWorkPerson` (from `person:`), `personOrganisation`, `fundingOrganisation`; `data/validate.ts` checks the four keys and reports a finding per bad one, with the sentences in `config/text/server.yml`; the collection reader keeps reading `joint-work.yml` through its own schema. `config/sources.yml` gains `people_file` and `organisations_file` beside `venues_file` and the `collections:` entry is renamed. The snapshot carries the two entity tables; the watcher covers the two files like every registry.
- State: `state/person.ts` (pure) joins one key to its organisation, joint work, projects and folder; `waitingLine` and the Map's collaborator nodes read `people` for the name instead of the collection's `title`.
- View: `views/Person.svelte` with `config/text/views/person.yml`; the collaborator tags on `shared/CardLinks.svelte` open it; the Joint work view is the generic collection view over the renamed file with its schema (a `person` field of type `select` whose options are the people keys, filled the way the bookmarks form fills its `root`).
- Migration, one script under `tools/oculus/bin/` run once by the worker and kept: for each of the 13 entries, a `people.yml` entry from `title`, `sub` and the two meta rows; an `organisations.yml` entry per distinct institution; `person:` set to the entry's own key; `title` rewritten to the work (from `sub`); the person's rows removed. Plus Axel's own entry and the five students, their names read from the folder names and marked for him to complete (`name` as the folder's word, `role: student`). The result is checked by `python3 tools/cockpit/build.py --selftest` (the cockpit reads the same files, so its `collaborations.yml` reference in `selftest.py` line 177 and its `layout.yml` entry follow the rename) and by oculus's findings at zero over the real hub.
