## What to build

1. **One shared function opens an entry's page.** `entryPage`, `venuePage`, `pageLookup` and the types `EntryPage` and `PageLookup` move from `views/boards/focus/rows.ts` to `src/lib/state/forms/page-entry.ts`, beside `pageView`. `Band.svelte`, `Focus.svelte` and `CommandCenter.svelte` import them from there; `rows.ts` keeps no re-export. `Band.svelte:9`'s head comment names the new place. The card and the lane card call these functions; no second copy of the lookup is written.
2. **Funding, Pillar, Datasets and domain tags open their entry's page.** A press on each opens the page of the entry its id names, the hub being `addressOfId(tag.id).hub` and the collection key `registryKey(<word>, that hub)` for the words `funding`, `pillars`, `datasets` and `domains`, through `entryPage`, opened with `openView(page.view, page.state)` in the current pane, as Team does. A Funding tag opens its scheme's page, not the funding collection. The domain tag of `ProjectCard.svelte:344-346` becomes a pressed tag the same way.
3. **The Venue tag opens the venue's page,** through `venuePage`. The tier glyph stays outside the button, with its colour, its tip and its own tab stop, beside a pressed tag of the venue's words that keeps `wrap`; one control per tab stop, no focusable element inside a button.
4. **A compute fact opens its resource's page** in its hub's `resources` collection. Each compute fact of `CardFacts.svelte` becomes a `ToolButton plain` whose `title` is `t('project.card.compute_tip', resource.label)`, with no outer `Tooltip`, so it shows one tip, in a box styled as `.folder` without the monospace face (the style block of `CardFacts.svelte` changes for it). The head comment `CardFacts.svelte:16-19` is rewritten to say which facts are presses.
5. **The Disseminated-in tags open their entry's page.** Each tag of `CardDetails.svelte` opens `pageView(entry.collection, entry.id, entry.title)` with `openView`, in place of `goto`.
6. **The Focus lane card's venue opens the venue's page.** The venue of `LaneCard.svelte:153-161,194-199` becomes a press through `venuePage`, keeping its short name and its tip.
7. **A tag whose entry is not found has no press.** When the lookup returns no page (a hub with no collection for the registry), the tag is drawn as today with no press, as a Related tag of no held project is; never a press that opens a collection or does nothing. Since merge drops an unresolved key, this is checked by reading the code path that returns null (C6).
8. **The code that loses its use goes.** `linkView` and `goto` in `card.ts` are removed with their doc comments, once nothing calls them; `utils/unused-exports.sh` adds no line.
9. **The words.** The head comments of `CardLinks.svelte` (`:2-33`), `CardFacts.svelte` (`:2-26`), `CardDetails.svelte`, `LaneCard.svelte` and `ProjectCard.svelte` that describe these tags say what a press on each does now, with no history. `docs/HELP.md` is not changed (`agents/spec.md:72`); every sentence of it that the change makes false (at least `docs/HELP.md:299` on the tags and the tier icon) is named in the report with its line and what is now true, for the orchestrator to bring to Axel.
10. **Out of scope.** The Map (`views/structure/map/node-view.ts:12`) draws nodes, not cards, and is not changed.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The page opened is the entry's page (`pageView`), as a card's title in a collection and a Focus line open it, since that is the page of one entry; the collection view is the list of all of them.
2. A tag with no entry draws as today with no press, as a Related tag of no held project does.
3. The press opens the page in the current pane, as the Team and Related tags do.
4. The shared functions live in `state/forms/page-entry.ts`, which already holds `pageView` and which every view may import.
5. The Venue tag keeps its tier glyph as its own control outside the press, so its tip and tab stop stay as they are.
