## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The full words on hover: a pressed tag's through its button's tip (a Tooltip, which also shows on keyboard focus), the whole words on the first line and the press's hint on the second; a tag with no press through its native `title`, as the cards' other long rows do, with no tab stop added. Taken here because the step's line asks for the full text "as the card's other long rows do", and a pressed tag already has a Tooltip tip that the native title would double.
2. A long tag is cut with an ellipsis, not wrapped, except where it is drawn with `wrap` today; since `.taglist` wraps, such a tag sits on a line of its own under its kind's label, which keeps a kind's tags readable as one run.
3. The fix sits on the box that does not give way, as the measurement shows, so all plain presses share it; a width set per card would leave the other callers with the same defect.
4. ToolButton's split goes into its own folder `src/lib/ui/controls/tool-button/`, with `tool-button.ts`, since `controls/` is at ten items and the button with its props type and its split part are one concern; the 230 import paths change with it.
