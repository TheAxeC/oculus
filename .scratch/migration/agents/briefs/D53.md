## What to build

1. **The mark.** `Registration` gains an optional `unique?: boolean`, with a comment that says what it means: no opener and no build makes a second instance of the view, and opening it goes to an instance already held on a page or a float. The ten registrations above say `unique: true`. No other view says it: Files, Search, Terminal, the session views, every view opened for something and every app stay without it.

2. **The rule reaches the layout.** The layout store is handed the rule as it is handed the opened rule: a `registerUnique((view) => registry.viewFor(view)?.unique === true)` call in `state/client/context.ts` beside `registerOpened` (762), and the store passes the rule to `createOpening`, `createAway` where it needs it, and the builds of item 4.

3. **Opening a unique view goes to where it is held.** `openView`, `openBeside`, `openNextTo` and `openAway` of a unique view look for an instance of it, whatever its state's identity (`findView` with the identity omitted), in this order: the pages drawn on the page on screen (`drawnBy` of the page on screen, also when the host asked for or touched is a float, or a page asked for by id), then the floats, then every other page in the page list's order. The first instance found is activated where it is: on a page drawn on screen, without changing the page on screen; in a float, as `activateFloated` does today; on any other page, by switching to that page (as `switchPage` does, the attention moved with it) and activating it there. A pane hidden in a folded child is expanded, and a maximised pane that is not the one holding it is restored first, as `openOpened` does, so the tab shows. A state the opener passes (`{ reveal: 'rail' }`, the language settings query) is given to the instance found, as `restate` does today. A new instance opens only when no page and no float holds one, where it opens today (with the keyboard focus in a float, as a tab of that float). `openNextTo` and `openAway` never move an instance found on another page or in a float into the catalogue's page: they answer its id and the instance stays where it is.

4. **No build makes a copy.** A page or float made from the config's arrangements or shipped floats holds every view they name, so `agents/guarded.md` G54 stays true: an instance of a unique view another page or float holds is moved into the place the arrangement gives it, with its id and state, and taken out of where it was (a pane left with no tab follows the empty-pane rule, a float left with none is removed). This holds for `openRailPage` making a page, `resetPage`, `resetPages` (a page it rebuilds and the floats it makes take from the pages it keeps; among what it builds, pages first then floats, the first place keeps the view and a later one leaves it out), `startOnRailPage` (the page first, then the floats), and an arrangement or float that names one unique view twice (the first place keeps it). `adoptFloats` already moves the first page-held instance into the float and removes the others (`pages.ts:142-148`), and stays as it is. A page made from the default instead (New page, `openPage`; the page left by closing the last page or by `resetPages` with no page; `keepDefaultPage`'s default page against the shipped floats it is given) leaves out a unique view a page or float holds, so its one pane is an empty pane. The tree is changed by pure functions over a layout, in a file of `layout/` that has room under 880 lines (`tree.ts` or `instance.ts`), and the held set is read from the pages and floats beside them; `layout.svelte.ts` calls them and stays at or under 880 lines. The tabs a reset forgets (`instances.forgetTabs`) do not include an instance moved into the rebuilt page.

5. **Copy into New Window.** `copiesToWindow` answers false for an instance of a unique view, so the tab menu, which offers the two window items in the app only, does not offer Copy into New Window on it. Move into New Window, Float, the splits and every drag stay moves.

6. **A catalogue drop.** A unique view dropped from the catalogue while a page or float holds it moves that one instance to where the drop puts a tab (the pane dropped on, a new pane at its edge, or for a drop on the catalogue's own pane the other pane `openAway` picks), and the page dropped on stays on screen: the drop opens nothing new, as a drop of a view a float holds does today. When no page or float holds it, the drop opens it as today.

7. **The catalogue click and the Settings rail entry.** A click or a shift-click on a unique view a page or float holds goes straight to it through item 3, without first showing the previous page or making the first rail page (`showTarget` is skipped for it), so no page is switched through on the way. A view no page or float holds is opened as today. `openSettings` (`ActivityRail.svelte`) goes to the Settings a page or float holds through `openView`; only when none holds one does it open the rail page whose arrangement names Settings first, as today.

8. **A saved layout is read as it is.** A layout saved with two instances of a unique view keeps both on load: nothing is closed or dropped, apart from what `adoptFloats` does today for an envelope saved before floats. Opening the view goes to the first one item 3's order finds; the reader closes the other.

9. **The text.** Every sentence of `docs/window.md:21` is made true for an ordinary view and for a unique one: "A view already open is activated where it is rather than opened twice", "Open on this page, which opens it in another pane of that page", "Dragging an entry onto a pane of the page the catalogue is on opens it there" and "A drop on the catalogue's own pane goes to another pane" among them (items 3, 6 and 7). `docs/window.md:54` says that a unique view's tab, like a Terminal tab, offers no copy. A new paragraph after the first paragraph of the Pages section (after line 33) names the ten unique views by their names as the catalogue shows them, says that no opener and no build makes a second one, that opening one (a click, a key, the status bar, a notice, a link) goes to the page or float that holds it, that a page made from its arrangement (its first opening, Reset, Reset all pages) takes a unique view it names from the page or float that holds it, that New page leaves one out, that Copy into New Window is not offered on one, and that a layout saved with two copies keeps them until one is closed. The head comment of `PagesReset.svelte` and `reset_all_tip` (`config/text/views/system/workbench.yml:106`) say that a page of the reader's own keeps its tabs but a unique view a rail page's arrangement names, which moves to that page. `layout/model.ts:142` says the default page starts on Status when no page or float holds it. `DESIGN.md` gains a bullet after line 480 saying that a registration's `unique` flag, read through the rule the client hands the layout store as `registerOpened` hands the opened rule, makes the opening look over every page and float and the builds move or leave out a held instance, naming `opening.ts` and the functions of item 4. Each comment the list under "What is on the tree" names is made true.

10. **Sizes.** A source file at or under 880 lines, a Svelte component at or under 520, at most 10 items in a folder; `layout/` and `layout/open/` take no new file.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The field is `unique?: boolean` on `Registration`, beside `listed`, absent meaning not unique. The plan line says "marked unique in their registration"; a list of ids in the layout store would put view knowledge into the layout, which `DESIGN.md:480` rules out (the store reads the registry only through a rule the client hands it).
2. The order the holder is looked for in (item 3): what the reader sees first (the page on screen and the pages it draws), then the floats, which are drawn over every page, then the other pages in list order. It extends today's order (the page, then the floats) by the pages not on screen.
3.
