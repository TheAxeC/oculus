## What to build

1. **The strip over the board.** `waiting()` counts the people and the venues of every project the board shows: the live projects as today, and a project of any other tier that sits on a lane (the same rule `laneItems` uses). A coauthor project on a lane waiting on a person adds to that person's count; an archive project with no lane `focus:` adds nothing.
2. **Distinct neighbours.** Choose each lane's colour in `focus_lanes` and each column's tone in `focus_lane_tones` so that, under every theme of `config/appearance.yml`, the header strip colours (`--c-<hue>-lane`) of neighbouring lanes differ by at least a stated colour distance (CIEDE2000 of 10 or more, or the measure `themes/derive.ts` already uses if it has one; say which). Neighbours are the lanes drawn next to each other in a cluster and across a cluster edge, and the three columns next to each other and next to the lane beside them, as `views/focus/Lanes.svelte` and `Band.svelte` lay them out; read the order from the markup. Keep each lane's colour close to its meaning where a choice allows it (Blocked red, Run green). A hex a lane needs that `palette:` does not list is added to `palette:` mapped to its hue.
3. **A test that holds it.** A node test deriving every theme's tokens (the way `tests/contrast.test.ts` does) and asserting the distance for every neighbouring pair, naming the theme and the pair on failure; the `-lane-txt` on `-lane` contrast stays measured by `tests/contrast.test.ts`.
