## What to build

1. **One visual language.** The strip draws every fact the same way: its label in the strip's muted colour (`--muted-txt, var(--muted)`), set smaller than the value (`0.8em` of the strip's type), in capitals with a little letter spacing, then its value in the slide's text colour at the strip's type (`--slide-fs-small`). No fact is drawn in a box: no `Chip`, no `Tag`, no background, no border, no pill, no filled stage. The colon of `'{0}: {1}'` is not drawn; a reader of the page (the accessible name) still reads label and value as one phrase, by a visually hidden separator whose words come from a catalog key through `t()` (never a literal in the component) or by the element's own text, the builder's choice named in the report. Every value is drawn as a Svelte text node, never through `{@html}`, so a value holding `|`, `<` or `&` is drawn as written.
2. **The hues as dots.** The form, the stage, the status chips and the domain carry their hue as a dot before their value (`parts/Dot.svelte`, sized to the strip's type), in the hue the strip gives them today (`statusMarkTone` for form and chips, the chips' dot red when any chip blocks through `isBlocked`, as today, `stageTone` for the stage, `domainTone` for the domain); a word with no colour gets the `grey` tone these functions give, as today. The other facts carry no dot and no glyph. The dot's size follows the strip's type through a custom property on `Dot.svelte` whose default stays 9 px, so the summary rows and the idea cards that draw it (`Summary.svelte`, `Grid.svelte`) are unchanged; `Dot.svelte`'s head comment names the strip as a third use.
3. **Facts set apart.** Facts on a line are separated by a fixed gap of about 1.25em of the strip's type, larger than the gap between a label and its value (about 0.4em), so each label reads with its own value. A fact is never broken across two lines: label, dot and value stay on one line together (`white-space: nowrap` inside a fact), and a value longer than its room ends in an ellipsis.
4. **A rule above the strip.** The strip is set off from the slide's content by a 1 px rule in `--line` across the strip's full width, with room above it (the `.strip`'s padding in `Slide.svelte`) and about 0.6em between the rule and the first line of facts. The rule is drawn inside `Facts.svelte`'s box, so the toggle hides it with the words and keeps its room.
5. **Short values.** A list value (pillars, datasets, lead, supervisors, collaborators, funding, compute, status chips) with more than one entry draws its first entry and then `+<n>` for the others ("Maryam Amir Haeri +2"), through a new words key in `config/text/views/academic/present.yml` beside `fact` (such as `fact_more: '{0} +{1}'`); a list of one entry draws that entry alone. A value is drawn no wider than about 16em of the strip's type, cut with an ellipsis beyond it; when the first entry of a list is cut, the `+<n>` stays drawn after the cut entry, so the count is never lost to the ellipsis. Each fact's tooltip gives the whole fact, label and every entry, in `present.fact`'s words ("Collaborators: Maryam Amir Haeri, Maureen Guichelaar, St<e acute>phanie M. van den Berg", the accented letter as the manifest writes it), as today.
6. **Two lines at most, and every fact seen.** The strip stays at most two lines (ruling 680). The aim is that every fact that holds a value fits those two lines on every project deck of the research hub in its frame (1280 by 720 by default) at text size 1. To get there the strip takes the slide's whole inner width (the frame less its padding, 1150 px in a 1280 px frame) rather than the reading column's 1040, the label is set at about 0.72em, the gap between facts about 1em, and a value at most about 14em; the builder sets these by reading the widths the hub's facts need and names the numbers in the report. The look measures the fit (C2-C4) and the screenshots go to Axel with the count of facts still hidden on each deck. A fact that still does not fit is not drawn on a third line, and a fact is never cut by the clamp half-drawn: the strip keeps whole facts or none of a fact, as today; a project with all 16 fields shows the whole facts that fit and hides the rest.
7. **Kept as it is.** The order of the fields (`FACT_FIELDS`), the words of each value (the server's, `data/shapes/project-facts.ts`, not changed), the stage drawn as it is, `failed` and `stopped` included, the toggle (words hidden with `visibility: hidden`, the room kept, so the slide's layout and its Too tall finding do not change with it), the strip's size at `--slide-fs-small` outside the slide's text factor, and the same strip on the pane, the presenter screen, the Overview's tiles, the audience window and the PDF, since all draw `Slide.svelte`. The strip's two lines with its rule take about the height today's two lines of chips take (two chip lines, about 59 px at 18 px type), and the look measures the room above the strip before and after.
8. **Themes.** The strip reads in every theme oculus ships (`config/defaults/appearance.yml`: the base themes, the VS Code themes, two of them high contrast, the terminal themes and the rice theme); its colours come from the tokens above, never a literal colour. Dropping `Tag` drops the rice theme's brackets around a fact (`Tag.svelte`, the `rice.brackets` setting), which is named in the report as a visible change.
9. **Docs and comments.** `docs/decks.md`'s section "The project facts strip" (lines 383-389) says how the strip is drawn after the step: each field as a muted label and its value, the hued fields with a dot, a list as its first entry and a count, a long value cut with its whole words in the tooltip, two lines at most. `docs/HELP.md:342` ("as a labelled chip in the words and hues of the project card") and `:343` ("a chip too long for its room is cut with an ellipsis") are rewritten to the strip after the step. `docs/decks.md:393` and `docs/HELP.md:347` (`{{project.<field>}}` in the strip's words, a list joined with ", ") are read and made exact: the placeholder writes the whole list, the strip draws the first entry and a count. The head comment and the CSS comments of `Facts.svelte`, the comment of `Slide.svelte` on the foot (`:30-39`) and on `.foot` (`:497-498`), and `Dot.svelte`'s head comment say what is true after the step.
10. **No new test.** Views have no guarded entry (`guarded.md`'s head), and a strip's look loses no work, breaks no installation and accepts no wrong configuration, so the step adds no test. The cases are checked by reading and at the orchestrator's look on landing; the existing tests that name a changed file pass, `tests/checks/source/keys.test.ts` (every catalog key has a reader) and `tests/checks/config/config-folder.test.ts` among them.
11. **Slide.svelte split by concern.** `Slide.svelte` (539 lines) is split so that it, and every file the split makes, is at or under 520 lines for a component and 880 for a source file, by concern: a part with one job (such as the measuring and laying out of the slide, or the foot with the strip and the key hint) moves into its own file in `src/lib/views/academic/present/screen/` (7 entries, room for one or two more), named for what it holds. Comments are not shortened and code is not compacted to fit (`spec.md:48`). The split changes no behaviour: every surface draws the same slide, and the report names each moved part with its old and new lines.
