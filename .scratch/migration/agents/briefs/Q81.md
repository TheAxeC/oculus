## What to build

1. **Dependencies.** `bits-ui` 2.19.3 and `fuzzysort` 4.0.2 as `dependencies`, with bits-ui's peer `@internationalized/date` as npm installs it; `package-lock.json` updated by `npm install`.
2. **Menus on bits-ui's Dropdown Menu.** `ui/menu/Menu.svelte` keeps its props and every caller unchanged, and is built on `DropdownMenu` (Root, Trigger through `MenuOpener.svelte`'s looks, Portal, Content, Item, Group with GroupHeading, Sub, SubTrigger, SubContent). The list is drawn on the body through bits-ui's Portal, placed by its floating positioning with collision handling: `placement` `up`, `down`, `beside` and `above`, and `align` `left` and `right`, map onto `side` and `align` so each opener's list opens where it opens today. A `MenuItem` with `items` is a cascading submenu opened to the side (SubTrigger and SubContent), as VS Code's are; the Back choice, `menuLevel`, `ui.menu_back` and the in-place list go. Keys: ArrowUp and ArrowDown, Home and End, type-ahead on each item's label (`textValue`), ArrowRight or Enter on a submenu's item opens it, ArrowLeft or Escape in a submenu closes it and gives the keyboard back to its item, Escape in the menu closes it and gives the focus back to the opener through `focusQuietly`, so no tooltip opens. A pick closes the whole menu and calls `onpick(id, event, group)` as today. An item keeps its `active` check, `glyph`, `tone` dot, `badge`, `badgeGlyph`, `badgeTone`, `badgeTitle` and `title`, and its accessible name stays its label. `MenuList.svelte` becomes the item and group drawing that Menu and ContextMenu share, or goes if they draw the items directly; the positioning, the outside-press listener and the key handler go.
3. **The context menu on bits-ui's Context Menu.** `ui/menu/ContextMenu.svelte` keeps its props and callers, and is built on `ContextMenu` (Root, Trigger wrapping what it wraps without a box of its own, Portal, Content, the items as in 2, submenus cascading). A right-click opens it at the pointer; the context menu key and Shift+F10 on the focused control open it at that control; the focus goes back to that control through `focusQuietly` when it closes; no items opens nothing.
4. **Tooltips on bits-ui's Tooltip.** `ui/menu/Tooltip.svelte` keeps its props (`text`, `placement`, `presentational`, `focusable`, `block`, `describes`, `over`, `children`) and callers, and is built on `Tooltip` (Provider, Root, Trigger, Portal, Content, Arrow for a bubble beside its control). It keeps every behaviour it has: the bubble after 150 ms of rest; on keyboard focus only when `:focus-visible` holds, never for the focus a pointer press gives; a press puts it away until the pointer has left and rested again; none during a drag; none on the focus `focusQuietly` gives back; the empty string draws a plain wrapper; the described control carries `aria-describedby` to the bubble (`tip-ids.ts`, `tooltip-target.ts` as far as bits-ui does not already give it); placements `below`, `beside`, `above`. The hand positioning and the body move go.
5. **Dialogs on bits-ui's Dialog and Alert Dialog.** `Confirm.svelte` on `AlertDialog`, `Prompt.svelte` on `Dialog`, each keeping its props and callers: focus on the confirm (Confirm) or the first field (Prompt), Enter answers yes in the Prompt, Escape cancels, Tab stays inside through bits-ui's focus trap, a press on the ground behind cancels, and the focus goes back to the control the dialog was raised from when that control is still on the page. A Confirm or Prompt drawn inside a tab is rendered in place, not portalled, so it stays inside its tab's DOM and the shell's `dialogShown` still tells a hidden one from a shown one; while its tab is hidden its focus trap is off, and it takes the keyboard back when the tab shows (`onShown`), as today. The hand-written Tab loops go. `CloseQuestion.svelte` stays a Confirm.
6. **The four modal overlays made modal.** `KeySheet.svelte`, `UpdateTray.svelte`, `Palette.svelte` and the Memories picture overlay on `Dialog`: the focus moves into the overlay when it opens, Tab stays inside it, and the focus goes back where it was when it closes. The shell keeps its keys: Escape and `?` for the sheet, Escape for the palette and the tray, Cmd-K and `:`; the three overlays the shell closes pass bits-ui's Escape through (its `escapeKeydownBehavior` set so the shell's handler is the one that closes them), and a Confirm inside the tray still takes the first Escape. The Memories overlay keeps its own Escape and its press outside.
7. **The body layer.** `Bell.svelte`'s toasts, `NoticeCentre.svelte` and `RepoItems.svelte`'s confirm are drawn on the body through bits-ui's `Portal`; `ui/frame/on-body.ts` is deleted and DESIGN.md:216 names the Portal in its place.
8. **The palette on bits-ui's Command list with fuzzysort.** `Palette.svelte`'s list is `Command` (Root with `shouldFilter={false}`, Input, List, Item), so the selection, ArrowUp and ArrowDown, Home and End, Enter and `aria-activedescendant` are the Command's; the hand selection and key handling go. `palette.ts` `match` ranks with fuzzysort over each item's title, label and text: every query word must match (fuzzysort's space-separated terms), the title's match ranks first, ties keep index order, and with no word the list is the views alone, as today. Each result draws its title with the matched characters highlighted from fuzzysort's result (`highlight()`), as VS Code's Quick Open does. The colon commands (`:<number>`, `:`, `:language`) are unchanged in what they do.
9. **The two other matchers.** `languageChoices(query)` and `matchingFiles(all, needle)` match with fuzzysort: with a query, the matches by score, best first, ties in the order they have today; with none, the list as today (Plain Text first, then by name; every file row). The palette's `:language` list highlights the matched characters of each language's name as the results do.
10. **Documents and words.** DESIGN.md:306 says a choice with a list of its own opens it to the side as VS Code's submenus do, names the menu keys, and drops `menuLevel` and the Back choice; DESIGN.md:216 as in 7; HELP.md:42 says the two page choices open their lists to the side, and ArrowLeft or Escape goes back; HELP.md:86 says a word matches fuzzily, with the matched letters marked. `ui.menu_back` is removed from `config/text/ui.yml`. `npm run diagrams` rerun.
11. **Tests.** The tests of the removed code go with it (`menuLevel` in `paneview.test.ts`, the Tab loops, the hand positioning); the tests listed under "What is on the tree" are brought to the new code. Every case below that a server render or a pure function can show is a test that names the revert that turns it red; a case only a browser can show (a key, a focus move, a trap, a delay) is stated in the report as seen at the landing's look, not as tested, since this project's tests render on the server and this step adds no DOM test environment.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Each primitive keeps its props, so none of its callers change (23 files import `Menu`, 15 `Confirm`, 10 `Tooltip`: `grep -rlE "import Menu from" src | wc -l`, and the same for the other two); a prop bits-ui makes meaningless inside the menu folder (`MenuList`'s `at`, `rise`, `element`) goes with its only callers there.
2. A dialog drawn inside a tab is rendered in place, not portalled, so the hidden-tab rule of the shell keeps working; only the body layer of 7, the menus and the tooltips go through the Portal.
3. The shell keeps its Escape for the palette, the tray and the sheet, since its tests hold the order of those keys and the tray's nested confirm (`tests/shell/workbench.test.ts`); bits-ui's own Escape on those three is passed through to it.
4. The match highlight is drawn in the list highlight colour the theme already gives search matches if one exists (`grep` the tokens for a highlight or match pair), else in `--accent` at weight 600, as VS Code draws `list.highlightForeground`; the builder names the token used in the report.
5. `languageChoices` and `matchingFiles` rank by fuzzysort's score with a query; without one their order is unchanged.
