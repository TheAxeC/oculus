## What to build

1. **The badge on every pane tab.** A `Tab` gains `badge?: string`, `badgeTone?: string`, `badgeTitle?: string`; `Tabs.svelte` draws a badge after the label as the rail does (the same chip pair by tone, `--fs-xs`, a pill); `PaneView.svelte` fills them from the registration through one shared function `badgeOf(registration)` in `state/rail-badges.svelte.ts` (`{ text, tone, title }`) that `ActivityRail.svelte` uses too, so the rail and the tabs never differ.
2. **The split cells.** `state/rail-badges.ts` gains `typeSplit(projects, order)` (active, manuscript, idea counts by `type`, the cockpit's `tsplit`) and `rollup(projects, buckets)` (the cockpit's `_rollup` rule: the first bucket whose `keys` meet the project's type or one of its status chips); `badgeOf` returns `cells?: { count, color, label }[]` for `projects` (the type split, coloured from `type_color` of `config/vocab.yml`, the cockpit's green for active), `archive` and `coauthor` (the `tab_rollup` buckets of `config/layout.yml`, their `color`), and `hpc` (running and pending); a pane tab draws the cells as one rounded strip after the count (`--fs-xxs`, bold, the cell's colour as background with the on-colour from `state/tones.ts` or the token pair the theme has); the rail button, 34 px wide, draws the count and puts the cells' "label: count" list in the badge's title.
3. **The missing badges.** LinkedIn overdue (`!`, red) and Applications active (green) if the rail lacks either today (read `rail-badges.svelte.ts` and say). Audit is O28's and is listed as not drawn.

Tests: `tests/state/rail-badges.test.ts` (the split, the rollup's first-bucket rule, a chip match, no bucket), `tests/shell/splitview.test.ts` or `workbench.test.ts` (a tab with a badge and cells rendered; the selectors of the browser suite unmoved), `tests/shell/rail.test.ts` (the title carries the cells).

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The cells live on the pane tab and in the rail badge's title; the rail's width does not change.

3. No new text key unless a title needs a word the catalog lacks; then `views.badge_cells` or the like, named in the report.
