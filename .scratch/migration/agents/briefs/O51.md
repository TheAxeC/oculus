## What to build

1. (Ruling Headerless panes, "a per-pane Hide Tab Row in the pane menu".) The pane menu of every pane holds "Hide Tab Row" while the pane's row shows and "Show Tab Row" while it is hidden, as its last item, after Move into New Window: `paneMenu` for the pane of a page and `floatMenu` for the pane of a float, each handed whether the row is hidden. The ids are `hide-tab-row` and `show-tab-row`, exported constants beside `MOVE_LIST`; the words are new text keys in `config/text/ui.yml` beside the `pane_` keys: `pane_hide_tab_row: Hide Tab Row` and `pane_show_tab_row: Show Tab Row`. The two ids join `PANE_ONLY`, so under `editor_actions: hidden` the strip's right-click menu and an empty pane's body menu hold the item too. A pick runs in the pane's `pick` before its `if (active === '') return;`, so a pane with no tab hides and shows its row; for the pane of a float the pick reaches the same store method. `isFloatItem` does not match either id.
2. (The same, "per-pane".) Whether a pane's row is hidden is a field of the pane, `rowHidden?: true` on `Pane` in `model.ts`, written as `locked` is: absent for a shown row, so a pane never hidden and one shown again read the same. It is carried by `repane` and by `keptPane`, so a hidden pane keeps its choice when its last tab closes and the pane stays, as it keeps its lock; both doc comments name it. It is set and cleared by a tree operation beside `setLocked` in `tree.ts`, exported where `setLocked` is (`src/lib/state/workbench/layout/index.ts`), and run by a store method beside `lockPane` in `layout.svelte.ts` through `change`, so the choice is saved with the layout and kept across a reload. `readPane` in `saved.ts` reads `rowHidden: true` and nothing else as hidden, and its head comment names the field. A pane that floats or docks keeps the field as `repane` carries it. The `Pane` doc comment says what the field does.
3. (Ruling 245 (a).) The new store method gets its row in `PATH_OPERATIONS` (`rerun.ts`), with the indexes of its path and page arguments, so a hide or show made in one browser tab while another moved the panes lands on the pane named by its tabs, and is dropped when that pane is gone. G102's two-tabs table gains one row, `a hide of the tab row of a pane the other tab moved lands on that pane`: the first client closes the pane at `[0]`, the second hides the row of the pane at `[2]`, and both clients end with `['b', 'c hidden']`; `panesOf` marks a pane whose row is hidden with ` hidden` after its name, as it marks a folded one with ` folded`. The table's `flatMap` runs the row in both orders.
4. (Ruling 246 (a).) `Menu.svelte` and `ContextMenu.svelte` each take a bindable `open` prop (`open = $bindable(false)` in place of the inner `$state`), closed by default, still bound to bits-ui as now; their head comments say that a caller may bind it to know whether the list is open. Every caller that does not bind it is unchanged.
5. (Ruling Headerless panes, "the hidden row shown while the pointer is at the pane's top edge".) A pane whose row is hidden draws its row and its path bar out of view: the view fills the pane from its top. The hidden row stays mounted and in the tab order (no `{#if}` removes it), so its Tab stop and its handlers stay. The row and its path bar show, drawn over the top of the view so the view keeps its size and a terminal is not resized, while any of these holds:
   - the pointer is in the pane's top edge, a band 4 px high along the pane's top, or over the shown row or path bar;
   - a menu opened from the row is open (the `...` menu, the strip's right-click menu, a tab's menu), read from the bound `open` of item 4;
   - the keyboard focus is inside the row and came there by a key press (Tab, Shift+Tab or a key that moves the focus), so the Tab stop of the strip and its pane menu show what they reach (`docs/HELP.md:80`); focus a closing menu gave back to its opener after a pointer pick does not keep the row shown;
   - a tab drag is in flight over the pane's top edge or the row, so a drop on the strip still lands, and a drag started from the shown row keeps it shown until the drag ends.
   It hides when none holds. Whether the row shows is state of the pane strip, never a read of the page (`spec.md`: a reader-visible behaviour is state, not a DOM side effect). The row drawn is the row the pane draws when shown (`stripShape` and the path bar, unchanged), with every control and menu it has. A pane whose row is `none` hides and shows its path bar alone; a pane with neither row nor path bar shows nothing at the edge. Shown over a view, the row and its path bar stand on the pane's own background, so they stay readable on a wallpaper. On a float the band lies inside the 6 px resize margin: a hover in it shows the row, and a press on the margin or on a docked float's sash still resizes.
6. (`spec.md`, the size rule and the folder rule.) `PaneView.svelte` stays at or under 520 lines and `layout.svelte.ts` at or under 880. The edge and the shown state live in `PaneStrip.svelte` and pure helpers in `pane-view.ts`, so `PaneView.svelte` gains only what hands the field and the pick on. If a file would still pass its limit, it is split by concern in this step into a file named for what it holds; `src/lib/shell/panes` and `src/lib/shell/panes/strip` hold ten items each, so a new file there means splitting that folder by concern into subfolders, the importers of each moved file edited, and the report names every moved file and why. Comments are not shortened and code is not compacted to stay under a limit.

Every comment and page line the change makes false is made true, each rewrapped as a whole paragraph at 100 columns: the head comment of `PaneStrip.svelte` (the row drawn under the two settings and the pane's own choice), the head comment of `PaneView.svelte` (the pane menu's items and the top row), the doc comments of `paneMenu` (`pane-view.ts:129-133`), `floatMenu` (`:280-283`) and `PANE_ONLY` (`:169-173`), `repane` (`model.ts:353-359`), `keptPane` (`tree.ts:172-178`), and the head comments of `Menu.svelte` and `ContextMenu.svelte`. `docs/HELP.md` gains one bullet after line 70, in the page's plain voice, saying that Hide Tab Row in the pane menu hides that pane's tab row and its path bar, that they show while the pointer is at the pane's top edge, that under `editor_actions: hidden` the item is in the strip's right-click menu, and that Show Tab Row brings the row back.

## Cases

- C1. (Ruling, "in the pane menu".) The `...` menu of a pane of a page whose row shows: its last item is Hide Tab Row. A pointer pick hides the row; the pane's `...` menu, opened from the row shown at the top edge, then holds Show Tab Row, and its pick shows the row again for good.
- C2. (Ruling, "the hidden row shown while the pointer is at the pane's top edge".) A pane with its row hidden: the view starts at the pane's top; the pointer moved into the top 4 px shows the row over the view, the view's size unchanged; the pointer moved down off the row hides it.
- C3. (Ruling 246: a menu opened from the shown row.) With the row shown at the edge, the `...` menu opened and the pointer moved into the menu below the row: the row stays shown while the menu is open, and hides when the menu closes by a pointer pick or a press outside with the pointer off the row.
- C4. (Ruling, "per-pane".) A page with two panes, one hidden: the other pane's row shows as before, and Hide Tab Row on it is its own choice.
- C5. (Kept across a reload.) A hidden row stays hidden after the page reloads; the layout file holds `rowHidden: true` on that pane alone.
- C6. (The pane of a float.) A float's `...` menu holds Hide Tab Row last; hidden, its row shows at the float's top edge, and a free float is moved by a drag of the shown strip's empty part.
- C7. (`editor_actions: hidden`, no `...` opener.) The strip's right-click menu, opened on the shown row, holds Hide Tab Row or Show Tab Row, and its pick runs as from the pane menu.
- C8. (The keyboard.) Tab moves the focus into a hidden pane's strip: the row shows while the focus is in it and hides when the focus leaves it.
- C9. (A drag onto the edge.) A tab dragged from another pane to the hidden pane's top edge shows the row, and a drop on the strip puts the tab there.
- C10. (The saved form, G29; a test.) A saved pane with `rowHidden: true` reads with the field; one with `rowHidden: false`, `"yes"` or without it reads without it.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The item is offered on every pane, a float's included: the ruling says per pane, and a float's row shown at its edge still carries the strip its drag starts from.
2. Its counterpart is "Show Tab Row", as Lock Group has Unlock Group: one item that names what its pick does.
3. The item is the menu's last: it changes how the pane is drawn, after the items that change what the pane holds or where it is.
4. The path bar hides with the row: the ruling's headerless pane is a view from its top, and a path bar left alone would be a header of its own. A pane whose row is `none` hides its path bar alone.
5. The shown row is drawn over the view rather than pushing it down, so a pointer passing the edge never resizes the view (a terminal reflows its lines on a resize).
6. The edge is 4 px, the same order as a sash, so it does not take a click meant for the view's first line.
7. The field is `rowHidden`, beside `locked`, saved with the layout: a pane's choice that a reload keeps, as Lock Group's is.
8. Focus keeps the row shown only when it came by a key press, so a pointer pick of Hide Tab Row hides the row at once although the menu gives the focus back to its opener in the row.
