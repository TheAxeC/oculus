## What to build

1. **The layout rule, one pure module.** One module (in `src/lib/views/academic/present/screen/`, beside `box.ts`, a view helper with no DOM) computes a slide's layout from what it holds, in the frame's own px: given the room under the slide's title (its width and height), each figure's width-to-height ratio, and whether the slide has text beside its figures, it gives each figure's box and the box the text has. Its inputs are measured from the drawn slide, never assumed: the room is the box the slide's content has under its title and above its foot (the reading column's width, at most `--slide-reading` 1040 px and less a part's own padding and border, such as a project slide's 13 px and 4 px, `Project.svelte:125-126`; the height from the title's bottom to the top of the foot), the text's height at the least size, and each figure's caption and explanation height. The gap between the text and a figure column is 26 px, the gap the project slide's columns have now (`Project.svelte:185-190`). A figure's box includes its padding and border (6 px and 1 px, `Panel.svelte:52-58`), and the image keeps its own ratio inside it. The rule:
   - The figures of a slide are placed as one block: one figure alone, or two or more side by side (the block's ratio the sum of their ratios, one height) or stacked (the inverse of the sum of their inverse ratios, one width), whichever draws the figures larger (the larger total area in the room).
   - A block whose ratio is below the room's is tall: it is a column the full height of the room, as wide as its ratio asks, the text beside it; when the slide has text, the column leaves the text at least 360 px of width and the gap between them.
   - A block whose ratio is at or above the room's is wide: it takes the full width under the text, as tall as its ratio asks and no taller than the room leaves under the text at its least size.
   - A slide with no text gives the block the whole room, centred.
   - No figure box is larger than the room: every figure stays inside the frame, drawn whole, never cropped by an `overflow: hidden` box.
   - A figure may be drawn larger than its pixels, as its ratio and the room ask.
   A figure's caption and explanation are drawn under it, inside its box's column, and their height is taken from the figure's room. A figure that fails to load has no ratio: its panel is drawn as the gap words, as text, and the layout does not wait for it.
2. **The text sized to its room.** The slide's text (every part that is not a figure: the chips, the sub, the hook, the specs, the lists, the eli5 box, the equation, a diagram, the words of a gap, a Markdown body, a summary's rows, a grid's cards) is drawn at the largest size between a least and a greatest size at which it fits the room the layout gives it. The size is one factor on the slide type scale of `themes/slide.css` (the title, the headings and the body sizes move together), times the app's `--text-scale` as now: least 0.75, greatest 1.25, the design sizes at 1. The factor is set on the slide's content (`.reading`'s parts), never on its foot: the key hint and the project facts strip keep their own sizes (`Facts.svelte` is not changed; O158 redesigns the strip), and the room is measured above the foot, the strip's kept room counted and the key hint left out, as the Stage's measure leaves it out, so a deck's first slide gets the same factor with and without the hint. The factor is found by measuring the drawn slide in the frame's own px, so it is the same on every surface: a bounded search run from the start at each trigger (a load, a change of frame, text scale, theme or slide), never an adjustment made from the size the search itself changes, so the resize observer does not run it again for its own change and a slide at the edge settles on one factor and never flickers. An equation keeps its own width fit (`Equation.svelte:21-40`) inside the text's box. This holds for every slide kind, the ones without figures included.
3. **Too tall only for text that does not fit.** A slide is Too tall only when its text does not fit its room at the least size; the finding, the tile's mark and the pane's scroll are as now in that case. A slide whose figures used to run past the frame fits once item 1 places them.
4. **Every surface the same way.** The Stage (the pane, both slides of the presenter screen, the Overview's tiles), the audience window and the PDF page draw the same layout and the same text size for a slide, since each lays it out at the frame's own px in the same content box: the print page's `.page` (`border: none`, `print/+page.svelte:164`) and the bare Stage (`Stage.svelte:134-136`) give the slide the same inner box as the bordered Stage (1150 by 590 in a 1280 by 720 frame), and the print page and the audience window draw at the app's text scale. Today only the shell sets `--text-scale` (`src/lib/shell/window/appearance.svelte.ts:109`), so both routes, which do not mount the shell, draw at 1; after the step they set it from the same setting. The layout is computed again when an image loads (its ratio known only then), when the frame or the text scale changes, and when the slide changes; the PDF page's ready signal (`data-ready`) waits for the layout after the images, so the PDF prints the laid-out slide. The PDF page's scale-down of a slide taller than its page stays for the too-tall case.
5. **The parts follow it.** `Project.svelte` and `Panel.svelte` place the panels' figures and the text by item 1's boxes, in place of the two fixed columns and `.fig { width: 100% }`; `Plain.svelte`'s image slide places its image by item 1 in place of `max-height: 480px`; a Markdown image inside text (`parts/Words.svelte:40-43`, `.block :global(img) { max-width: 100% }`) is drawn no wider than its text box and no taller than the room. A panel's diagram markup stays derived one line above its `{@html}` site, as the allow list of `tests/checks/source/html-sites.test.ts:37` (`'views/academic/present/Panel.svelte:diagram'`) states; if the site or the name moves, that allow-list line is changed with it. The other parts keep their look and take item 2's size.
6. **The docs and comments.** `docs/decks.md` says how a slide is laid out: the frame's shape kept and scaled to the screen; the figures by their proportions (tall a column beside the text, wide under it, several side by side or stacked, whichever is larger); the text sized between its least and greatest size to the room left; Too tall only for text that does not fit at its least size (its section "Slide findings", the `image` and `project` kinds, and a new paragraph at the end of its section "Slides", lines 160-166, on how a slide is laid out in its frame; the frame lines 101-102 of the section "deck" are not changed, since O155 rewrites line 100 beside them). The head comments of `Slide.svelte`, `Stage.svelte`, `Framed.svelte`, `Panel.svelte`, `Project.svelte`, `Plain.svelte`, `themes/slide.css` and the two routes say what is true after the step.
7. **No new test.** A test exists only as the test of a guarded entry, views have none (`guarded.md`'s head: "Views have no entry: the look on TEST covers them"), and a test exists only for behaviour whose failure loses work, breaks an installation or accepts a wrong configuration, which a slide's layout does not. The module's cases C1-C9 are checked by a run the report quotes: one `node --input-type=module -e "const m = await import('./src/lib/views/academic/present/screen/<module>.ts'); <a call per case>"` command (Node 24 strips the types; `tsx` is not installed and no dependency is added), typed on the command line and quoted whole with its output, no file written for it. The module therefore imports nothing through `$lib`: it is pure arithmetic, with at most relative imports of other modules that are themselves free of `$lib`. Items 2-5 are checked by reading and at the orchestrator's look on landing. The existing tests that name a changed file pass.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Tall and wide are told apart by the room's own ratio, so a figure is a column exactly when the full room height leaves it narrower than the room. Five real cases in the 1280 by 720 frame, on a project slide's room of 1023 by 418 (ratio 2.45; the reading column of 1040 px less 13 px padding and 4 px border, the height under a one-line title above the facts strip): `btfno.png` 0.97 tall, column 405 wide (text 592); `hccnet.png` 1.30 tall, column 543 (text 454); `meseret_landmark.png` 1.56 tall, column 652 would leave the text 345, so narrowed to 637 by 408; `incidentaf.png` 3.75 wide, 1023 by 273; `eq_meseret.png` 10.2 wide, 1023 by 100.
2. The text beside a column keeps at least 360 px, a little more than the 300 px the two columns of a project slide hold now, so a list item of the body size keeps about 15 words a line.
3. The least and the greatest text size are 0.75 and 1.25 of the slide type scale, constants of the layout module, not settings: no key of `present.yml` or of the settings is added.
4. A figure's ratio is read from the drawn image once it loads (`naturalWidth`, `naturalHeight`), which every format the browser draws gives, SVG included; the server's `sizeOf` (`src/lib/server/content/images.ts:303`) sizes no SVG and is a layer the deck reader may not call.
5. A diagram's markup and the gap words count as text: they have no ratio of their own, and their caption and explanation stay under them in the text's box.
6. The layout module is a view helper beside `box.ts`, since `src/lib/state/life` holds its ten entries.
7. The step carries the app's text scale to the print page and the audience window (item 4), since the step line asks every surface to draw the same size.
8. The facts strip and the key hint keep their own sizes (item 2), since O158 redesigns the strip; `Facts.svelte:14` ("scales with the slide, the text size and the theme") stays true.
9. A figure may be drawn larger than its pixels (item 1), so a small bitmap such as `dongho_silhouette.png` (636 by 526) fills its box in a large frame.
