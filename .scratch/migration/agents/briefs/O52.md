## What to build

Each item names where it comes from.

1. (Step line, "take one shape"; Decisions 1.) A new view partial `src/lib/views/boards/focus/FoldHead.svelte` draws a Focus section's heading row, the one place its markup and its style live: a bare `ToolButton` fold control (the fold glyph of the state, `expanded` set, its label `Fold <words>` or `Unfold <words>` from `focus.fold` and `focus.unfold`), then the title in bold with the section's glyph before it, then whatever the section puts at the end of the row (the command center's HPC pill). Its props are the section's glyph name, the title words, the words the fold control names, whether the section is open, the toggle, and an optional snippet for the end of the row. Its row has the command center's geometry today (ruling Focus headings): `gap: 8px` between the fold control and the title, 6 px between the glyph and the title, the title in `var(--fs-md)` at weight 600, `align-items: center`, `column-span: all`. The fold control, the glyph and the title stay on one line in every pane width (step line, "at the same places and the same height"): they sit in one group that does not wrap and that shrinks, and the title's words are in an element of their own that shrinks and is cut with an ellipsis (`min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; white-space: nowrap; text-overflow: ellipsis`), so a title longer than the pane is cut on the fold control's line rather than wrapped under it. The end-of-row snippet follows the group at the right end of the row, and in a pane narrower than the group and the snippet it wraps to a second line, as the command center's pill does now (`flex-wrap: wrap` on the row). Its head comment says what it draws and which sections use it.
2. (Step line, "the Command center ... headings".) `CommandCenterView.svelte` draws its heading through `FoldHead`, its HPC pill in the end-of-row snippet; its own `.head`, `.head > b` and `.pill` rules go, or the part of them `FoldHead` does not take stays where it is still used.
3. (Step line, "Tasks headings".) `Tasks.svelte` draws its heading through `FoldHead`; its own `.head` and `.head > b` rules go.
4. (Step line, "the career heading draws a bare glyph inside a `<summary>`".) `career/CareerPanel.svelte`: the banner is no longer a `<details>` with a `<summary>`; it is the same box (`.career`, `data-surface`, its borders and background as now) given the command center's `padding: 4px 8px`, with `FoldHead` as its first row, the career glyph `focus.career_glyph`, the title words `summary`, the fold control named with the new heading word (item 6), so it reads "Fold Career strategy" or "Unfold Career strategy" whatever the career line holds, an empty line included, open from `unfolded`, which still starts false, and the toggle flipping `unfolded`. The body (`.body`) stays mounted while folded and is hidden, as the closed `<details>` keeps it, so the career editor keeps its refusals, its result words and its saving state across a fold (premise above); its padding changes so its content keeps today's inset from the box's edges with the box's new padding. The rules `.career > summary` and `.career > summary > b` go. The head comment (lines 2-18) and the comment over the banner's style say what is true after the change.
5. (Step line, "at the same places and the same height".) With the same box padding, the same borders and one `FoldHead`, the three fold circles, glyphs and titles stand at the same x, and the three rows have one height, the command center's 22 px.
6. (Ruling 225.) `config/text/views/boards/focus.yml` gains `career_heading: Career strategy` under `focus:`, between `career_glyph` (line 9) and `career_next` (line 10), and `CareerPanel.svelte` names its fold control with it through `focus.fold` and `focus.unfold`, as the other two headings name theirs with their heading words. The row's title stays the career line (`summary`).

One text key is added, `focus.career_heading` (item 6, ruling 225); it is a fixed word, so it has no empty case, and `focus:` holds no other key of that name. The step otherwise uses `focus.fold`, `focus.unfold` and the glyph and title keys that exist.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. One component for the three headings (step line, "take one shape"), so their markup and their geometry are written once and cannot drift apart again, in place of the same rules copied into three files; it is a view partial of the Focus board beside the sections that use it.
2. (Ruled by 225, no longer a decision of this brief.) The career fold control is named with the heading word `focus.career_heading` (item 6).
3. The row takes the command center's gap of 8 px, not the Tasks' 4 px, since ruling Focus headings aligns the career heading with the command center's.
4. The career heading behaves as the other two headings do (ruling Focus headings, aligned with the Command center heading): its fold control is a bare `ToolButton`, so it shows its name, "Fold Career strategy" or "Unfold Career strategy", as a tooltip, as the other two show theirs; and a click on the title words no longer folds the banner, since on the other two headings only the fold control folds.
