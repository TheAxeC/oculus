# L1, one tree per page

Read `../spec.md` first, its section "The principles every package is judged on" above all. Axel ruled on 2026-09-14 at 20:29 (plan.md Rulings): pages and panels become one model. A page is one tree; the docked panel, its toggle, its dock side, its size and the pane menu's move-to-panel go; every child of a split collapses to its edge through one control and expands again, the collapsed state saved with the layout. His reason, in his words: a split that looked like the panel was not the panel, because only the docked area had the dedicated button. Nested pages are L2, on top of this package; nothing here prepares them beyond keeping the tree one tree.

## What to build

1. **The model.** `Workbench = { root: Layout; focus?: Focus }`, `Focus = { path: number[] }`; `Target`, `Dock`, `DOCKS`, `PANEL_MIN`, `PANEL_MAX`, `PANEL_DEFAULT`, `clampPanel`, `dock`, `resizePanel`, `dockedAt`, `panelSize`, `holderOf`, `treeOf`, `withTree` go. `Split` gains `folded?: number[]`, the indexes of its children collapsed to their edge; a folded child keeps its share in `sizes` for when it is expanded; `checkLayout` refuses a `folded` index past the children and a split whose every child is folded, and `fold(layout, path)` refuses to fold the last open child of a split. The one-tree operations of `tree.ts` are the operations; `transfer` and `splitWith` become `moveInstance` and a one-tree `splitWith`; `maximise(workbench, path)`; `defaultWorkbench` is one `status` pane.
2. **Where a view opens.** `openView(view, state?)` opens in the pane that holds the active instance, as it does in main today. What shift-click on the rail and `openPinnedView` did (open in the panel) becomes: a new pane split to the right of that pane (`splitPane` with `row`, after), unless the view is already open, which activates it as today. One rule for both, `openBeside(view, state?)`.
3. **Folding.** Every child of a split can be folded and expanded: the pane menu gains one item (`ui.pane_fold`) that folds the menu's pane in its parent split, absent on a root pane; a folded child is drawn by `SplitView.svelte` as a strip of a fixed thickness along the split's direction (a `FOLD_PX` constant in `ui/sash-size.ts` beside `SASH_PX`, and the split's free space computed without it) carrying one control that expands it (`ui.pane_expand`, the glyph named in `config/glyphs.yml` with an ASCII stand-in, chosen by the worker from the vocabulary or added with both forms) and, for a pane child, the label of its active tab; the sashes on either side of a folded child are not drawn. Folding the child that holds the maximised pane restores the workbench first. The store: `foldPane(path)`, `expandChild(path, index)`.
4. **The saved shape.** `LAYOUT_VERSION` 4. `parseLayout` reads 4, and reads 3 and 2 by migration: a page whose `dock` is `hidden` or whose panel is an empty pane keeps `main` as its root; otherwise the root is a split, `row` for `right` and `col` for `bottom`, of `[main, panel]` with sizes `[1 - panelSize, panelSize]`; a hidden dock over a panel that holds tabs is the same split with the panel child folded, so no tab is lost. `PREVIOUS_KEY`'s version-2 workbench goes the same way. `checkWorkbench` checks the root and the focus. `parseArrangement` reads `root` (a node) and nothing else; `config/layout.yml`'s comment and the `sessions` arrangement are rewritten as one `root` (a row split of the old main and the old panel pane at 0.7 and 0.3).
5. **The shell.** `Workbench.svelte` draws one `SplitView` over the root; the between-trees sash, `dock` and `share` go; `PaneView.svelte` takes `path` alone, carries `data-pane-path` and no `data-pane-target`; the drop targets carry a path and no target; the rail's dock menu goes with its four catalog keys; `workbench-keys.svelte.ts` and `keys.ts` drop the target (`KeyContext.target`, `KeyAction` `open`'s target); `Palette.svelte` and `UpdateTray.svelte` where they name a target. `ui.move_to_main`, `ui.move_to_panel` and `ui.panel` go if nothing reads them after (`tests/keys.test.ts` reports an orphan).
6. **DESIGN.md.** The shell paragraph, "Layout as data" and step 3 say one tree per page with foldable children; a dated decision line (2026-09-14, Axel: one tree per page, every split child foldable through one control, the panel category retired because a split that looked like the panel was not one) is added beside the earlier docking decisions, which stay as the record they are.
7. **Tests.** The layout, pages, arrangement, drag, pins, workbench and keys tests follow the model: `layout-fixture.ts` builds a one-tree workbench; new cases for `fold`, `expand`, the refusals (the last open child, a root pane), the migration of a version-3 page with a right panel, a bottom panel, a hidden dock over an empty panel and over a held panel, and of a version-2 workbench; `openBeside` opening a new pane to the right and activating an open view; the shipped arrangement parsing to one tree. Browser: `tests/browser/pages.spec.ts` and `tinted.spec.ts` follow; a new `layout.spec.ts` folds a pane from its menu, sees the strip with the tab's label, expands it from the strip's control, and checks the folded state survives a reload. The test count does not fall except for cases whose subject is gone (the dock, the between-trees transfer), each named in the report.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. A folded child is recorded as an index list on its split (`folded`), its share kept in `sizes`, so expanding restores the width it had.
2. What opened in the panel opens beside the active pane, in a new pane to its right; this is the user-visible successor of shift-click and of a pinned session view, and it is reported as a decision for Axel to reverse.
3. The fold control is the pane menu's item and the strip's one button; no keyboard chord is added, the menu being the keyboard path.
4. The hidden-dock migration keeps a held panel as a folded child rather than dropping its tabs.
