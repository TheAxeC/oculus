## What to build

### Part A: motion for the actions the reader starts and waits on

Every place below shows motion from the press until the action's answer, with words naming what runs, and none under the system's reduce-motion setting: a turning glyph uses `Glyph`'s `turning` (or `ToolButton`'s), a moving line uses `Progress` with `percent: null`; both already stop under reduce-motion, and no new animation is written. Under a terminal palette the glyph's stand-in stays still while the words and the moving line show, as for every turning glyph today (Decision 7). Background work gets no motion and no new words: git auto-fetch, the HPC poll, the feed watcher, a language server starting, a file save. The LaTeX build and an app's progress keep the motion they have and are not changed.

1. **Which git action runs.** The repositories store keeps, per root, the name of the action in flight in place of the bare busy list, so `busy(root)` answers as it does today, and a new reader answers the running action's words for a root, and for a repository's roots together (every root whose `GitState.top` is that repository's), so a view or item following another root of the same repository sees the action. The words are those of Decision 1; every other action keeps "Working". Two repositories running two actions keep their own words. A send refused because the root is busy changes nothing. The state is the page's only: a tab closed while an action runs leaves the action's words to every other place that shows the root until its answer, and a reloaded page holds no action in flight.
2. **The Source Control view and the repository's row.** While one of the actions of Decision 1 runs on its repository: the view (`Repo.svelte`) draws a moving progress line across its top, under its header, labelled with the action's words, and its busy line says the action's words in place of "Working" (Cancel stays as it is); the repository's row in the Repositories list (`RepoList.svelte`) draws a moving progress line along its foot and takes the action's words as its title in place of "Working"; and every other place of the view that says "Working" for that repository, the commit box's placeholder and button titles (`CommitBox.svelte:90,198,215`, those three lines only, and no progress line in that file), a folder row (`FolderRow.svelte`) and the graph's actions (`GraphActions.svelte`), says the action's words. An action not in Decision 1 keeps "Working" everywhere and draws no progress line.
3. **The status bar's git item.** While push, pull, fetch, sync or publish runs on the item's repository (any of its roots, item 1), the sync item draws the `sync` glyph turning before its counts, in place of the `behind` and `ahead` glyphs, the counts kept, and its hover words are the action's words; the Publish item, for one remote or several, is drawn while publish runs as one status `ToolButton` with the `sync` glyph turning and the action's words, in place of its `publish` glyph or its `Menu`. When the action ends the item is drawn as today.
4. **Clone.** Each clone the reader starts posts a notice with a moving progress line (`progress: { percent: null, cancel: null }`) from the moment the folder is picked until the answer, worded `app.clone.running` with the project's title or the repository's name (Decision 1), and at the answer that notice is removed and the done line or the refusal the store posts today is posted. The project card's Clone control also turns its glyph while `cloning(id)` where it is drawn (the card's row, or the More menu's entry), with its words "Cloning..." as today; `CardAction` gains what that needs.
