# Q15, Live HPC stopping

Read `../spec.md` first, its section "The principles every package is judged on" above all. Axel reported on 2026-09-13 at 12:00 that "live hpc seems to sometimes just stop working". This package is the diagnosis first, then the fix of what the diagnosis finds, then the head's "as of" made visibly stale when a poll is late. Oculus is a port of the cockpit: the cockpit's poller (`tools/cockpit/src/cockpit/hpc.py` lines 17 to 26 and 248, `serve.py` line 125) runs `ssh -o ConnectTimeout=8 -o BatchMode=yes <host> <batch>` every `slurm.poll_min` minutes with a 40 s budget and publishes `unreachable` with the last stderr line as the hint and an empty job list; oculus does the same, and the one behaviour that differs from that, the last good rows kept under the stale mark, is Axel's yes of 2026-09-15 00:47 (item 7).

## What the orchestrator measured (2026-09-15, 00:25, on Axel's own server on port 8790; the files named below are unchanged at dispatch, 03:24, against 7b3ee54c)

- A subscription to the `hpc` channel over the server's socket (`node hpc-probe2.mjs`, a `ws` client with an allowed Origin) answered: `status: unreachable`, `hint: "ssh: connect to host hpc-head1.ewi.utwente.nl port 22: Operation timed out"`, `updated: 2026-09-14T22:23:52Z`, `pollMin: 20`, message version 14. So at that moment the cluster was unreachable from his machine (no VPN) and the page showed the hint with no "as of" and no jobs, which is the cockpit's behaviour too.
- `~/.ssh/config`'s `utwente` block sets `ControlMaster`, `ControlPath` and `ControlPersist` (the option names were read, nothing else). Every poll therefore goes through a shared multiplexed master when one is alive. A master whose connection died with the VPN or a sleep keeps its socket file until its own TCP timeout, and a client that connects to it waits on the master rather than on `ConnectTimeout`, which only governs a fresh TCP connect. This is the one lead for a poll that hangs rather than fails: the 40 s budget in `src/lib/server/hpc/ssh.ts` does fire, `child.kill()` ends the client, and the master stays, so every following poll meets the same dead master until it expires.
- `src/lib/server/hpc/poll.ts`: one `setInterval` at `pollMin` minutes (20); after a failed poll the next attempt is 20 minutes later; a Refresh control is the only sooner path. `prewarm` is read in `src/lib/data/sources/config.ts` lines 414 to 442 and used nowhere in the server: the cockpit's `slurm_prewarm_logs` loop (`hpc.py` 422, `serve.py` 134) is not ported. This is an open item for Axel in the state file, not part of this package.
- `src/lib/views/hpc/Head.svelte` draws "as of hh:mm" and "every 20m" only for status `ok`; for `unreachable` and `error` it draws the state word and the hint in a `code`; nothing marks an `ok` payload whose next poll is overdue.
- `tests/server/hpc/poll.test.ts` covers the runner injected: start, success, a rejected timeout, refresh, timers. `ssh.ts`'s `run()` itself has no test.

## What to build

1. **Diagnosis, reproduced with stubs, in tests.** A stub `ssh` executable written by the test into a temp dir put first on `PATH` (the runner spawns `ssh` by name) proves each of these against `run()` and `watchHpc`: (a) a command that never returns (`sleep 300`) rejects with `timeout` at the budget and the child is gone afterwards (`kill -0` on its pid fails); (b) a command that ignores SIGTERM (`trap '' TERM; sleep 300`) still rejects at the budget and is gone within a short grace (see 2); (c) `mux_client_request_session: read from master failed` on stderr with exit 255 publishes `unreachable` with that line as the hint; (d) `Operation timed out` on stderr with exit 255 publishes `unreachable` with that line. The report states which of (a) to (d) failed before the fix, with the output.
2. **The runner.** `run()` in `ssh.ts` passes `-o ControlMaster=no -o ControlPath=none` so a poll never waits on a shared master and `ConnectTimeout` governs every connect; on the budget it sends SIGTERM and, 2 s later if the child is still alive, SIGKILL, and the promise rejects at the budget as now. Every caller (`poll.ts`, `actions.ts`) gets this since they share `run()`.
3. **The cadence after a failure.** While the last poll was not `ok`, the poller retries after `RETRY_MIN` (2, a module constant in `poll.ts`) instead of waiting the full `pollMin`; a poll that comes back `ok` restores the regular cadence. Implemented as one timer that reschedules itself with the interval of the last outcome, so `close()` clears one handle. A test with fake timers proves: failed poll, `RETRY_MIN` later a second attempt, `ok`, then `pollMin` until the next.
4. **The head.** `Head.svelte` takes the client's clock (`useState().clock.minuteNow()`, a minute tick) and, for an `ok` payload whose `updated` is older than `pollMin + 2` minutes, draws the "as of" part with a `late` class (the muted tone with the `bad` colour the head already has) and the text `hpc.as_of_late` ("as of {0}, the next poll is late"); for `unreachable` and `error` it draws `hpc.last_ok` ("last reached {0}") after the state word when a previous `ok` time is known. For that the payload carries `lastOk?: string`, the `updated` of the last `ok` publish, set by the poller on every publish (`HpcPayload` in `src/lib/data/model.ts`). The words go in `config/text/views/hpc.yml`.
5. **Tests.** The stub cases of 1; the runner's kill escalation; the retry cadence; the head's three states with a fixed clock (`tests/views/hpc/head.test.ts`, or beside the existing hpc view tests); the payload's `lastOk`.
7. **The last good rows.** On a poll that is not `ok` the poller publishes the status and the hint with the `jobs`, `running`, `pending`, `total`, `recent` and `sinfo` of the last `ok` payload (held in the watcher beside `quota`), `updated` the failed poll's time and `lastOk` the last good one; before any `ok` those fields are empty as now. The view draws the tables whenever `jobs` or `recent` hold rows, under the head's state word, hint and `hpc.last_ok`, and the `hpc.unreachable_msg` line stays above them when the status is not `ok`. A test proves the rows survive a failed poll and clear on `close()`/a fresh watcher.
6. **DESIGN.md.** The Live HPC paragraph names the retry after a failure and the late mark, one sentence each, no history.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible)

1. The poller bypasses the user's ssh multiplexer (`ControlMaster=no`): a connection every 20 minutes and one an hour for quota cost nothing, and a stale master is the one way a poll can wait past `ConnectTimeout`.
2. `RETRY_MIN` is a constant, not a config key: the cockpit has no such key, and a key is a user-visible shape Axel has not asked for.
3. A failed poll keeps the last good rows under the stale mark (item 7), a difference from the cockpit Axel allowed on 2026-09-15 at 00:47.
