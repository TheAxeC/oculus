## What to build

1. **The key.** A row of `categories:` takes `sort:`, a list of the words `stage`, `year` and `title`. `stage` orders by `stageRank` over the board's stage order, as `compare` does; `year` orders by `project.year`, newest first, a project with no year after every project with one, a year of 0 being a year; `title` orders by `title.localeCompare`, as `compare` does. The words are read left to right, each breaking the ties the words before it leave. A card's place in its section is: every pinned card before every unpinned one, and within each of the two groups the row's `sort`, then, for cards the row's words leave tied, the order `boardItems` gives (pinned, stage, title), so a row's order never depends on the order the projects were read in. A row with no `sort:`, or with `sort: []`, keeps today's order. A one-word text (`sort: year`) reads as the list of that word, as every `multi` field of the options catalog does. A word named twice is read once. `categoriesOf` reads `sort` into `Category` (a field holding the words in order); a `sort` that is null, not a list or a text, or holds a word outside the three reads as no `sort`.
2. **The order inside a section.** `cardSections` orders each section's cards by its row's `sort` as item 1 says, the row being the one of `preset.categories` that gives the section its label and colour, so with two hubs every card of a section goes by the first hub's row (the primary's when it has the key), whatever hub the card is from. The section of cards in no row keeps `boardItems`' order, and so does a board whose `cards:` is `grid`, which draws no sections. `boardItems` and `compare` keep their order unchanged, so the readers of `selectors.board` see no change. The comparison of each word lives once, in `src/lib/state/boards/board/presets.ts` beside `stageRank`, and both `compare` in `items.ts` and the section order in `facets.ts` use it, so neither file imports the other for it and `tests/checks/imports/cycles.test.ts` stays green.
3. **Oculus's rows.** `config/catalogs/vocab.yml`'s `publications` and `other-outputs` rows carry `sort: [stage, year, title]`. Every other row carries none.
4. **One finding per wrong value.** `categoriesFindings` checks a row's `sort` against the options catalog's spec of the field (item 5) through `refusedBy`, so a word outside the three, null, or a value that is neither a list nor a text is one finding naming `categories[<n>].sort`, worded by `findings.config.boards_value` as the other row fields are. The whole-file shape gives no second finding for it: the vocabulary file's `owned` in `src/lib/data/shapes/catalog-files.ts` claims the keys `categories.<n>.sort` and `categories.<n>.sort.<i>` for every row index, by a predicate (`under` takes prefixes only, so it cannot name an index), and claims nothing else, so every other issue of the file is reported as it is today. The shipped rows give no finding. The report shows, by a case-run program, that `sort: [stage, nonsense]`, `sort: 3`, `sort: null` and `sort: year` give, on oculus's file and on a hub's merged rows, exactly the findings item 4 names (one, one, one, none). The report also says, with the run that shows it, whether a second hub's `categories` rows reach any check; whatever it shows, that reach is the same for every field of a second hub's rows and is not changed in this step.
5. **The shapes and the help.** `categoryRow` in `vocab.ts` gains `sort`, a word of the three or a list of them (a union, so the editor's completion keeps the words), with a description saying what it orders and how. `config/options/catalogs/vocab.yml`'s `categories` entry gains the field `sort`, `control: multi` with `options: [stage, year, title]`, its help in the line's own lower-case style saying that it orders the category's cards, read left to right after pinned projects, `year` newest first with no year last, and that a row with none goes by stage, then title. The entry's `help` and the comment above `categories:` in `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:262-264` say the same in one sentence each.
6. **The documents.** `docs/HELP.md:307` says, after the sentence on the sections, how the cards are ordered inside one, on a board and in the Awaiting box's tab: pinned first, then the category's `sort`, `publications` and `other-outputs` going by stage, then newest year, then title, every other category by stage, then title, a board with `cards: grid` by stage, then title. `docs/templates.md:445-452` says a row named there replaces oculus's row whole, `sort` included, and names `sort` in the example row. The head comments of `items.ts`, `facets.ts`, `categories.ts`, `boards.ts`, `presets.ts` and `Projects.svelte` and the doc comments of `compare`, `cardSections` and every function touched are made true. Grep `sort`, `categories` and `cardSections` across `src`, `tests`, `config` (`--exclude=local.yml`), `docs` and `README.md` and carry the change to every sentence it makes false.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Cards the row's words leave tied fall back to the board's own order (pinned, stage, title), so `sort: [year]` still breaks a tie by stage and title and every order is total.
2. Pinned cards stay before unpinned ones in every section whatever the row's `sort`, and each group is ordered by the row's `sort`, since a pin is the reader's own order and the ruling places the key after pinned projects.
3. The order is applied in `cardSections`, per section, and `boardItems` is unchanged, since the category is what sets the order and every other reader of `boardItems` draws no sections.
4. A section goes by the row the board's union gives it (the first hub holding the key, the primary first), the row that already gives the section its label and colour, so one section never mixes two orders.
5. A grid board and the section of cards in no category keep the board's order, since neither has a row to read a `sort` from.
6. A wrong `sort` is one finding from `categoriesFindings`, the shape claiming none of it through `owned`, and the row's `sort` is then not read, as a wrong `stages` word is not read; a repeated word is no finding, since it changes no order.
7. The Settings control is `multi` with `options:`, since it keeps the order of the picks and refuses another word.
