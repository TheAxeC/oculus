## What to build

1. **Two new lists of stages.** `position_stages` and `teaching_stages` join `config/catalogs/vocab.yml` after `fund_stages`, each a list of rows `{ label, color, statuses }` with a one-line comment above it in the style of `fund_stages`'s:
   - `position_stages`: `{ label: "Active", color: "#16a34a", statuses: [active] }`, `{ label: "Applied", color: "#0891b2", statuses: [applied] }`.
   - `teaching_stages`: `{ label: "Ongoing", color: "#16a34a", statuses: [ongoing] }`, `{ label: "Planned", color: "#7c5cff", statuses: [planned] }`.
   Each is registered in every place `fund_stages` is (the list under "What is on the tree"): the zod shape (the same row shape, one definition shared by the three keys, its descriptions naming no column: "The words the stage is drawn with in its Focus column." and "The statuses of an entry that place it in this stage."), `UNION_FIELD` by `label`, `OWN_HUB_KEYS`, `HUB_LIST`'s row and comment in `hub-names.ts`, `WHOLE` in `in-place.ts`, the options file's row (help naming the Focus Job applications or Teaching & supervision column, the same fields), both file headers' list of keys replaced whole, `docs/HELP.md:538`, `docs/templates.md` (a commented example after `fund_stages`'s), and `tests/checks/config/config-folder.test.ts`. A hub sets each as it sets `fund_stages`: its list replaces oculus's whole.

2. **The band's three columns stack by stage.** `collectionLanes` reads the three lists by one rule, the one `fund_stages` has now: each stage of the union of every hub's words holds the entries whose status the row of the same label in their own hub's words names, sorted as now, a stage holding nothing left out. Funding reads the funding registry's entries by `fund_stages`, positions the collections flagged `band_positions` by `position_stages`, teaching the collections flagged `band_teaching` by `teaching_stages`. `Band` becomes `{ tones, funding, applications, education }` with each of the three a `BandStage[]`; `stageItemCount` and `bandHasCards` read them so. `Band.svelte` draws the three columns with one snippet, the stage blocks the funding column draws now, each column headed by `LaneHead` with the count of its cards. The positions sub-line `focus.jobs_sub` reads "job applications" (it now holds active ones too), and the teaching sub-line `focus.teaching_sub` reads "running and planned".

3. **The Applications badge counts what the Positions column shows.** `openCount` in `rail-badges.svelte.ts` counts the entries of the collection that a stage of `position_stages` holds, by the same function the band uses, over every hub of the workspace and by each hub's own words as the band reads them (export it from `focus-lanes.ts`; no second filter written beside it). An entry two stages hold is counted once. The badge counts the column unfiltered by the Focus filter, as every rail badge does.

4. **The Awaiting box lists the applications by `deadline_met`.** `statuses.applied` gains `deadline_met: true` (it keeps `closes_deadline: true`). The Awaiting box's applications are the entries of the collections flagged `awaiting` whose status carries `deadline_met` in their own hub's words, as its grants are (`focus.ts:621-629`); the filter's copy at `filter.ts:465-480` reads the same. A funding scheme of status `applied` is then listed under the box's Grants, by the same flag. The comment of `statuses` at `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:102` says `deadline_met` also lists an application in the Awaiting box.

5. **The three `focus:` keys leave.** `applications_open`, `education_open` and `applications_sent` are removed from `config/defaults/behavior.yml` (with the comment above them) and `config/options/behavior.yml`, and `focusWords` with them when it is left with no caller. The G126 row at `hub-vocab.test.ts:160-166` keeps what it proves on a list that still exists: with `statuses: { applied: null }`, the `Applied` row of oculus's `position_stages` loses `applied` from its `statuses`, with no finding.

6. **The dated lines move to Tasks (ruling 446).** The ticker splits its lines by who sets the date:
   - The Tasks section draws every due follow-up (an `after:` day that has come, or none), every open subtask (with a deadline, past it, or with none) and every open entry of the collection flagged `tasks` (dated, past its date, or undated, whatever `deadline_horizon_days` says), and every project whose `deadline:` is not a date as now, each in its group as now (Follow-ups, Subtasks, Tasks). Inside a group the dated lines come first, by date, the most overdue first, then the undated ones in the order they have now.
   - A dated row of the Tasks section carries its date as its tail (`dateTail`: "in 3d", "3d ago") and its tone (`daysTone`: red once past, amber within 14 days), as a Command Center row does.
   - The Command Center keeps the deadlines of papers, venues, funding, positions, leads, events, teaching and joint work, and its Overdue group keeps those lines alone: an entry of the tasks file is in neither. Its "Subtask deadlines" and "Follow-ups" groups are removed with their rows, their catalog words (`focus.command.grp_subtask_dl_label`, `focus.command.grp_followups_label`) when nothing else reads them, and their names in `CommandGroups` and `hasCommandRows`; `runsByKind` and the `kindGroups` snippet go with them. A Command Center left with no line is not drawn, as `hasCommandRows` decides now.
   - `Ticker` loses `followups` and `subtasks`; `tasks` holds what this item lists. The Focus filter keeps and counts `tasks` as it does now, and the section's "N hidden" counts every line it hides.
   - Snoozed lines stay in the Command Center's Snoozed group, as now.
   - The comments of `Ticker`, `ticker()`, `taskGroups`, `Tasks.svelte` and `CommandCenterView.svelte` say what they do after the change ("the command center is what is dated" is no longer true).

7. **The documents.** Each sentence the premises list as made false is rewritten to say what holds after the change: `docs/HELP.md:529` and `:547`, `config/defaults/behavior.yml:36`, `config/options/sources.yml:80-89`, `config/options/behavior.yml:161` (the tasks file's dated entries are no longer among what the horizon bounds), `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:202` and `docs/templates.md:66` and `:276` (a `ticker:` category names a Command Center group, and the tasks file's lines are Tasks lines). `docs/HELP.md:294` says, in the Focus bullet: the band's three columns stack by `fund_stages`, `position_stages` and `teaching_stages`; the Awaiting box lists the applications whose status carries `deadline_met`; dated subtasks, due follow-ups and the tasks file's entries are lines of the Tasks section with their date, and the Command Center holds the deadlines of the other records. `docs/data-map.md` is kept out of the paths: the report's "Doc text" section gives the new lines 82 ("these 51 keys", and the two keys in the list of keys replaced whole) and the two lines after 108 (`position_stages:` and `teaching_stages:` in the style of `fund_stages:`), which the orchestrator applies at landing; the line numbers are those of the main checkout (80 and 106 at HEAD).

The rules file's, unchanged: ASCII, catalog words through `t()`, `ui/` primitives, one element per line in Svelte, a comment rewrapped at 100 columns, no history in comments or test titles, no migration code (an old `focus.applications_open` in a workspace file is a finding, never read), the size limits (880 lines, 520 for a component), at most ten items in a folder.
