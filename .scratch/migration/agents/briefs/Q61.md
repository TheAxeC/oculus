## What is on the tree (read on main at a0cb6d14)

A tone the palette does not know gives `var(--c--lane)`, which resolves to nothing, so that header draws no fill and its text falls back to whatever it inherits. `config/vocab.yml` `focus_lane_tones` is where a wrong or empty tone would come from.
