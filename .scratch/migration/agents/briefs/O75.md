## What to build

1. **The facets.** In `presets.ts`, `FACETS` becomes, in this order: `slice`, `form`, `status`, `role`, `year`, `domain`, `venue`, `pillar`, `funding`, `dataset`, `compute`, `reproduced`, `code`, `sessions`. `stage` and `category` are no facets any more: the slices of item 2 stand for them. In `facetValues`: `form` gives `[project.form]` or nothing; `venue` gives the key of the item's venue (`keyOf(item.venue.id)`) or nothing (an item with no venue, as G92's fixture items are, gives nothing), so a venue two hubs share is one value, as G92 says of a record; `status` gives `item.project.chips` only, so the Situation group lists no form (the card's `item.chips` stays as it is). The value orders: forms in the order of `forms`; venues by count, then by name; slices as item 2 says. A form's label is its `chipLabel`; a venue's is `item.venue.short`, the venue's name where it has no short name. `facetHeading` gains Form and Venue; the status heading's words become "Situation". A saved filter of `stage` or `category` is dropped by `parseBoardState` as any facet not in `FACETS` is, with no code of its own for it.
2. **The slices.** `src/lib/state/boards/board/facets.ts` gains the slices of a board (that folder holds ten items, so no new file): for each category the board's projects are in (in the order of `categories`, the cards in no category last as "other"), one slice per stage of that category's projects, in the order of `stages`, or, for a category whose row has a `forms:` block, one slice per form of its projects, in the order of `forms`. A slice's value is `<category key>/<stage or form>` (the empty key for "other"), its words the label of its stage or form up to the first " (" ("de-risking", "in preparation", "journal article"), with the whole label as its tip, its colour the stage's `color`, or for a form slice its category's colour. A project is in a slice when its category is the slice's and its stage (or, under a forms block, its form) is the slice's word; each project is in exactly one slice, and a project with no stage (the schema requires one, and no hub has such a project) is in no slice and is shown only while no slice is picked. `facetValues(item, 'slice')` gives that one value, so `applyFilters` keeps the projects of any picked slice (picks joined with or) and `facets` counts each slice over what the other filters leave, as every facet is counted. Only the slices the board's projects hold are drawn, from the board's projects before any filter, so a slice keeps its place at 0. `parseBoardState` drops a saved slice value that names no slice of the board, as it drops a category today; the `categories` argument becomes the board's slice values.
3. **The bar, line 1** (`FilterBar.svelte`, rewritten): the search; stale; the deadline toggle reading "deadline 3", its count the board's projects that carry a deadline, before any filter; Mine only, drawn on every board while a `self` is named, and where `offersMine` is false drawn disabled with the tip "Every project here is yours" (a new key of `config/text/views/boards/projects.yml`), and applied only where `offersMine` is true, as now; then one group button per facet that has values, in the order Form, Situation, Role, Year, Domain, Venue, then More holding pillar, funding, dataset, compute, reproduced, code and sessions as headed sections. A button reads its facet's name, or, while a value is picked, "<Facet>: <first picked value's words>" and " +<n>" for the others ("Form: journal article +1"); More reads "More" and the count of its picks. The group buttons that do not fit beside the controls before them in the bar's width go into More, last in, first out, by `fit()` over their measured widths, so line 1 is one line at any pane width; More's list then holds their sections first, and More carries the red badge and the amber mark of item 5 while it holds Situation or Venue (Z1003). A facet with a picked value always has its button (in line 1 or in More), and its list holds the picked value at 0 when no project holds it any more, so every value that narrows the board can be taken out there (Z1002); a role value is applied only while the bar offers the Role group, so a role saved while no `self` is named does not narrow the board. The deadline toggle reads "deadline 0" on a board with no dated project (Z1005).
4. **The group list** (a new partial of the board, `src/lib/views/boards/projects/PickMenu.svelte`, since `src/lib/ui/menu` holds ten items; on bits-ui Popover and Command, ruling Z996): a button that opens a list with a search field at its top, then the values, each with a dot in its colour (item 6), its words and its count; the picked values ticked. Typing filters the values by their words; the arrows move; a press or Enter toggles the value and the list stays open; Escape and a press outside close it and give the focus back to the button. A list of several facets (More) heads each facet's values with its name, and the search runs over all of them. The Venue list draws behind each venue's count a bar as long as the count is against the largest count of the list. Its look follows `MenuList.svelte`'s (the list's box, rows, dots and badges in the same tokens), written in its own file.
5. **The counts on the controls** (ruling 351 (a)): the totals line (`Stats.svelte`) is deleted with its key `board.stats`, `stat_*` keys and its `<Stats>`. `stats()` is reshaped to what the controls draw, counted over the board's projects before any filter: how many projects, how many carry a deadline, how many are blocked (a chip flagged `blocked`), and the venues that reach `venue_conc_warn` counted only over the projects at a stage flagged `live`, as `venueLoad` counts them. The Situation button carries a red badge "2 blocked" while one or more projects are blocked. The Venue button carries an amber mark while a venue reaches the warning, with the tip "BSPC: 5 live projects, the warning is 4" (one clause per warned venue, joined by "; "). `submitReady` and `STATS_VENUES` go with the totals line; `humanSize`, `disseminated` and `waitingLine` stay.
6. **The colours of the picks** (ruling 348 (a)): a picked value is drawn filled in its own colour: a slice in its stage's (a form slice in its category's), a domain in its domain's `color`, a value of a facet whose values carry none in its facet's colour. The facets' colours are tone names in `config/text/views/boards/projects.yml` beside the glyph names it holds (`facet_<key>_tone`), one per facet, each read by a whole literal key as `filter-menu.ts:40-42` reads the headings, since the keys gate refuses a computed key: Form pink, Situation purple, Role blue, Year slate, Domain teal, Venue amber, and More's facets cyan. A value not picked is drawn with its colour as a dot (in a list) or an outline (a slice).
7. **The bar, line 2** (a new partial `src/lib/views/boards/projects/SliceLine.svelte`, ruling 350 (a)): for each category the board holds, its label in small capitals in the category's colour, then its slices as chips with their words and counts ("journal article 9"), each pressed to toggle, picked ones filled as item 6 says. A press on a category's label picks all its slices, or, when all are picked, takes them all out. The line is one line at any width: the slices that do not fit fold, last in, first out, by `fit()` over their measured widths, into a button "+<n>" at the line's end that opens a PickMenu of them, headed by category (Z1001); the count and Clear all stay on the line. It ends with "16 projects" ("1 project" for one), or "5 of 16 shown" while a filter, a toggle or the search hides cards, and, while any facet value is picked, Clear all, which takes every facet value out, the slices included, and leaves the search and the toggles. The line of in-force chips under today's bar is not drawn: each pick shows on its group button or its slice.
8. **The section headings** (ruling 354 (b)): `FoldHead.svelte` moves to `src/lib/views/shared/card/FoldHead.svelte` (`src/lib/views/shared` holds ten items) and gains an optional `tone` (the title drawn in that hue) and `count` (a badge after the title); its glyph becomes optional; the three Focus users import it from there, drawn as before. The folded sections are saved per board, as a new field `folded` of `BoardState` (the category keys, the empty key for "other" kept), read by `parseBoardState` as a list of strings (a value that is not a text dropped) and not passed in `storedState`'s `lists`, which maps items to records. A board whose row says `cards: grid` draws its cards and its list with no heading and no folding, as one section (Z1004). The heading stays at the top of the pane's body while its section scrolls under it (`position: sticky; top: 0` on the heading, with the pane's background behind it). The header's Expand all and Collapse buttons, `bulk()` and their keys `board.expand_all` and `board.collapse` are removed; a card still opens and closes in place by its own control.
9. **The list view** (ruling 355 (a), a new partial `src/lib/views/boards/projects/BoardList.svelte`): the list is drawn under the same headings as the cards, one `Table` per section, folding as item 8 says; each section's table draws its own header row, not sticky, under the sticky section heading (Z1006).
