## What to build

Start each item by reproducing it in a browser spec on the served demo hub, seen failing, then fix it.

1. **Tooltips put away on a press.** A pointer press on a control hides its bubble and the focus that press gives does not show it again; a bubble opens on focus only when the focus came from the keyboard (`:focus-visible` on the control, read at `focusin`). Hover after the press shows it again after the delay as before. The rail's and the tab strip's bubbles are covered by the same change.
2. **Scrollbars in the theme.** One rule for every scrolling box: `scrollbar-width: thin` and `scrollbar-color` from two new tokens (`--scroll-thumb`, `--scroll-track`) given a value for every theme the way the other surface tokens get theirs (read `src/lib/themes/derive.ts` and `tokens.css` first), so no box draws a light bar on a dark theme; the rail list and the tab strip keep no bar.
3. **The rail list.** The list takes the height above the foot and the foot never covers an entry under any theme, rice included; when the list has more above or below what is shown, a fade at that edge says so, driven by the list's scroll position (state, not a DOM side effect), gone at the ends.
4. **The rail's foot labelled.** Every foot control draws its glyph with a short visible name under it (`stacked`): Search, Update, Theme, CRT, Wallpaper, Glyphs; the three text-size controls sit in one row under one name, Text, as smaller, reset and larger; text reset draws `note_back`, so no two foot controls share a glyph. The names are catalog lines. The foot's total height is measured before and after on the demo at 800 px window height and quoted in the report.
5. **The pane menu.** The per-page items go. In their place two items, "Move tab to page..." and "Show a page here...", each present only when it has at least one page to offer; picking one replaces the menu's list with the pages it offers (the same labels and filters `pageMoveItems` and `embedPageItems` compute today) and a first item Back that returns to the pane menu's own list. A layout of seven pages gives a pane menu of its fixed items plus two.
6. **Tabs keep their names.** A tab does not shrink below its label up to 16 characters; a longer label ends in an ellipsis with the whole name in the tab's tooltip; an overflowing strip scrolls with its existing controls.
7. **The palette.** Each result row draws its kind in the first column in words (View, Project, Collection, Feed, catalog lines) and the label button in the second, filling it; with no query the list shows the views only, and projects, collections and feeds join once a word is typed.
8. **The two theme defects.** Under a rice theme no rail entry sits under the foot (item 3 covers the layout; the spec runs under a rice theme too). Under the light theme the rail's glyph toggle draws its active state in the same tone as the CRT toggle; find why it is orange (quote the rule) and fix it at that rule.
