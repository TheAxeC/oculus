## What to build

1. **A deck for every one of the 95 research projects** (the step line; rulings 679 and 685). In the hub worktree, each research project's `present/` folder holds a `present.yml` and its slide file, written from the project's own sources alone:
   - its manuscript: the `.tex` file that is the paper, with the files it inputs (for the thesis, `thesis.tex` and its chapters), or, where the project holds the paper only as a PDF, that PDF, read whole through its pages; a cover letter, a supplement on its own, an archived or old draft (`archive/`, `admin/old/`), a derivation note and a reviewer response are not the manuscript;
   - its `README.md`, and a file the README or the project's `CLAUDE.md` names as holding the results (such as raw-pls's `RESULTS.md`);
   - its `project.yml`, every field;
   - its `CLAUDE.md`, for facts only.
   The 70 projects without a deck get one, and the 25 decks there are replaced the same way. The failed and stopped projects are written like every other: what was asked, what was done, what came out. A deck names no other project and no work of another project, except that a coauthor project's deck says the paper's authorship as its manifest gives it (`lead`, `outcome`), never as the user's lead. The decks of `projects/manuscripts/covert-theory` and `projects/manuscripts/covert-applied` never name or describe each other, though their own files name each other.
2. **Every slide right** (the step line; rulings "One slide per project preferred" and "Decks are internal"):
   - Each deck is one slide, or two where two read better, never more.
   - The first slide is `kind: project` and carries, for a project with a manuscript or a README, at least `title`, `hook`, `eli5` (`purpose` and `plain`), `method`, `results` and `headline`, and `sub`, `specs`, `mechanism` and `equation` where its sources give them; a project with neither carries what its manifest gives (`eli5`, `outcome`, `publication.abstract`, `results:`). `equation` is the project's defining formula where its manuscript or README states one, written so it parses: in a single-quoted YAML value or a plain block, never a double-quoted value holding a backslash. `tag` is set where the sources say what kind of evidence the result is, with `class` one of `measured`, `design`, `proof`, `mixed`.
   - A second slide, where one is used, is drawn as carefully as the first: a `project` slide with `panels`, or a `columns` or `equation` slide with a heading, never a plain list of leftovers. A fact the first slide has no room for goes on the second; no fact is dropped for room.
   - Figures: a figure on a slide is a png, jpg, jpeg, gif, webp or svg the project's own folder holds, copied into the `present/` folder with `cp` and named relative to it; a PDF or EPS figure is not used and nothing is converted. A figure already in a `present/` folder is never deleted: it stays in the folder, and is drawn only where it shows the project's current result.
   - Every number on a slide is the project's current number, taken in this order: the manuscript's; for a project without one, its README's or the results file it names; for a project with neither, its manifest's `results:`, `publication.abstract`, `outcome:` or `latest:`. Where two sources give different numbers, the slide takes the one this order gives and the report lists the disagreement with both places. Every framing is the project's current one, from the same sources.
   - Nothing is hidden: no slide leaves out a fact because it is confidential, unfinished or negative; the dead ends and the targets a project's README or `CLAUDE.md` names as failed are on its slides.
   - Every slide is titled: a heading, or a `title` the `project` kind draws.
   - Every slide fits its frame at 1280 by 720: no slide raises "Slide too tall". Write to fit (short lines, at most four items in a list, `specs` of at most four chips, one panel per slide), since the fit is measured on a drawn page ("The measured fit").
   - Every front-matter key is in the shape `docs/decks.md` gives it, since a key of the wrong shape is dropped with no finding.
   - Each deck reads with no finding of the deck reader, the portfolio deck too, and the hub's equation floor holds (at least 13 slides with an equation that typesets).
   - No line of slide text is written the same in two decks unless it is a fact true of both, each such line listed in the report.
3. **The facts strip drawn below the slide's text** (ruling "Facts strip smaller"). `src/lib/themes/slide.css` declares two slide sizes of the strip's own in `.slide-frame`, beside the other design sizes: `--slide-design-facts: 12px` for its values and `--slide-design-facts-label: 10px` for its labels, each times `--text-scale`; `body.rice .slide-frame` gives them as its 12/13 rule gives every slide size (11 px and 9 px), and the rice comment's list names them. `Facts.svelte` sets the strip's values and its labels from them in place of `--slide-fs-small` and `0.72em`. The dot before a hued value stays 9 px at the design size, times the text scale; the strip's other measures (the rule's room, the gaps) follow the strip's own type. The strip keeps the factor 1 and its room while hidden. Its head comment, its style comment at `:152-156`, `slide.css`'s comments and `Slide.svelte:33-34` are made true.

3. The current number is the manuscript's, then the README's or its results file's, then the manifest's, and a disagreement is reported, not settled in silence.
4. Under a rice theme the strip's sizes follow that theme's 12/13 rule (11 px and 9 px), as every other slide size does.
5. The dot keeps its 9 px at the design size, so a hued value stays as visible as today on a smaller strip.
6. A figure already in a `present/` folder stays on disk, since some exist nowhere else in their project.

- Deck text follows the rules file's prose rule: plain words, a capital first letter and a full stop for a sentence; ASCII only, except an accented letter in a name (such as the umlaut o of a Swedish place name in `hccnet-fl-sweden`), which stays as the name is written; LaTeX inside `$...$` or the `equation` key for a formula; a YAML value holding `:` or `#` quoted, and a value holding a backslash single-quoted or in a block.
