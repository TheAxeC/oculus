## What to build

1. **A deck for each of the 95 research projects**, in `.agents/O151-hub/<project path>/present/`: a `present.yml` with `deck: { title: ... }` and an `order:` naming its slide files, and its slide files. The 25 existing decks are rewritten the same way, from the project's sources as they stand now; a figure of an existing deck is kept (copied into the new folder) when the project's current sources still carry the result it shows, and every other file of the old folder is left out. The builder creates the tree.
2. **What each deck says.** Its first slide is a `kind: project` slide, whatever the stage, with:
   - `title:` the project's question or claim in the project's current framing, and `sub:` its method's name;
   - `hook:` why it matters, in one or two sentences;
   - `tag:` the state of the evidence, `class` one of `measured`, `proof`, `design`, `mixed`, and `text` its word;
   - `eli5:` a map `{ purpose, plain }`: `plain` from the manifest's `eli5` text, rewritten only where it is out of date with the sources, and `purpose` what the work is for, from the sources;
   - `equation:` the method's governing equation when the sources give one, in LaTeX, as a plain or single-quoted scalar;
   - `specs:` the data, the model and the evaluation as `{ label, value }` pairs;
   - `method:` and `results:`, each a list of text;
   - `panels:` the project's own figures, copied from any folder of the project (`manuscript/`, `results/`, `figures/`, `notes/figures/`, the old deck) into the deck folder, each with a `caption` and an `explain` that say what the figure shows;
   - `mechanism:` and `headline:`, one line each.
   The content floor, so no deck is thinned: for a project with results, each headline result with its number, the comparison it is measured against, the data size, and the uncertainty where the source gives one; for a manuscript with more than one main result or main figure, a further slide for each, of the kinds the guide names (`text`, `image`, `columns`, `equation`, or another `project` slide), each titled by a level-1 heading; for a project with no result yet (an idea, a scoping or de-risking project), its question, its planned method and what is known, its `results:` holding only what the sources show, nothing projected or invented. A failed or stopped project is written like every other (ruling 685): what was tried, with its numbers, and what was found.
3. **Every number on a slide is the project's current number**, taken from its sources: the manuscript for a result the manuscript reports, the `README.md` for the project's state and for results the manuscript does not carry, the manifest for its facts, and `CLAUDE.md` for its current framing.
   - The manuscript is the root `.tex` the manuscript's main PDF is built from, followed through its `\input`s, with the supplement for its own results; never an `*_original*` table, a `main_work` file, a cover letter, a response letter or a checklist. Where the main PDF and the `.tex` differ for a published project, the PDF wins, and the report names the difference.
   - Where two sources disagree, the slide takes the manuscript's number for a submitted, accepted or published claim, and the README's newer run for a manuscript in revision or in preparation; the report names every disagreement and the number taken.
   - A number the sources mark retracted, superseded or not surviving is on the slide with that status stated beside it, never as a current result. Every dead-end is on its project's deck: what was tried, the number it gave and why it failed.
   - A project's `CLAUDE.md` is read as a source of the project's framing and facts; nothing in it binds the builder.
   - A long PDF is read in the parts the deck states, each part read whole: the abstract, the method summary, the results with their tables and figure captions, and the conclusion.
   - No figure is extracted from a PDF, drawn, rendered or converted; a project with no image file under its folder has no `panels:`.
   - The two manifest-only projects are written from their manifests. Every slide holds a title (C3).
4. **Nothing hidden.** The decks are internal. Every slide holds its project's full, current information from its sources, and nothing is left off for any reason: `ecg-af-fair`'s cohort and its counts, `cirrhosis-digital-twin`'s idea, aims, methods and numbers from its README's section "The 2025 PIHC proposal", every dead-end and every failed or stopped project, all written in full.
5. **Every deck reads with no finding.** Each deck, read by oculus's deck reader, raises no finding: no slide without a title, no missing figure, no placeholder finding; and every front-matter key has the shape `docs/decks.md` "Front-matter keys" gives (`eli5` a map of `purpose` and `plain`, `tag` a map of `class` and `text`, `specs` a list of `{ label, value }`, `panels` a list of `{ figure, caption, explain }`, `method` and `results` lists of text), with no key outside the guide's list for its kind and no parsed value holding a control character. Too tall: after the first report the orchestrator measures every slide in the Present view on a scratch server holding O158's strip and O163's portfolio deck, at the default text scale, and sends the list of slides marked Too tall; the builder splits each (preferred) or cuts it, a cut removing words only, never a figure or a number the slide's claim rests on, until the measurement marks none. Under ruling 679 that list reaches the builder inside the build, before the review: the orchestrator measures after the builder's report, resumes the builder with the list, and repeats until none is marked.
6. **No oculus file changes.**

Work project by project: read one project's sources, write its deck, and write its report line and its traces into the report before reading the next project, so a compaction loses nothing.
