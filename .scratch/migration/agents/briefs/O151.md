## What to build

1. **A deck for each of the 70 projects.** `<project>/present/present.yml` with `deck.title` a one-line headline of the project's point, as the 25 decks have, and `order: [main.md]` (with more files when item 3 splits), and `<project>/present/main.md`.
   - A project at `nascent` or `scoping` (the stages the vocabulary flags `idea: true`) gets a `kind: idea` slide whose `desc` is one or two sentences saying what the project is and what it would show, from its manifest (`eli5`, `next`, `title`) and its `README.md` where one exists. A project at `exploring` or `de-risking` whose sources hold no method and no result gets the same.
   - Every other project gets a `kind: project` slide: `tag` (class by the evidence the sources give: `measured`, `design`, `proof` or `mixed`, its text the class's word), `title`, `sub` (who leads it and on what data), `hook`, `eli5` where the manifest gives one, `specs`, `method`, `results`, `mechanism`, `headline`, `equation` only where the manuscript itself displays the model's equation, copied as LaTeX, and `panels` with up to two figures. A failed or stopped project's slide states what was built and what was measured, as the sources say, like every other (ruling 685); its stage shows in the strip as it is (ruling 684). A project whose sources are only the manifest gets `title`, `sub`, `hook` from `eli5` or `outcome`, and `results` only from the manifest's own `results`.
   - Sources: a number or a claim is taken from the project's manuscript where one exists (`manuscript/main.tex`, or `manuscript/main.pdf` quoted by page), and from its `README.md` or manifest only where no manuscript holds the fact; a field the sources do not fill is left out, never filled by guess. Every number keeps its interval and qualifier as the source states it.
   - A coauthor project's slide names the lead in `sub` from the manifest's `lead` and Axel's role as the manifest's `outcome` gives it; the slide never presents the lead's work as Axel's. Nothing a source marks confidential or another person's unpublished proposal reaches a slide.
   - A figure is a PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP that the project's manuscript draws (named by an `\includegraphics`, or a figure of the PDF that exists as a raster beside it) or that its `results/` holds; it is referenced in place by its path relative to `present/`, never copied; a PDF is never named in `figure:`; a project with no such figure has no panels.
   - Every slide has a title of its own: a project slide its `title:`, an idea slide its project's name.
   - The front matter uses only keys the slide's kind reads.
   - The text follows the rules file's prose rule and character set; the existing decks' `<b>`, `<i>` and `$...$` markup is allowed, as the reader typesets it, and an accented letter in a name stays as the source writes it.
2. **The 25 decks.** Every slide that the reader marks untitled is given a title of its own. The `extra:` block of `btfno` and `covert-applied`, read by nothing, becomes the slide's `notes:` (its label and body joined), so its words stay and are read; the slide keeps every other key.
3. **Too-tall slides.** Every slide of the 95 decks that the Present view marks Too tall, at text scale 1 in the default theme, is split into several slides of the same kind (a second slide file, each slide titled, `order:` naming the files) or cut, until none is too tall. A cut removes only repeated words; every number keeps its interval and qualifier, or the number goes with them, and what is left stays true.
4. **Read with no finding.** Each of the 95 decks, opened in the Present view of the scratch oculus on the refreshed hub copy, shows no finding: no untitled slide, no Too tall slide, no missing figure. A finding of the portfolio deck's own files (`deck/`) is O150's or its open item's, named in the report and never fixed by O151.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. A deck is one `main.md` with one slide, in the shape of the 25 decks, split into more slides only where the slide is too tall.
2. The kind follows the vocabulary: `idea` for the stages flagged `idea: true`, and for an `exploring` or `de-risking` project with no method or result yet; `project` for every other stage, the failed and stopped ones included.
3. Figures are referenced in place, not copied, so the hub holds no second copy of a binary and a moved figure is a missing-figure finding; at most two a slide.
4. A field the sources do not fill is left out rather than written by inference.
5. The fit is measured at text scale 1 in the default theme, the scale the hub's workspace file leaves; a reader's larger scale may draw a slide Too tall.
6. `deck.title` is a headline of the project's point, as on the 25 decks.
