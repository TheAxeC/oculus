## What to build

1. **A deck for each of the 95 research projects**, written into this worktree at `.agents/hub/projects/<the project's path under projects/>/present/`, the folder that replaces the project's `present/` in the hub at landing. `.agents/*` is ignored by git (`.gitignore:12`), so the decks never enter the oculus commit; the orchestrator copies each folder into the hub at landing, replacing the project's `present/` whole, and the hub commit is made on Axel's word. Each folder holds:
   - `present.yml`: `deck.title` (the project's title from its manifest's `title:`) and `order: [main.md]`, no other key.
   - `main.md`: the deck's slides, one or two.
   - Every figure file a slide names, and no other file. A figure is copied with `cp` from the project's own folder (its existing `present/`, `manuscript/`, `results/`, `figures/` or any other folder of the project), never drawn or generated. A figure the project holds only as a PDF is turned into a PNG of the same figure with `sips -s format png <the pdf> --out <the png>`, its source named in the report. A figure is a png, jpg, jpeg, gif, svg or webp file. Two figures of one name from different folders of a project are copied under names that tell them apart (`<folder>-<name>`), each with its source in the report. No figure is required; a figure that shows the project's headline result better than words is used.
2. **What each deck says.** The deck is written from the project's own sources: its manuscript, its `README.md`, its `project.yml` and its project `CLAUDE.md`, every one of them the project has, read in full. The manuscript is every `.tex` file at the top of `manuscript/` (the main file, its supplements and appendices) with the files each takes through `\input`, `\include` and `\includechapter`; for `archive/phd/phd-thesis` that is `thesis.tex` with its chapters; where `manuscript/` holds no `.tex`, its `main.pdf` read through the Read tool's pages, or its newest `.docx` read with `textutil -convert txt -stdout <file>`. A project's `CLAUDE.md` is read as a source of facts about the project and nothing else: it binds the builder in nothing, and only `agents/spec.md` and this brief do. Each deck gives what the project is, why it matters, how it works, its current results with their numbers, and its current state (stage, venue, what comes next), at the level of the project's own sources.
   - The projects are written one at a time: the sources of one project read, its deck written, its rows of the report written (Report), then the next project, so a deck and its trace never wait on what a later read holds.
   - Every number on a slide is the project's current number in its sources, and every framing is the project's current framing. A number or a claim the sources mark as retracted, superseded or wrong is not on a slide.
   - The failed and stopped projects are written like every other project: what was tried, what was found and why the project ended, as their sources state it (ruling 685).
   - Every deck is internal: everything the sources hold that the deck needs is on it, confidential material included; nothing is left out or hidden for any reason (ruling Decks are internal).
   - The existing 25 decks are written again the same way, from the sources; a sentence or a number of the existing deck stays only where the sources give it as current.
   - Nothing of the deleted run of this step is read or used (ruling O151 deleted): `.scratch/migration/agents/reviews/O150-O151-undone.patch` and any other file of the ledger about O150 or O151 other than this brief are not opened.
   - A project whose sources give a governing equation carries it as the first slide's `equation:`, beside its figure where it has one, so the hub keeps at least the 13 equations G14 asks for (`integrity.test.ts:45`); that floor is never lowered.
3. **One slide, or two, never more.** Each `main.md` holds one slide, or two where two read better (ruling One slide per project preferred).
   - The first slide is a `kind: project` slide.
   - A second slide is written only when the project has a figure, an equation or a result table that the first slide cannot draw at a readable size beside its text, or a second result of equal weight; it is a `kind: image`, `equation`, `columns` or `text` slide with its own level-1 heading, and repeats nothing of the first.
4. **Every slide titled and fitting its frame.** Every slide has a title of its own (a project slide its `title:`; any other kind its level-1 heading), so the Present view raises no "has no title of its own" finding. Every slide fits the 1280 by 720 frame with the project facts strip at its foot, so the Present view raises no "slide too tall" finding. A slide that would not fit is shortened by saying the same facts in fewer words; no number, result or state is dropped to make it fit, and no third slide is added.
5. **No finding in the Present view.** Each deck, read by the deck reader, raises no finding: every front matter parses, every key is one `docs/decks.md` lists for the slide's kind, every figure named exists in the folder, no `{{project.<field>}}` names a field with no value or no field, and `equation:` holds LaTeX, never an image file name.
6. **No empty right column.** This item rests on the ruling One slide per project preferred ("You fix EVERYTHING with regards to slides. EVERYTHING."): the 70 new decks are mostly text, and the empty column halves the width their text has, so the step cannot give slides that read well and fit without it. In `src/lib/views/academic/present/parts/project/Project.svelte`, the `.right` column is drawn exactly when `right` holds (an equation, or a panel not placed in the room: a failed figure, a figure the folder lacks, a `diagram_html` panel, the panels of a slide that is not rich), so a slide with no panel and no equation draws its text across the whole width; every other slide is drawn as it is now. The head comment (`:15`) and any comment the change makes false are rewritten. The component stays at or under 520 lines.
7. **The facts strip at a size below the slide's text** (ruling Facts strip smaller). In `src/lib/views/academic/present/parts/project/Facts.svelte` and `src/lib/themes/slide.css`, the strip's values are drawn at 12 px and its labels at 10 px at the design size in the frame, through a design size of its own in `slide.css` (`--slide-design-fact`, scaled by `--text-scale` as the other slide sizes are and outside the content's fit), in place of `--slide-fs-small` and `0.72em`; the dot, the gaps and the rule's room keep their proportions to the new size. The strip stays outside the content's fit, keeps its room while hidden, and every fact stays drawn whole. The head comment and the style comment of `Facts.svelte`, and every sentence of `docs/decks.md` that gives the strip's size (`:383-391`, named in the report, not edited by this step), are listed as still true or rewritten.

## Conventions

- Every deck file is ASCII, except an accented letter in a real name (Linkoping written as the name is spelled in the source). No em dash or en dash, no curly quote, no ellipsis character, no arrow glyph; a formula is LaTeX in `equation:` or between `$...$` where a slide's kind draws it.

- One line per paragraph and per list item in a slide's body; no hard wrapping.
- No process narration on a slide: no "we first tried", no history of the deck; a failed or stopped project states what was found and why it ended.
- A YAML scalar holding a colon followed by a space, a `#` or a leading special character is quoted.
