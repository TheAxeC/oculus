## What to build

1. **The three states of a list that may be empty.** In the list branch of `FieldControl.svelte`, for a field whose spec has `empty` set and a `control` of `list`:
   - key absent (`value === undefined`): `InlineItems` draws the words `settings.unset` above the Add;
   - held empty (`Array.isArray(value)` and no item): it draws the words `settings.list_empty` above the Add, and beside them a button labelled `settings.list_clear` that calls `onpick(undefined)`, which takes the key out;
   - one or more items: drawn as on the tree, with no note and no button.

   A list field whose spec has no `empty`, and a `parked` field, are drawn exactly as on the tree. `InlineItems` still holds nothing: the note and the clear action come in as optional props.
2. **The words.** In `config/text/views/system/workbench.yml`, under the `settings` words beside `unset`: `list_empty: Empty list` and `list_clear: Clear`.
3. **The comments that say what `empty` does.** The doc comment at `catalog/options.ts:83-85` and the head comments of `FieldControl.svelte` and `InlineItems.svelte` say what `empty` does on a list. Where `config/options/appearance.yml`'s comment block describes `empty`, it says the same.

Implied, read in the diff: the Clear button is what calls `onpick(undefined)`, and removing the last item hands `onpick([])`, which is the held empty state, so an emptied list shows Clear.
