## What to build

1. **The keys.** A side project's manifest accepts `deadline`, `subtasks`, `followup`, `snooze`, `priority`, `pin`, `short`, `latest`, `collaborators`, `waiting-on` and `related`, besides its five keys, each with the research manifest's name and shape. `normalise-personal.ts` reads them with the functions `project()` uses (`str`, `strings`, `followups`, `subtasks`, `pin === true`), moved or exported so both readers import one copy; `SideProject` gains `short`, `pin`, `priority`, `latest`, `deadline`, `waitingOn`, `followups`, `snooze`, `subtasks`, `relatedIds` and `collaborators`, named as on `Project`. A value of the wrong type for one of them costs that field alone, with one `field_type` finding, as the five keys do; a key outside the sixteen is still a `key` finding. `side_project_schema` gains a row per key with the type, the `from:` and the help text of its `manifest_schema` row (the `followup` group as its two dotted rows), so the YAML editor completes, explains and checks them in both manifest kinds.

2. **Focus.** A side project whose status is not `parked` (decision 2) is read by the ticker beside the research projects: its `deadline` gives a line in a new deadline group `side` (heading "side projects", the Personal view's glyph `spark`), after `task` and before `conference`, overdue lines joining the overdue group as a paper's do; its due `followup` notes join the follow-ups; its open `subtasks` join the subtasks and the tasks, grouped under the side project's label; `snooze` keeps its lines off as a project's does. A line's label is the `short` or the title, as `focusLabel` makes it. A press on a line's name opens the side project's manifest in a source tab, as the Personal card's title does. Its `waiting-on` values count in the Focus waiting tally beside the research projects'. A parked side project draws no line.

3. **The buttons on a side project's lines.** Snooze, wake, tick, reopen, delete and clear write the side project's manifest as they write a research project's: `writeManifest` and `writeSubtask` take a side project's id (`side:<slug>`, `root:<key>`), and the server finds its file through `readPersonal`, never `readManifests`, writing only a file `readPersonal` found (a `project.yml` under the hub's side-projects tier, or `.oculus/project.yml` at a served root), with the field names of `side_project_schema`. A field outside it is refused as a research write refuses one outside `manifest_schema`. Each write is logged and undone as a research write is (G9). The request and its answer keep their shape (decision 3).

4. **The Personal card.** A pinned card (`pin: true`) is drawn first within its status, with the pin glyph the project card's menu names (`project.card.pin_glyph`); `priority` draws the flames the Focus lane card draws for it (`views/boards/focus/lanes/LaneCard.svelte`, `flames`); `latest` is drawn under the explanation; `waiting-on` draws as the project card's waiting line (`ProjectCard.svelte:113`, `:358`, `waitingLine`); `collaborators` and `related` draw as the project card's links (`CardLinks.svelte`), a related research project opening its project tab. The card reuses those components and helpers where their props take what a side project holds, and otherwise draws with the same classes and words. The card's words stay edited in its manifest, as today (`Personal.svelte:89`).

5. **The ticker rows.** In `Ticker.svelte` a row is at `--fs-xs`, the name at weight 400, on an 18 px row (`calc(18px * var(--text-scale, 1))`) and a 14 px line (`calc(14px * var(--text-scale, 1))`); `.plain` and `.tail.toned` drop to 400 with the name. A row's button keeps its words, its border and its 6 px sides and takes `padding: 1px 6px`, so it is 18 px high and the row stays 18 px when it shows (decision 4). No heading of Focus, of a band or of a group changes (`focus.yml`'s group headings, the band titles and their CSS). The head comment of `.act` and of the row is made true.

6. **The text.** The heading `focus.command.dlcat_side` ("side projects") in `focus.yml`. `normalise-personal.ts`'s head comment, `readPersonal`'s, the Personal board's and `side-project-schema.yml`'s head comment say what the manifest now holds. `docs/views.md`'s Personal and Focus sections and `HELP.md`'s Personal row say that a side project takes the research keys and shows on Focus. `DESIGN.md` names side projects among what the ticker reads where it lists what Focus reads. Each sentence that names the five keys as the whole manifest is made true (`grep -rn "five keys\|holds five" src config docs DESIGN.md HELP.md --exclude=local.yml`).

7. **Sizes.** A source file at or under 880 lines, a component at or under 520; `ticker.ts` (638) takes the side lines in a function of its own or a new file in a folder with room; `state/boards` and `server/edit/writes` are full, so a new file goes into a subfolder or a folder with room. ## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. A side project's deadlines form a group of their own, `side`, headed "side projects" with the Personal view's glyph `spark` (Z746): a side project's date is a different job from a paper's submission, a grant's or a task entry's, which is why the ticker groups by kind. Putting them under `paper` or `task` is the lazy option and misnames them.
2. A parked side project draws no line on Focus (Z746), as a research project that is not live draws none; `archive_dead` already counts `parked` as not live. Its manifest keeps its keys, and the Personal board still draws it.
3. A side project's write goes through the existing `writeManifest` and `writeSubtask` requests, its id telling the server to look it up through `readPersonal` (Z746): the request and answer keep their shape, and the server's two readers stay apart, as `readPersonal`'s comment requires. A new write kind for side manifests is a second request with the same fields.
4. A row's button takes `padding: 1px 6px`, 18 px high (Z746), so the row stays 18 px when its buttons show and the board does not move; a 22 px button on an 18 px row would make every row with a shown button 22 px, which the plan line's 18 px row rules out.
5. The Personal card draws the six keys as oculus draws them for a research project (Z746): `pin` sorts first with its glyph, `priority` the lane card's flames, `latest`, `waiting-on`, `collaborators` and `related` as on the project card. Reading the keys into the snapshot and drawing none of them is the lazy option: a key the reader writes and never sees.
6. `short` labels a side project on Focus as it labels a research project; the Personal card keeps the title.
