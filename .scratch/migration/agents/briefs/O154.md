## What to build

The Present view's filter is the project board's filter: the same bar, drawn by the same component, computed by the same functions, saved in the same state shape, over the projects whose decks the deck's groups of project decks hold. Nothing of O147's own filter is left.

1. **One computation for both.** The chain the Projects board runs in its script (`offersMine`, `facets`, `sliceGroups`, `parseBoardState`, the quick filters, `appliedFilters`, the counted facets, `filterGroups`, `applyFilters`) becomes one pure function in `src/lib/state/boards/board/filter-menu.ts` (the cycle-free file; no new file in the folder), exported through `index.ts`, given the board items, the preset, whether a person is marked as the user, the hubs of the Hub group, the raw saved state and the stale lag, and answering what the bar and the board read (`held`, whether Mine only applies, the slices, the counted facets, the groups, the quick filters and the items shown). `Projects.svelte` reads its filter through it and computes none of it itself. Its behaviour on every board and on the Awaiting box's tab is unchanged.
2. **One bar for both.** The board's two bar snippets (`toggles` and `bar`, `Projects.svelte:374-430`) become one shared component in `src/lib/views/shared/forms/bar/`, drawing `FilterBar` with the search, the stale, deadline and waiting toggles, Mine only, the Role group, the groups of `LINE_FACETS` with the rest in More, the Hub group by the board's rule, the blocked and venue badges, and `SliceLine` with the slices by category, the shown count in `shownWords` and Clear all, in the board's texts. It is handed the function's answer, the board stats of the same items (the `boardStats` selector of `state/client/selectors.svelte.ts`), the search placeholder and one `write(next: Partial<BoardState>)`, and every control, prop and text it draws is the same for both callers. `Projects.svelte` draws it in place of its snippets; the Present view draws it under its toolbar.
3. **The Present pane's state.** The pane saves the bar's fields under the board's keys at the top of its instance state, beside `slide`, `overview`, `recent` and `facts`: `search`, `stale`, `dated`, `waiting`, `mine` and `filters` (the slice picks under `slice`, as on the board). `PresentState` and `parsePresentState` hold these fields, so every write of the pane's state (`Present.svelte`'s `write`, `screen/box.ts`, `acts/presentation.svelte.ts:68`, the Overview, the facts toggle) keeps them. They are read by `parseBoardState`'s rules, through the function of item 1, so Mine only is on until the reader turns it off, and a saved value the board would drop (a facet outside `FACETS`, a slice no member's category holds, a `compute` value no member offers) is dropped. A saved O147 pick under `tier` or `pillar` is read as the board reads it; one under the Map's Stage id (`STAGE_FACET`) is dropped as any key outside `FACETS` is. `leftDeck` sets every bar field to `emptyBoardState`'s value when the pane opens another deck. No pick, search or toggle writes a file.
4. **The projects it runs over.** The items are the board items of `mapPreset(configMap(), vocabOf)` that are member projects of the deck's groups of project decks: a member's project matched by its key (`DeckMember.projectId`) to a board item of the deck's own hub (`deck.hub`), so a project of another hub with the same key is never one. The members are every member of every answer of the deck, kept or not, so the bar never runs over a list a pick has already narrowed. The counts on the toggles, the badges and the shown count are over those members before any filter, as the board's are over its projects. A member that matches no board item (a deck in a group of project decks that is no project's deck, a project the snapshot lacks) is not counted, as a board counts projects only. A deck with no group of project decks draws no bar and keeps no room for one.
5. **The kept list.** The deck is read with the keys of the members the bar shows, each once and sorted, so one state asks under one query key; it is `null` (the whole deck, a deck that is no project's drawn) when the bar shows every matched member, and under any narrowing the server's rule drops a deck that is no project's, as on the base. A list that keeps no member is `[]`, which empties every group of project decks. The pane, the Overview, the presenter screen, the audience window and the PDF draw the deck read under that list. `keepFor(instance, ref)` gives the list the pane's own `keep` gives, whether the pane is drawn or not: the members it reads from are kept by the Present store for a pane while it presents or exports, so the audience window and the print page get the pane's list after its tab is hidden; while the members or the snapshot are not yet known it is `undefined` (asks nothing, never `null`), and the print page's 5 s wait and its `present.print_unanswered` stand. While the members are not yet known, no window draws a deck the saved controls would narrow: a pane opened on a deck whose saved state narrows it shows its loading state until the narrowed answer is in, and never the whole deck as the filtered one; that wait on each open is accepted, since Mine only is on by default. A change of the controls keeps the rule of O147's acts: the pane goes on drawing the answer it drew until the new one is in, stays on the slide on screen when the new deck holds it, and goes to its first slide when the filter took it out.
6. **What goes.** From `present.ts`: `FILTER_FACETS`, `FILTER_GROUPS`, `FILTER_LAYOUT`, `filtersOf`, `pickedIn`, `filterGroupsOf`, `keptKeys`, `deckTally`, `DeckFilters` and `filtering`, and any other part that only they used; the `STAGE_FACET` import goes with them, and `STAGE_FACET` becomes local to `map-bar.ts` when no other file imports it. From `collection/bar.ts`: `projectFilter` and `projectKeeps`, their head-comment sentences (`:31-34`), and `ProjectLayout` and any helper only they used (each kept only where the collection's own bar still calls it, `grep` proves which). From `Bar.svelte`: the "Filtered" line, its Clear button, their CSS and the `FilterLine` prop shape; the toolbar holds the deck's controls only. From `present.yml`: `filter`, `filter_clear`, `filter_clear_tip`, `filtered`, `filtered_one`. Every behaviour in which the Present view's filter differs from the board's is gone.
7. **The comments and the docs.** The head comments of `present.ts` (`:13-18`), `filter.svelte.ts` (`:1-19`), `Present.svelte` (`:31-34`), `Bar.svelte` (`:2-10`) and `Projects.svelte` (`:60-71`, `:89-97`), the head comments of `FilterBar.svelte` (`:2-16`, its callers and the sentence that the Present view gives no search and no Mine only) and `SliceLine.svelte` (`:2-4`, its callers), and the comments of `map-bar.ts:16` and `:40` say what each now does. `docs/templates.md:1914` says the Present view draws the Projects board's filter and its picks write nothing to `present.yml`. The heading "The filter" of `docs/decks.md` keeps its words, so the link at `:131` stays. `docs/decks.md` "The filter" (`:375-380`) and the Present bullet of `docs/HELP.md:331` say: a deck whose groups hold project decks draws the Projects board's filter under the toolbar, the same bar and rules over the projects whose decks those groups hold; what it narrows (kept from the current text, which stays true); the shown count and Clear all of the slices line in place of the "Filtered" line and Clear; Mine only on by default where it applies; and that the picks are the pane's, kept with its page, dropped when it opens another deck, and write no file. Every other sentence in `src`, `docs`, `README.md` and `config` that names the Present filter's own groups (Stage, Status, Tier, Pillar and Form) or the "Filtered" line is rewritten (grep `Filtered`, `Pillar and Form`, `FILTER_GROUPS`, `keptKeys`, `projectFilter`).

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The shared computation is a pure function in `filter-menu.ts` and the shared bar a component in `views/shared/forms/bar/`, so the board and the Present view cannot drift: the Present view draws the board's bar by drawing the board's own code, not a copy. The builder names both and says so in the report.
2. The pane's state shape is the board's bar fields (`search`, `stale`, `dated`, `waiting`, `mine`, `filters`), saved under the board's own keys at the top of its instance state rather than under a nested key, so `parseBoardState` reads them unchanged. The board's card fields (`mode`, `more`, `labels`, `folded`, `columns`) are left out, since the Present view draws no cards.
3. Mine only is on by default on the Present view as on every board, so a deck one of whose members is not the user's opens narrowed to the user's projects. This follows the ruling "exactly the same filter" (ruling Board filter on decks); the report's before and after names it.
4. The kept list is `null` whenever the bar shows every member, so a state that narrows nothing asks for the whole deck under one query key.
5. A saved O147 pick is read by the board's parser and nothing else, with no code that carries the old form.
6. No test is added: the function of item 1 composes functions G124's tests cover, a wrong filter costs no lost work, and views have no entry (`guarded.md:3`). G124's test files are run since the step changes its code. A builder who finds a G124 unit's behaviour changed reports it.
