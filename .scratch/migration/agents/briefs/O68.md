2. The career file and its model (rulings 293, 301, open item 319; Z982's `goal`).
   - The career file holds `title`, `goal` (one text), `milestone` (one text), `milestone_date` (a day `YYYY-MM-DD`), `situation`, `order`, `order_note` and `schema`.    - The flag `obtained` is added to `awarded` and `granted` in `config/catalogs/vocab.yml` `statuses:`, described in the comment above `statuses:` with the other flags, in the words "the status is funding obtained, which the career card's track record counts; compared trimmed and in lower case (`wordFlag`)", and accepted by the `statuses` shape in `vocab.ts` as the other status flags are, with its help sentence in the shape's doc.
