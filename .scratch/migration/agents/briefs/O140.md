## What to build

1. In `config/catalogs/vocab.yml`, `interview` (:155) and the Interview row of `position_stages` (:205) take `color: "#ec4899"` in place of `"#7c3aed"`, their other keys and the column alignment of the lines kept. No other line changes.
2. In each of the 14 blocks listed for pink, `--c-pink` gets a pink of its own. In each of the 17 blocks listed for teal, `--c-teal` gets a teal of its own. After the change, in every one of the 49 themes:
   - `--c-pink` is at a CIEDE2000 of 10 or more from every status token (`--c-red`, `--c-amber`, `--c-brass`, `--c-orange`, `--c-green`, `--c-teal`, `--c-cyan`, `--c-sky`, `--c-blue`, `--c-violet`, `--c-grey`, `--c-slate`), on the hue and on the derived `-txt`;
   - `--c-teal` is at 10 or more from every other status token, `--c-pink` included, on the hue and on the derived `-txt`.
   - Each new value is a pink or a teal in the theme's own tone: light on a dark ground, darker on a light one, and close to the theme's own hues in saturation.
   - Every pair of `--c-<hue>-lane` headers that is at 10 or more on the base, with `teal-lane` or `pink-lane` as one side, stays at 10 or more, so the `focus_lanes` comment stays true.
   - No other token of any block changes.
3. The head comment of each file whose hue changes gains a clause naming where the added hues come from: their source when one is taken from another palette or system, as `singles.css:4-6` does for Oxocarbon, or that the hue is oculus's own, chosen to read apart from the theme's other status hues. The comment is rewrapped as a whole paragraph at 100 columns.
4. The case-run program. Ruling 666 approves one program in the builder's scratch folder, run with `npx tsx` from the worktree root, which reads the theme files and writes no file of the worktree. It resolves the 49 themes of `config/defaults/appearance.yml` through `parseBlocks`, `resolveTheme` and `deriveTokens`, and prints, with culori's `differenceCiede2000`:

   The report quotes its source and both runs.

If a block cannot meet item 2's bars together, hand it back with the run before writing it: do not loosen a bar.
