## What to build

1. **Tile work takes turns.** A small module beside `Slide.svelte` under `src/lib/views/academic/present/screen/` makes a queue from its inputs: the frame source, the clock and the time budget. It holds no module-level mutable state (`agents/spec.md`, No globals). `Overview.svelte` makes one queue for itself and hands it to its tiles through Svelte context, so `Group.svelte` does not change. The queue runs waiting jobs in the order they were asked, a time budget's worth per animation frame, and yields between frames, so input and painting go on while tiles arrive. At least one job runs per frame, so a slow job cannot stall the queue. While the tab is hidden the frames stop, and the queue goes on when it is shown, losing no job. A job that throws has its error reported on the console, and the rest run.
2. **Only tiles use it.** A prop on `Slide.svelte`, passed through `Framed.svelte` and set by `Tile.svelte` alone, sends every layout of that slide through the queue: the first one, and those from a font load, a probe change (text size, theme) and an image load or failure. A slide with no such prop lays out exactly as it does now, so the Present pane, the presenter screen, the audience window and the print page and its `onlaid` are unchanged.
3. **A tile's slide is mounted at its turn.** `Tile.svelte` draws its shape, place, title, menu and drag at once, and mounts `Framed` through a job of the queue, flushed inside the job so that its cost counts against the budget. The tiles' first draw is then only their empty shapes.
4. **A layout stays inside its tile.** `.shot` in `Tile.svelte` takes CSS containment (`contain: layout paint style`), so that the forced layouts of one slide do not lay out the rest of the page.
5. **A slide waiting its turn is drawn as it is now before layout:** its content hidden by `.waiting`, the tile's shape, place, title, menu and drag all there and working. A press on such a tile shows the slide.
6. **A tile not yet laid out writes no measure.** `Framed.svelte` reports a tile's measure only once its slide has been laid out (`onlaid`), and reports the latest one at that moment. So a waiting tile carries no Too tall mark and no Findings row, and a tile destroyed while waiting leaves none. A slide with no queue prop reports as it does now.
7. **Repeats collapse.** A slide already waiting is not queued twice; a font load arriving while tiles wait costs each of them one layout.
8. **A queue entry that goes away is dropped.** A tile destroyed before its turn (the Overview closed, another deck chosen, the filter narrowed, a tile moved) runs nothing and leaves nothing waiting. Reopening the Overview starts a new set.
9. **Every tile is still drawn and measured.** No tile is skipped for being off screen, so the Too tall mark and the Findings view hold the same slides as before, once the queue has emptied.
10. **The measure's store writes stay cheap.** `SvelteMap.get` of an absent key subscribes to the map's version (`node_modules/svelte/src/reactivity/map.js:131-153`), and `set` of a new key or `delete` of a present key raises the version, so every tile with no mark re-derives whenever any tile gains or loses one. `tooTall` is changed so that one tile's mark does not re-run the other tiles. The report says what changed.
11. **Comments.** The head comments of `Slide.svelte`, `Framed.svelte`, `Tile.svelte` and `Overview.svelte` say what the code now does.

## Cases

No test is kept: a slow Overview loses no work, breaks no installation and accepts no wrong configuration. C1 to C6 are quoted runs of the queue module through a case-run program in `.agents/scratch/`, removed before the report, with a fake frame source and a fake clock. C7 to C10 are the orchestrator's look.

- C1 Ten jobs of 1 ms with a budget of 4 ms: at most 4 or 5 run per frame and all ten run in order.
- C2 One job of 50 ms ahead of others: it runs alone in its frame, and the next frame goes on.
- C3 A job asked twice while waiting: it runs once.
- C4 A job cancelled while waiting: it does not run, and the jobs behind it do.
- C5 A job that asks again while it runs: it runs once more, later, and the queue ends.
- C6 A job that throws: the error reaches the console and the rest still run.
- C7 The portfolio deck of the real hub in the Overview, 110 tiles: tiles arrive top to bottom, scrolling and pressing a tile work while they arrive, no long task over about 100 ms apart from one for the first draw of the empty tiles, which stays under about 500 ms, and a reload with the Overview open does the same. A single layout over about 100 ms is a finding. Measured with a `PerformanceObserver` for long tasks.
- C8 While tiles wait, a waiting tile shows no Too tall mark and adds no Findings row. After the queue has emptied: every tile shows its slide, and a slide known too tall on a scratch deck carries the mark and its Findings row.
- C9 Switching the theme with the Overview open: the page keeps answering and every tile ends laid out in the new theme.
- C10 Choosing another deck while tiles are arriving: the first deck's tiles stop and the new deck's arrive.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The time budget per frame is the builder's constant, about 8 ms, named and commented.
2. Jobs run in the order they were asked, which is the deck's order at the first mount, top first; a job asked again goes to the back, and a tile that comes on screen gets no priority, which keeps every tile measured with one rule.
3. The module's file name is `layout-turns.ts`.
4. There is one queue per Overview: two Overviews in two panes have two queues, each dropped with its Overview, and at about 8 ms each they stay inside one frame.
