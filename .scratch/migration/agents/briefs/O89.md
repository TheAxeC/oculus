## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. **Where the record card lives.** `views/shared/card/record/`, with `CardActions.svelte` moved in, so `views/shared/card` stays at 10 items; `card-menu.ts` becomes `record-menu.ts` there, since every kind's menu is built by it. The registry helper is renamed so "record card" names one thing.

The thread has no Deadline.
3. **The edit form of a line.** Ruling 453 names the add form; the form that edits an existing follow-up or subtask in its row stays the compact one-line form of the base.
4. **The deadline form's heading.** "New deadline" for a record with none and "Change deadline" for one with a deadline, since a record holds one deadline and "New" would read as a second.
5. **The strip of each kind** is item 3's list: the actions a reader of that view presses most, at most three, every action also in the menu.
6. **The project card's related projects** move from its Related line of `CardLinks` to the foot strip, since ruling 456 makes the strip the foot of every kind that lists projects; the rest of its body draws as on the base.
7. **Quick add's records of a collection** are all its entries, by display title; Project keeps the live projects the board hands it now.
