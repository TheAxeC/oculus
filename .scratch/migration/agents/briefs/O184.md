## What to build

1. **The switches at the bar's size** (ruling Open item 754 (a), `plan.md` Rulings). In `src/lib/views/sessions/terminal/bar/ShowSwitch.svelte`, Live and Transcript are `compact` icon buttons with their glyphs (`terminal`, `chat`), as the font buttons beside them are, so each is 20px high and the bar is 33px with and without them. Each keeps `active` and `pressed` for the one in force, Live keeps `disabled={!live}`, and each keeps its `label` as its accessible name. The component's head comment says what the two buttons are now (icon buttons the bar's height, the name in the tooltip) and drops any sentence the change makes false.
2. **The name in the tooltip** (the same ruling). Each tooltip opens with the button's name, in the form of the Lock tooltip in the same tab's header (here the switches differ from the font buttons, whose tooltip is the label alone, since the switches have a sentence to say): the name, a colon, then what the button does. The three tip strings of `config/text/views/sessions/terminal.yml:110-112` are rewritten to that form, the name taken from the same words the label shows (Live, Transcript); their keys stay. The disabled Live's tooltip opens with the name too and keeps its reason.
3. **The Lock line of HELP.md** (ruling Open item 754, which ruled this line into the step, `plan.md:1740`; the exception of `agents/spec.md:72`). `docs/HELP.md:257` says Lock keeps "the tab's box and its grids" from taking typing, the words of the Lock tooltip, the rest of the line unchanged.

The bar's height is the same, to the pixel, for a terminal tab with no session, a tab whose session links, and a tab showing Transcript, at text scale 1 and 1.25, in the default themes and under a terminal palette. The grid does not move when the session links. The one exception is the wrapping every bar has: at a width where the bar fits on one line without the switches and needs a second line with them, the bar gains that line when the session links; this is accepted and not counted against the step.

No other file changes. `TerminalBar.svelte`, `Terminal.svelte`, `ToolButton.svelte` and `Pane.svelte` need no change for this; if the builder finds one is needed, it says why in the report before changing it.
