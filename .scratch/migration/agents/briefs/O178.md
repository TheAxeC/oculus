## What to build

The rulings are `plan.md` Rulings "Open item 738" and "Open item 739". When this step is done, nothing in `cv.ts` names a CV section the website has, except the two sections the hub's sources feed by their nature (`students` and `projects`, lines 330 and 392) and the default sections of `CAREER_LISTS`, which are oculus's career file's own.

**1. The settings.** In `config/options/behavior.yml`, beside `website.kinds`, under the same `heading` and `section`:

- `website.cv_sections`: a list of records with the fields `key` (text), `fields` (list), `period` (a choice of the one word `timestamp`, `empty: true`) and `record_fields` (list, `empty: true`). Each field has a help line saying what it does. A row's `period: timestamp` means the row's period goes in the field `date`, as a timestamp in the CV and as the period text in an employer CV; left out, or the empty text the Settings view writes when it is unset, the period text goes in `duration`. A supervision entry's student always prints in `duration`, and the help line says so. A row's `record_fields` names what an employer CV's row of that section takes from the record it names when it holds none of its own; left out, `location` and `when`; an empty list takes nothing.
- `website.cv_words.months` (list), `website.cv_words.open_end` (text, may be empty), `website.cv_words.award_time` (text, may be empty).

In `config/defaults/behavior.yml`, `website:` gains `cv_sections: []` and `cv_words: { months: [], open_end: '', award_time: '' }`. oculus ships no section and no word.

**2. The reader.** `websiteSettings` returns `cvSections` and `cvWords`. A row a zod shape refuses is left out with a `shape` problem, as `zenodoSettings` does it. The shape takes a `key` matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]*$` (so no empty key and no `__proto__`), other than `personal`, which is the file's person block, and a `fields` list of at least one name. `period` reads the empty text as left out. A row whose `key` an earlier row holds is left out with the `repeat` problem of `reader.ts` (`{ key, where: 'repeat', name }`), the earlier one standing. `website.kinds` is left as it is.

**3. `cv.ts`.** `SECTIONS`, `TITLED`, `AWARDS`, `FROM_RECORD`, `MONTHS`, `OPEN_END` and `AWARD_TIME` are gone. `CvInput` gains the sections and the words, and each caller passes them from `websiteSettings`.

- `cvFile` writes one section per row of the setting, in the rows' order, each with its row's `fields`.
- `withPeriod` reads the section's row. A section no row names prints in `duration`.
- A profile section takes its row's `record_fields`, else `location` and `when`, kept as a constant under its present name `FROM_RECORD_ELSE` or a better one.
- A row of the CV whose section the setting does not list gives one problem `audit.msg_cv_unknown_section` with the row's place and the section, and is written nowhere. So each `Row` knows its place: `<collection>.<entry>` for a collection entry, `<list>.<index>` for a career row, `projects.<id>` for a manifest, `<entry>.students.<index>` for a student, the forms already built there.

Without `months` holding twelve entries a point prints its year alone. Without `open_end` an open period prints its start alone. An empty `award_time` prints the day alone and is no problem.
