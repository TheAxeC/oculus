### 2. A task's subtasks under it, as a subheading (ruling 546 (2) (a))

When done, the Tasks group of the Focus Tasks section draws each task of the file flagged `tasks` that has open subtask lines in the section as a subheading, with those subtasks as rows under it; the Subtasks group no longer holds a task's subtask.

- The subheading is the task's own line drawn as a heading: its title, in a heading style distinct from both the group label (`.lbl`) and a plain row, so a reader sees one task and its subtasks under it. A press on the title opens the task's page, as the task row does now (`openLine`). It keeps every button the task row has now, in the same order and with the same writes (status, agent, the snoozes, Remove asking the Delete question), and its date tail and tone when the task is dated.
- Under it, the task's subtask lines as rows, each with the buttons, tail and tone a subtask row has now, in the order the section's list gives them (dated first, `datedFirst`).
- A task with no open subtask line in the section stays a plain row, as now.
- A subtask line belongs to a task when its target names the same hub, collection and entry id as a line of the section whose target carries `task: true`; two hubs each with a task `work` keep their subtasks apart. A Tasks-group line with no `task: true` (a project's undated deadline, another collection's undated entry) is never a subheading, and its record's subtasks stay in Subtasks.
- A row under a subheading is labelled by the subtask's own `do` text alone, since the heading names the task; a subtask row in the Subtasks group keeps `<record>: <do>` as now.
- The subheading behaves as the task row does in every other way: Tab reaches its title and reveals its buttons as a row's do (`Ticker.svelte`, `onfocusin`); the button the row keeps drawn without hover (`keptAction`, the task's status) stays drawn; while a source tab holds unsaved text of the tasks file its buttons are disabled with the file's words as their title (`fileBlock`); its Remove opens `DeleteAsk` under the section, and a second Remove asks about the second task alone.
- Each subheading keeps its task line's place in the Tasks group's order (`datedFirst`), and its undated subtasks keep their file order. A subheading and its rows are kept together across the section's two columns (`break-inside: avoid`), as a group is now.
- The subheading is drawn by the ticker primitive itself, as a heading variant of a row (a field of `TickerRow` in `src/lib/ui/display/ticker/ticker-rows.ts` that `Ticker.svelte` draws in a heading style), never by restyling the ticker's rows from the view through `:global` and never by a component that rebuilds the ticker's buttons and reveal.
- A subtask line whose task has no line in the section (the task snoozed, its status dropping its own line while `live_subtasks` keeps the subtasks, or the Focus filter hiding the task line and keeping the subtask) stays in the Subtasks group, so no line is drawn under a heading that is not drawn.
- The Subtasks group keeps the subtasks of projects, funding schemes and every collection entry that is no task, as now. The Follow-ups group does not change. A snoozed subtask stays in the Command Center's Snoozed group, as now.
- The grouping is computed in the state layer: the subtask loop of `ticker.ts` (`:516-539`) is unchanged, since its lines already carry their record's target; `taskGroups` (`ticker.ts`) gives the Tasks group as a list of tasks each with its subtask lines, the Subtasks group without them; `hasTasks` counts every line once. `Tasks.svelte` draws it; its head comment says the new grouping. The rows and their buttons stay `rows.ts`'s (`rowMaker`, `act`, `removeAsked`); a change there is made only where the subheading needs it.
- The section's hidden count (`hidden`) and what the Focus filter hides do not change.
- A Svelte component stays at or under 520 lines and a source file at or under 880 (`spec.md:47`). `src/lib/views/boards/focus/` is at its ten items, so a new view component for the section is not put there: `Tasks.svelte` and any component it gains move together into a new subfolder `src/lib/views/boards/focus/tasks/`, with `Focus.svelte`'s import and head comment and `FoldHead.svelte:9`'s comment following. With no new view component, `Tasks.svelte` stays where it is.

1. `lead` is drawn right after `position`, so the two job groups stand together where `position` stood.
2. The `position` word and its text keys keep their names; only their words change (`dlcat_position: applications`, `glyph_position: inbox`), since ruling 546 (1) (a) keeps `position` for `applications.yml`.

7. The subheading is a variant of the ticker row in `ui/display/ticker/`, so its buttons, reveal and kept button are the ticker's own and no view restyles a primitive (`tests/checks/tree/allow-styling.ts`).
