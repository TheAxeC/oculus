## Decisions ruled

1. **Where the API keys live, and their names** (Z500). One map `literature:` in `config/local.yml` with seven optional text keys: `wos_key`, `scopus_key`, `scopus_insttoken`, `openalex_key`, `semantic_scholar_key`, `ncbi_key` and `mailto` (the contact address OpenAlex, Crossref and NCBI ask callers to send). `LOCAL_KEYS` (`src/lib/data/sources/config/local.ts:111-130`) holds no literature key today; an unknown top-level key is collected (`local.ts:362`), warned of on stderr by `readLocal` (`:440-442`) and ignored, and an unknown key under `literature:` is treated the same way (corrected on main at db68c2cf3: `grep -n "^export const LOCAL_KEYS\|unknown" src/lib/data/sources/config/local.ts`). The Settings view lists `local.yml` keys without their values (`HELP.md:261`). OpenAlex without a key allows about 100 searches a day and Semantic Scholar's search answered 429 to four keyless requests of four (probes 1 and 3), so the two optional keys are what makes those sources usable.
2. **The wire format** (Z497). One request per source, `GET /api/literature?source=<id>&q=...`, the page sending one per toggled source at once and merging the answers as each arrives; fast sources draw while a slow one (arXiv answered its 429s after 10.6 s) is still out; the merge is a pure client function.
3. **The source state vocabulary** (Z497), the words of each source's answer and the line the view draws for it: `ok` (count, and the total the source reports), `limited` (429, with the wait when the source names one), `timeout` (no answer within `timeout_s`), `refused` (401 or 403: a key refused or not entitled), `failed` (any other status, an answer that does not read, or an answer above 5 MB), `no-key` (a keyed source with no key), `skipped` (a filter the source cannot hold, such as arXiv under a type other than preprint).
4. **The document type vocabulary** (Z497). One shared list, `any`, `article`, `review`, `preprint`, `conference`, `book`, `chapter`, mapped to each source's own types by the table under "What to build" item 4.
5. **What sort means** (Z497). Every source asked in its own relevance order, the merged list sorted on the page by relevance (fused rank), date or citations. The evidence: Crossref's `sort=published&order=desc` for "tensor decomposition EEG" answered three unrelated works dated 2103, 2103 and 2028 (probe 8), Europe PMC's `sort=P_PDATE_D desc` answered dates out of order, and Semantic Scholar's search has no sort. "Newest" means newest among the relevant hits asked for, which decision 6 widens.
6. **Paging** (Z497). A `More` control after the list that asks every source still holding more for its next page and merges it in.
7. **The merge identity** (Z497). Two hits are one when they share a DOI, else a PubMed id, else an arXiv id, all compared exactly after normalising; a preprint and its published version are one hit when any source names both identifiers (Semantic Scholar's `externalIds` with `DOI` and `ArXiv`, OpenAlex's `locations` holding an arXiv landing page beside the DOI, arXiv's `arxiv:doi` element), the published fields preferred and the preprint's PDF kept. No match on title.
8. **The Add to Lit Watch record** (Z497). The radar's 561 entries carry `title`, `authors`, `venue`, `year`, `url`, `bucket` and `found` each, and 376 carry `project`, `relevance` and `why` (`grep -oE '^ +[a-z_]+:' feeds/literature-radar.yml | sort | uniq -c` for the other keys, `grep -c '^  - title:'` for `title`). Add opens a small form on the card: bucket (the `radar_buckets` of `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:289-293`, `adjacent` preselected), project (optional, the portfolio's projects), relevance (`high`, `med`, `low`, `med` preselected), why (optional text); Enter writes `title`, `authors`, `venue`, `year`, `url`, `bucket`, `project`, `relevance`, `why`, `found` in that order, the empty ones left out.
9. **The text written into the hub file** (Z497). The radar file holds no character outside printable ASCII today (`LC_ALL=C grep -c '[^ -~]' feeds/literature-radar.yml` prints `0`). Markup stripped and entities decoded, then curly quotes to straight, an en or em dash to `-`, the ellipsis character to `...`, a non-breaking space to a space, and every other character kept (accented letters, Greek letters).
10. **The behaviour settings** (Z497). A `literature:` block in `config/behavior.yml` after `search:`, `timeout_s: 20` (seconds one source is given), `per_source: 25` (hits asked of each source per page), `cache_min: 30` (minutes an answer is kept for the same request), each with its `options.yml` entry (item 10 of "What to build") and read by a `literatureSettings` reader beside `searchSettings`, as the Search view's `search:` block is (`config/behavior.yml:175-179`).
11. **The view's place** (Z497). Id `literature`, words "Literature search", the `incoming` group at order 5 after the Calendar, so the chords of Inbox, Lit Watch, Position Leads, LinkedIn and Calendar stay as they are and the group's chords become `e1-e6`, a tab after `linkedin` in the first pane of the Incoming arrangement (`config/layout.yml:72`), glyph `book` (`config/catalogs/glyphs.yml:124`, `icons.yml:123`, no new glyph).
12. **Where the toggles and the search are kept** (Z497). The tab's instance state, saved with the layout: the query, the filters, the sort and the toggles, every keyless source on and a keyed source on when its key is set; a keyed source with no key (`wos`, `scopus`) is drawn as a disabled toggle whose tooltip names its `local.yml` key. Z497's one-line summary in `plan.md:1223` and `choices.md` reads "keyless toggles disabled with their key named"; the option it rules is the one written here, and the six keyless sources are never disabled.
13. **A query that is a DOI** (Z497). A query matching `^(https?://(dx\.)?doi\.org/|doi:)?10\.\d{4,9}/\S+$` is looked up by DOI in each source (the requests are under item 2 of "What to build").
14. **A guarded entry for the merge** (Z501). G45 (`agents/guarded.md:77-79`): "Hits that share an identifier are one hit, and hits that share none are never one." Its Code line is the merge of `src/lib/state/feeds/literature/`, and its test is `tests/state/feeds/literature-merge.test.ts`, 'merges hits by DOI, PubMed id or arXiv id and keeps hits that share none apart', one table over the merge function (T6 under "Tests"), written by this step.

## What to build

1. **The route.** `GET /api/literature` in `src/routes/api/(docs)/literature/+server.ts`, a thin join to a handler in `src/lib/server/content/literature/`, as `routes/api/(files)/search/+server.ts` joins `handleSearch`. It refuses a request without the token with 403 `{ error: 'token' }` before anything else, as `handleSearch` does, and answers with `cache-control: no-store`. Three forms (decision 2):
   - `?sources` answers `{ ok: true, sources: [{ id, keyed, available, key }] }` for the eight sources in the order `openalex`, `semantic_scholar`, `crossref`, `pubmed`, `europe_pmc`, `arxiv`, `wos`, `scopus`; `keyed` is true for `wos` and `scopus`; `available` says whether the source can be asked (a keyed source only when its key is set); `key` is the name of the `local.yml` key a keyed source needs, never a value.
   - `?source=<id>&q=<text>&from=<year>&to=<year>&type=<word>&oa=<1 or 0>&venue=<text>&author=<text>&min_cites=<n>&page=<cursor>` answers `{ ok: true, source, state, total, more, cursor, cached, hits }` for `state: 'ok'`, and `{ ok: true, source, state, status?, wait_s? }` for every other state of decision 3. `total` is the count the source reports, null when it reports none; `more` and `cursor` page the source (decision 6); `cached` is true when the answer came from the cache of item 3. `from` without `to` reads `to` as the current year plus one, and `to` without `from` reads `from` as 1000, so each source is sent a closed range. `oa` reads `1` (open access only) and `0` (any); a missing `oa` is `0`.
   - `?bibtex&doi=<doi>`, `?bibtex&s2=<paper id>` or `?bibtex&arxiv=<id>` answers `{ ok: true, bibtex, from }` (`from` one of `doi`, `semantic_scholar`, `arxiv`) or `{ ok: true, bibtex: null }` when the source has none.
   - Refusals, each with 400 and one word, before any request leaves: `query_empty` (nothing after trimming), `query_long` (over 1000 characters, decision taken 2), `source` (an id outside the eight, or `source` given twice), `year` (a year that is not a whole number from 1000 to the current year plus one, or `from` after `to`), `cites` (not a whole number from 0), `type` (outside decision 4's list), `oa` (any value other than `1` or `0`), `venue_long` (a venue over 200 characters), `author_long` (an author over 200 characters), `page` (a cursor that is not of the form this source's answers carry: a whole number from 0 for a source paged by offset or page number, printable ASCII of at most 200 characters for Europe PMC's `cursorMark`), `doi` (a `bibtex` DOI that does not match `^10\.\d{4,9}/\S+$`), `id` (an `s2` that is not 40 hex digits, an `arxiv` that is not an arXiv identifier). The words are one list typed in `data/model/model-search.ts`, as the Search view's are one list in `data/helpers/search/query.ts:58-75`; each word is worded by a `refused_<word>` line of `config/text/views/feeds/literature.yml`, the one catalog file of every refusal word of this route, and the view words a refusal of this route from those lines first, so a word that is also a shared `OpsRefusal` word never reads as the ops sentence of `failedAnswer`. No value of the request reaches a URL except through `URLSearchParams` or `encodeURIComponent`.
   - A key is never in an answer, a refusal line, a log line or a thrown message. `wos` and `scopus` carry their key in a header (`X-ApiKey`, `X-ELS-APIKey`, and `X-ELS-Insttoken` when set); `semantic_scholar` in `x-api-key`; OpenAlex and NCBI take theirs as the `api_key` query parameter (from their documentation), so no request address is ever logged or put in a line. A failure line names the source, the status and the source's own code when it gives one, as `refusalLine` (`connect/api.ts:41-44`) does, and never the body.
   - A source answer is read up to 5 MB; an answer above that is that source's `failed`, the read stopped at 5 MB.
2. **One adapter per source**, in `server/content/literature/sources/`, each a function from the parsed request to the source's answer read into the per-source hit shape, its answer read through a zod schema beside it (`DESIGN.md:151`); PubMed's `efetch` XML and arXiv's Atom are parsed with `fast-xml-parser` (Z502) before their schema. Every source is asked in its relevance order (decision 5), `per_source` hits a page. The request each sends (`<q>` the query, `<n>` `per_source`):
   - `openalex`: `https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=title_and_abstract.search:<q with commas replaced by spaces>[,publication_year:<from>-<to>][,type:<t>][,is_oa:true][,cited_by_count:><min-1>][,raw_author_name.search:<author>]&per-page=<n>&page=<p>&select=id,doi,display_name,publication_year,publication_date,type,cited_by_count,open_access,best_oa_location,primary_location,authorships,ids,abstract_inverted_index[&mailto=<mailto>][&api_key=<key>]`. The abstract is rebuilt from `abstract_inverted_index` (each word put at each of its positions). A DOI query uses `filter=doi:<doi>`. The `raw_author_name.search` filter is from the documentation; when OpenAlex refuses it the builder drops it and the after-fetch filter of item 4 holds.
   - `semantic_scholar`: `https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/search?query=<q>&offset=<o>&limit=<n>&fields=title,abstract,year,venue,authors,externalIds,citationCount,isOpenAccess,openAccessPdf,publicationTypes,publicationDate,journal,url,citationStyles[&year=<from>-<to>][&publicationTypes=<t>][&openAccessPdf][&minCitationCount=<min>]`, header `x-api-key` when set. A DOI query is `GET /graph/v1/paper/DOI:<doi>?fields=...`.
   - `crossref`: `https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=<q>&rows=<n>&offset=<o>&select=DOI,title,author,published,type,is-referenced-by-count,container-title,abstract,link,license&filter=<from-pub-date:<from>,until-pub-date:<to>,type:<t>>[&query.author=<author>][&query.container-title=<venue>][&mailto=<mailto>]`. A DOI query is `GET https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`. Crossref names no open access, so its hits are open only through a merged source or a PDF link (item 4). Its `link[]` entries are text-mining links and are never the open PDF.
   - `pubmed`: `esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed&term=<q>[ AND <from>:<to>[dp]][ AND <pt>[pt]][ AND free full text[sb]][ AND "<venue>"[ta]][ AND "<author>"[au]]&retmode=json&retmax=<n>&retstart=<o>&sort=relevance&tool=oculus[&email=<mailto>][&api_key=<key>]`, then `efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=<ids>&retmode=xml` for the titles, authors, journal, dates, DOI, publication types and abstracts. PubMed gives no citation count. A DOI query is `term=<doi>[doi]`.
   - `europe_pmc`: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=TITLE_ABS:(<q>)[ AND PUB_YEAR:[<from> TO <to>]][ AND <type clause>][ AND OPEN_ACCESS:y][ AND JOURNAL:"<venue>"][ AND AUTH:"<author>"]&format=json&resultType=core&pageSize=<n>&cursorMark=<cursor or *>`. The open PDF is the `fullTextUrl` with `documentStyle: pdf` and `availabilityCode: OA`. A DOI query is `query=DOI:"<doi>"`.
   - `arxiv`: `https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?search_query=<terms>[ AND submittedDate:[<from>01010000 TO <to>12312359]]&start=<o>&max_results=<n>&sortBy=relevance&sortOrder=descending`, `<terms>` each word or quoted phrase of the query as `all:<term>` joined by ` AND `, an author filter adding ` AND au:<author>`. Its hits are preprints, open, with the PDF link of the entry; the abstract page and PDF addresses are built as `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>` and `https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>` from the entry's identifier, since the entry's `<id>` is `http`. Under a type filter other than `any` and `preprint`, under a venue filter or under a citation minimum above 0 it is not asked and answers `skipped`. A DOI query of an arXiv DOI (`10.48550/arXiv.<id>`) is `id_list=<id>`; any other DOI query skips it. The source's pacing is one request per 3 s (item 3).
   - `wos`: `https://api.clarivate.com/apis/wos-starter/v1/documents?q=TS=(<q>)[ AND PY=<from>-<to>][ AND DT=(<dt>)][ AND SO=(<venue>)][ AND AU=(<author>)]&db=WOS&limit=<n up to 50>&page=<p>&sortField=RS+D`, header `X-ApiKey`. A hit's fields: `uid`, `title`, `types[]`, `source.sourceTitle`, `source.publishYear`, `names.authors[].displayName`, `citations[]` (the `WOS` count), `identifiers.doi`, `identifiers.pmid`, `links.record`. No abstract, no open access flag. A DOI query is `q=DO=(<doi>)`.
   - `scopus`: `https://api.elsevier.com/content/search/scopus?query=TITLE-ABS-KEY(<q>)[ AND PUBYEAR > <from-1> AND PUBYEAR < <to+1>][ AND DOCTYPE(<dt>)][ AND OPENACCESS(1)][ AND SRCTITLE(<venue>)][ AND AUTH(<author>)]&count=<n>&start=<o>&sort=relevancy`, headers `X-ELS-APIKey`, `X-ELS-Insttoken` when set, `Accept: application/json`. A hit's fields: `search-results.entry[]` with `dc:title`, `dc:creator` (first author only), `prism:publicationName`, `prism:coverDate`, `prism:doi`, `citedby-count` (text), `subtype`, `openaccessFlag`, `pubmed-id`, `eid`, `link[]`; an empty answer is one entry with `error: "Result set was empty"`, read as no hits. A DOI query is `query=DOI(<doi>)`.
   - A venue or author is put into a source's quoted clause (PubMed's `"<venue>"[ta]` and `"<author>"[au]`, Europe PMC's `JOURNAL:"<venue>"` and `AUTH:"<author>"`) with every double quote of the value removed first, so the value never closes the clause early; the query itself is sent as typed.
   - The per-source hit: `{ source, id, doi?, pmid?, arxiv?, title, authors: string[], venue?, year?, date?, type, abstract?, cites?, oa?, pdf?, url?, rank, bibtex? }`, typed in `data/model/model-search.ts` and exported through `data/model/index.ts`. `doi` is normalised: lowercased, `https://doi.org/`, `http://dx.doi.org/` and `doi:` taken off, percent escapes decoded, trimmed, a trailing period dropped. `rank` is the hit's place in its source's answer, from 1 over the pages. `date` is `YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY`; a date more than a year after today (Crossref's 2103) is read as absent, and so is a year outside 1000 to the current year plus one. Every text field has its markup removed and its entities decoded (`entities` is a dependency already) and is drawn as text, never as HTML. `pdf` and `url` pass `httpsLink`. A record with no title and no identifier is dropped.
3. **Pacing, timeouts and the cache**, hand-written (Z502), one object on the server context (`ServerContext`, `server/root/context.ts:104`, made once beside `mailConnects` at `:459`), handed to the handler through `locals`, never module state (spec.md's no-globals rule). Each source has a pace: one request at a time per source, and a minimum gap between the start of two requests, 3000 ms for `arxiv`, 1000 ms for `crossref` and `semantic_scholar`, 340 ms for `pubmed` keyless and 100 ms with a key, 0 for the rest. Each request is given `timeout_s` through `timedFetcher`, and a request whose page aborts (a new search, the tab closed) is aborted. A 429 is never retried by the server; its `Retry-After`, or OpenAlex's `x-ratelimit-reset` with `x-ratelimit-remaining: 0`, gives `wait_s`. An answer with `state: 'ok'` is kept for `cache_min` minutes under a key made of the source and every parameter of the request (`q`, `from`, `to`, `type`, `oa`, `venue`, `author`, `min_cites`, `page`, and whether it is a DOI lookup), the API key left out; at most 200 answers, the oldest dropped first; `cache_min: 0` keeps nothing; nothing else is kept. The toggles are not part of a per-source request, so a source toggled off and on inside `cache_min` is answered from the cache.
4. **The merge, the filters and the sort**, pure functions in `state/feeds/literature/`.
   - The merge is decision 7. Fields of a merged hit come from its hits in this order of sources: title, venue, year, date and type from `crossref`, `pubmed`, `europe_pmc`, `scopus`, `wos`, `openalex`, `semantic_scholar`, `arxiv` (the first that has the field); authors from the hit with the longest list in that order; abstract from `pubmed`, `europe_pmc`, `semantic_scholar`, `openalex`, `arxiv`, `crossref`, `scopus`; `cites` the highest count any source gives, with each source's count in the tooltip; `oa` true when any source says open or any hit has a `pdf`; `pdf` from `openalex`, `europe_pmc`, `semantic_scholar`, `arxiv`; `url` `https://doi.org/<doi>` when it has a DOI, else the arXiv abstract page, else `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/<pmid>/`, else the first source's own `url`. A merged hit lists every source that found it. Two hits of one identifier inside one source's answer are one hit at the better (lower) rank of the two.
   - The type map (decision 4): `article` is OpenAlex `article`, Crossref `journal-article`, Semantic Scholar `JournalArticle`, PubMed and Europe PMC `journal article`, WoS `Article`, Scopus `ar`; `review` is OpenAlex `review`, Semantic Scholar `Review`, PubMed `review[pt]`, Europe PMC `PUB_TYPE:"review"`, WoS `Review`, Scopus `re`; `preprint` is OpenAlex `preprint`, Crossref `posted-content`, PubMed `preprint[pt]`, Europe PMC `SRC:PPR`, arXiv; `conference` is Crossref `proceedings-article`, Semantic Scholar `Conference`, WoS `Proceedings Paper`, Scopus `cp`; `book` is OpenAlex and Crossref `book`, Semantic Scholar `Book`, Scopus `bk`; `chapter` is OpenAlex `book-chapter`, Crossref `book-chapter`, Semantic Scholar `BookSection`, Scopus `ch`. A source type outside the map reads as `other`, shown under `any` only. A filter with no source parameter is not sent to that source.
   - Every filter is also applied to every merged hit after the fetch, whatever the source did with it, so no source that ignored a parameter can show a hit outside the filter: the year range on `year`; the type on `type`; open access only on `oa`; venue and author as a case-insensitive match of each word on `venue` and on the authors joined, a double quote in the filter read as a space; the citation minimum on `cites`. A hit that lacks the field fails a filter set on it (a PubMed-only hit fails a citation minimum above 0).
   - The sort (decision 5): relevance is the fused rank, the sum over the hit's sources of `1 / (60 + rank)`, highest first; date is `date` newest first; citations is `cites` highest first; in each, a hit lacking the field goes last, and ties keep relevance order.
