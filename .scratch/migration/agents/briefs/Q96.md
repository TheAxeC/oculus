Read `tools/oculus/.scratch/migration/agents/spec.md` first; its rules govern this step unchanged. The configuration lists no further standards.

## What is on the tree (read on main at 24c5cf8b)

**The Rail section.** It is `src/lib/views/system/workbench/RailEditor.svelte` (262 lines).
- Each row (`data-rail-row`) draws, in order:
  1. a `.mark` span holding the row's glyph;
  2. a `TextInput` (label);
  3. a `Select` (glyph);
  4. a `Select` (page);
  5. three `ToolButton`s (up, down, remove).
- No field has a visible word. Each is named only by its `aria-label`: `workbench.rail_label` "Label of button {0}", `workbench.rail_glyph` "Glyph of button {0}" and `workbench.rail_page` "Page of button {0}", at `config/text/views/system/workbench.yml:25-29`.
- The heading is `workbench.rail_title` "Rail". Checked by reading the file and `sed -n 1,50p` of the text file.

**The glyph field.** It uses `src/lib/ui/forms/Select.svelte`, a native `<select>`.
- Its options hold text only, so no icon can be drawn in them.
- It always offers the empty choice first ("Choose a glyph", `workbench.rail_glyph_empty`).
- It shows a stray value the choices lack, so a manifest is never silently retyped.
- Its choices are `glyphNames(merged)`: the 165 names of `config/catalogs/glyphs.yml` (`python3 -c "import yaml; print(len(yaml.safe_load(open('config/catalogs/glyphs.yml'))['glyphs']))"`).

**Other words and readers.**
- The refusal lines say "glyph" in `workbench.rail_why_glyph` and `workbench.rail_why_glyph_unknown` (`workbench.yml:40-41`).
- `src/lib/ui/display/Glyph.svelte` is the one place a glyph reaches the DOM, and it hides the drawing from a screen reader.
- `tests/views/system/workbench/settings.test.ts` (188 lines) covers the rail half. Lines 40-47 read a row's `.mark`.
- `tests/views/whole/type-scale.test.ts:99` lists RailEditor's font-size rules.

**bits-ui.** bits-ui 2.19.3 is installed, by Q81.
- Its Select is what ruling Z24 (a) names.
- Q81's menus draw their lists through the portal at z-index 70, and `tests/ui/menu/OpenMenu.svelte` shows how a list is rendered open for a server test.

**One tooltip at a time.** This item was booked for Q81 (plan.md, the sub-bullet under Q96's line): a row action inside a tree row that carries its own words opens two bubbles, the row's and the action's.
- `src/lib/ui/menu/Tooltip.svelte:242-302` wraps every tooltip in its own `Tooltip.Provider`.
- bits-ui's provider closes its open tooltip when another of the same provider opens (`node_modules/bits-ui/dist/bits/tooltip/tooltip.svelte.js:155-158`, `onOpen`). With a provider per tooltip, nothing closes the first bubble.
- `src/routes/+layout.svelte` renders every page's children and holds no provider.

## What to build

1. **The words of the Rail section.** These follow rulings Z23 (a) and Z24 (a), and the word is "Icon".
   - **Before each field in every row**, a visible word on the same line: "Label", "Icon", "Page". They are three new strings in `config/text/views/system/workbench.yml`. The word is decoration beside a field whose accessible name already holds it, so it is hidden from a screen reader.
   - **The field names use the word Icon:**
     - `workbench.rail_glyph` reads "Icon of button {0}";
     - `workbench.rail_glyph_empty` reads "Choose an icon";
     - `workbench.rail_why_glyph` reads "button {0} names no icon";
     - `workbench.rail_why_glyph_unknown` reads "button {0} names an icon glyphs.yml does not hold".
     - The keys keep their names. `glyphs.yml` stays the file's name.
   - **Under the heading**, one sentence, a new string: "Each row is a button on the rail: its words, its icon, and the page it shows."
2. **The icon dropdown.** It is a new primitive, `src/lib/ui/forms/GlyphSelect.svelte`, on bits-ui's Select (single).
   - **Trigger:** it draws the chosen name with its icon (`Glyph`) before it, or the empty words when none is chosen. It is styled as `.select` in `Select.svelte` is: the same border, background, radius, font size and padding, under the rice theme too.
   - **List:** it draws the empty choice first, then a stray value when the value is not among the choices, then every choice, each with its icon before its name.
     - The name is the item's text, so bits-ui's type-ahead reads it. When the icon is drawn first by CSS order, the name comes first in the DOM, as Q81's menu items do.
     - The list goes to the body through bits-ui's portal at z-index 70, like the menus. It scrolls, with a height that keeps it in the window.
   - **Props:** `value`, `label` (accessible name), `empty`, `choices: string[]` and `onchange`.
   - **Split for testing:** the list's markup is split into its own component, so that a test harness under `tests/ui/forms/` renders it inside an open bits-ui Select, as `tests/ui/menu/OpenMenu.svelte` does for the menus.
   - `ui/forms` imports `ui/display`'s `Glyph`, which is downward in the ui order.
3. **RailEditor.**
   - The glyph field becomes `GlyphSelect`. The Page field stays `Select`.
   - The row's separate `.mark` goes, since the trigger now shows the row's icon.
   - The row still wraps at narrow widths, each word staying with its field.
4. **One tooltip at a time.**
   - A new `src/lib/ui/menu/TooltipGroup.svelte` holds the one `Tooltip.Provider`, with the settings the provider in `Tooltip.svelte` carries today (`delayDuration={DELAY}`, `skipDelayDuration={0}`, `disableHoverableContent`, `ignoreNonKeyboardFocus`). `DELAY` moves with it or is shared, never duplicated.
   - `src/routes/+layout.svelte` wraps its children in it.
   - `Tooltip.svelte` draws `Tooltip.Root` with no provider of its own.
   - The tests that render a tooltip render it inside the group, through a harness if needed.
   - `DESIGN.md:306`, which describes the shell's tooltips, gains that one tooltip is open at a time, and names `ui/menu/TooltipGroup.svelte`.
