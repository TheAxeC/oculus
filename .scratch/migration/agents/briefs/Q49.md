## What to build

1. **A hidden button takes no room.** While a row is neither hovered nor holding the keyboard focus, its revealed buttons take no width in the row's flow, so the name has the whole row. The `kept` button keeps its place as it does today.
2. **They draw over the row's end when they appear.** Hovering the row, or moving the keyboard into it, draws the buttons over the right end of the row rather than pushing the name. The name under them is not reflowed, and a name too long for the row is cut by the row's own rule, not by the buttons.
3. **The keyboard still reaches them.** The buttons stay in the focus order and reachable by Tab from the row; nothing is `display: none` while it must take focus. A press works the same as today.
4. **A touch device** (the `@media (hover: none)` branch) keeps the buttons drawn, and there they may take their room as they do now.
