## What to build

1. In `GroupHead.svelte`, a group whose section stands inside the body of another `GroupHead` (at any depth of components between them) draws its strip one level down:
   - its label at `--fs-sm`, its count beside it at `--fs-sm`;
   - its strip's content indented so its fold control starts where the parent strip's glyph or label starts: one fold-control box (`var(--bare-box, var(--tab-action))`) plus the strip's 6 px gap per level, so it holds at every `--text-scale` and in the rice themes;
   - the indent is padding inside the strip, so the solid `--panel` paint covers the strip's whole band from its group's left edge and nothing scrolling under it shows beside it;
   - everything else as a top-level strip: sticky under its parent's strip, one line, the fold control, the glyph, the tone, the actions, the 8 px under it.
   The rule is written once in the component's style, keyed on the nesting itself: a value the body passes down to the groups inside it (as `--group-strip` and `--group-above` are), or a scoped `.body` with a `:global(...)` tail. Never a selector that matches another component's `.body`, and never a prop a caller sets. A group nested two deep is indented two levels, each body adding its level to the value it passes down.
2. No caller changes. Each place of "What is on the tree" where a `GroupHead` stands inside another's body takes the new look by item 1 alone; confirm the list by reading the callers, and list each place in the report with the file and line of both groups. A `GroupHead` that is not inside another's body keeps its look exactly.
3. The head comment of `GroupHead.svelte`: the sentence at `:29-31` says the label and count sizes of a top-level strip and of a nested one, and a sentence says how a nested strip is indented and why the indent is painted. `:48` stays. `:55-56` and `:61-62` already name the nesting views and stay. The comment stays filled at 100 columns.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. "One level down" is the label and count at `--fs-sm` and the indent to the parent's label, as ruling 668 (a) states; no other property changes.
2. The nesting is read from the nesting itself (a value a group's body passes down, or a scoped body selector), so a caller cannot opt out and no prop is added.
3. The indent starts the nested fold control where the parent's glyph or label starts (one fold-control box plus the gap), since no nesting parent draws a glyph today and the step is the same with one.
4. The indent is padding, not margin, so the strip keeps painting its whole band, as the head comment and `docs/HELP.md:573` say a strip does.
