# Brief: O7, the layout tree, the sash and the split view

Read `../spec.md` first; it governs this package unchanged. Then `tools/oculus/DESIGN.md`, "Layer 3, view" (primitives, shell, boundaries), "Layout as data", "Client state", and the decision of 2026-09-10 on layout. This package is the data half of the workbench: the tree type of `DESIGN.md`, every operation on it as a pure function, its persistence, and the two components that render a split and let a sash resize it. O8 adds the rail, the tabs, the panel and the view registry on top of it.

## What to build

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/state/`:

- `layout.svelte.ts`. The types exactly as `DESIGN.md` writes them: `ViewId`, `Instance = { id, view, state }`, `Layout` as the union of `{ kind: 'split'; dir: 'row' | 'col'; sizes: number[]; children: Layout[] }` and `{ kind: 'pane'; tabs: Instance[]; active: string }`. `ViewId` is `string` with a doc comment saying the view registry (O8) is its vocabulary; a layout may name a view that is not registered (a saved layout from a later or earlier version) and the shell shows an empty pane for it. A node is addressed by its path, the list of child indices from the root (`[]` is the root, `[1, 0]` the first child of the second child); nothing in the data carries a node id, and an instance is addressed by its `id`.
- The operations, each a pure function that returns a new tree and never mutates its argument, exported for the tests and used by the store: `paneAt(layout, path)`, `panes(layout)` (every pane with its path, depth first), `findInstance(layout, id)` (the instance, its pane path, and its index), `openInstance(layout, path, instance)` (append to the pane's tabs and make it active), `closeInstance(layout, id)` (remove the tab; the pane's `active` moves to the neighbour; a pane left with no tabs is removed from its split, a split left with one child is replaced by that child, the root pane is never removed and stays empty), `activate(layout, id)`, `moveInstance(layout, id, path, index)` (between panes or within one; the source pane is cleaned as `closeInstance` does), `splitPane(layout, path, dir, instance)` (replace the pane by a split of the pane and a new pane holding `instance`, sizes `[0.5, 0.5]`; a pane already inside a split of the same `dir` is inserted as a sibling instead), `resize(layout, path, sizes)` (sizes normalised to sum to 1, every entry at least `MIN_SIZE = 0.1`, the length checked against the children).
- The invariants, in one exported `checkLayout(layout): string[]` the tests and `parseLayout` use: a split has at least two children; `sizes.length` equals `children.length`, sums to 1 within 1e-6, and every entry is at least `MIN_SIZE`; a pane's `active` names one of its tabs unless it has none, when `active` is the empty string; instance ids are unique across the tree; `dir` and `kind` are the allowed words.
- `parseLayout(value: unknown): Layout | null`: accepts only a tree that passes every shape and invariant check, so a hand-edited or stale `localStorage` value never reaches the shell. `serialiseLayout(layout): string` is `JSON.stringify` of `{ version: 1, layout }`; `parseLayout` reads that envelope and returns null on any other version.
- The store: `layout` as `$state` (the current tree), `defaultLayout()` (for O7, one root pane with no tabs), `loadLayout(storage: Pick<Storage, 'getItem' | 'setItem'> = localStorage)` under the key `oculus.layout.v1`, and one `apply(next: Layout)` that replaces the tree and saves it; every operation above reaches the store through `apply`. The store module runs in the browser and in a test (a fake storage in the test); it never touches `localStorage` at import time.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/ui/`:

- `Sash.svelte`: the handle between two children of a split. Props: `dir` (`'row'` makes a vertical handle that moves horizontally, `'col'` the other way), `onresize(delta: number)` called with the pointer's movement in pixels since the last call, `onreset()` on double-click. Pointer events with `setPointerCapture`, so the drag survives the pointer leaving the handle; arrow keys move by 16 px; `role="separator"`, `aria-orientation`, `tabindex="0"`, the `aria-label` through `t('ui.sash')`. The handle is 6 px wide with a 1 px `--line` down its middle, `--accent` while dragging or focused, `cursor: col-resize` or `row-resize`, and it sets `user-select: none` on the body only while dragging. Tokens only, no literal colour, no literal string.

Under `tools/oculus/src/lib/shell/`:

- `SplitView.svelte`: renders a `Layout`. A split is a flex container in `dir` whose children get `flex: <size> 1 0` (the sizes are fractions, so a child's basis is its fraction of the free space) with a `Sash` between neighbours; a sash's `onresize` converts the pixel delta to a fraction of the container's size along `dir` and calls `onresize(path, sizes)`; `onreset` gives every child of that split an equal share. A pane is rendered by a snippet the caller passes, `pane(node, path)`, so O8 supplies the tab strip and the mounted view and O7 supplies nothing but the box; `SplitView` imports no view and no registry. Props: `layout`, `pane` (the snippet), `onresize(path, sizes)`. It carries no state of its own; the store is the caller's.

Under `tools/oculus/src/routes/`:

- `+page.svelte`: the layout store is loaded on mount; O7's page arrangement, used only when the store holds nothing saved, is a row split of two panes (sizes 0.6 and 0.4) holding no tabs; the `pane` snippet renders the O6 status block (socket status, portfolio version, the counts) in the first pane and the pane's path in the second, both through `t()`. Dragging the sash resizes the two panes, and the sizes survive a reload, which is what the package proves. O8 replaces this page.
- `config/text.yml` gains, under `text:`, a `ui:` group with `sash: "Resize"`, and under `app:` whatever keys the page needs (`pane_path: "pane at {0}"`, for example).

Under `tools/oculus/tests/`:

- `state/layout.test.ts`: every operation on hand-built trees, every invariant of `checkLayout` shown violated once, `parseLayout` rejecting a wrong version, a wrong `kind`, a split with one child, sizes that do not sum to 1, a duplicate instance id, and accepting a valid envelope; `openInstance` then `closeInstance` returning to the same tree; `closeInstance` collapsing a one-child split; `moveInstance` across panes keeping the ids unique and cleaning the source; `resize` clamping to `MIN_SIZE` and renormalising; the store's load and save through a fake storage object, including a saved value that fails `parseLayout` falling back to the default and being overwritten on the next `apply`. Nothing in these tests touches `localStorage` or the DOM.
- `shell/splitview.test.ts`: `render` from `svelte/server` (this works under Vitest's node environment with no further dependency: checked on 2026-09-10 by rendering `+page.svelte` to HTML in a throwaway test) over a three-pane layout (a row split holding a pane and a col split of two panes): the HTML holds three pane boxes rendered by a test snippet that prints the path, two sashes, and `flex` styles carrying the sizes. `Sash.svelte`'s pointer behaviour has no DOM in the suite; the report says so rather than claiming coverage, and `svelte-check` and the page are what exercise it.

## Facts checked on the tree, 2026-09-10

- Svelte 5.56 with runes everywhere (`vite.config.ts` sets `runes: true` outside `node_modules`); `$state`, `$props`, `$derived`, snippets and `{@render}` are the idiom, and `.svelte.ts` is the file form of a state module.
- `tests/boundaries.test.ts` lets `ui/` import `ui/**`, `themes/**` and `state/text`; `shell/` may import `shell/**`, `ui/**`, `state/layout`, `state/ui`, `state/text` and `views/registry`; `state/` may import `state/**` and `data/model`. `SplitView` importing `ui/Sash.svelte` and `state/layout` is inside the table; the table is not edited.
- `tests/keys.test.ts` fails on any `t(` call whose first argument is not a string literal, and on a literal the catalog lacks; `config/text.yml` is where a key is added, under `text:`.
- The cockpit's `assets/css` is not a source for this package: the sash and the split are new, and the design's tokens (`--line`, `--accent`, `--panel`, `--bg`, the radii) are what they use.
