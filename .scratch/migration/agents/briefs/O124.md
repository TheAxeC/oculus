## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. Audit is one group holding its six sections, nested, because "a section of Findings" is what was ruled, and the six stay foldable. So `GroupHead` learns nesting.
2. The search covers both sections. A search that ignored half the rows in one view would read as "no match" while a match stands above it.
3. The first section is labelled Files, and it comes first, as the errors that stop records from resolving.
4. The status bar shows the one count too, since it opens this view and two numbers for one view contradict the ruling. Its tooltip changes with it.
5. The Gallery's state of Search and Segment is lost when its section closes, since "draws nothing" means unmounted.
6. The three text files keep their paths, since the audit file also carries the scan's messages and `config-folder.test.ts` lists them.
7. A closed section reads as a group head only, with no count and no Reset.
8. `findings` keeps `rail: true`: ruling 635 takes only `repositories` and `problems` off the rail, and a finding is something to act on.
9. A muted finding is not counted, as today: the reader chose to leave it.
10. Status goes untinted and wraps its content in `Surface`, since the Gallery exists to show what a card looks like and a tinted pane takes a card's fill away.
11. The System chords move as the catalogue's order gives: y7 History, y8 Processes, and Processes gains a key.
12. The pane-state key is `showGallery`.
