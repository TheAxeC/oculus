## What to build

When the step is done, every card of a project at an accepted or published stage whose manifest gives a year says in its facts the year the paper appeared, the project board card and the Focus lane card alike, and no card of a project at another stage shows a year.

1. **The rule.** A pure function in `src/lib/state/boards/focus.ts`, beside `laneFacts`, gives the year a card draws for a project, read in the words of the project's own hub: the project's `year` when its stage carries both `terminal` and `public` in `stages`, and nothing otherwise (no `year`, a stage without both flags, or no stage). It reads the flags with `hasWord`, never a stage name. Its doc comment says what it gives and why only those stages: the year of a paper in flight is not the year it appeared. `focus.ts`'s head comment, where it lists what the file holds, names it.
2. **The board card.** In `CardFacts.svelte`, when the rule gives a year, a `Tag` with the glyph of `project.card.year_glyph` (`calendar`) and the year as its words, wrapped in `Tooltip` with `project.card.year_tip` ("Year: {0}", the year in the place), drawn after the chips and before the code status. The head comment of `CardFacts.svelte` (lines 2-8) and the facts paragraph of `ProjectCard.svelte`'s head comment (lines 2-7) name the year among the facts, in their order, each paragraph rewrapped whole at 100 columns.
3. **The lane card.** `LaneFact`'s kinds gain `year`, and `laneFacts` gives the year fact first among the facts (before the role, the tier and the venue), under the rule of item 1, with the year as its label and its whole words. `LaneCard.svelte` draws it with the glyph of `project.card.year_glyph` and the tip of `project.card.year_tip`, through `FACT_GLYPHS` and `factTip`. The doc comment of `laneFacts` and the head comment of `LaneCard.svelte` name the year fact.
4. **The words.** `config/text/views/boards/project.yml` gains `year_glyph: calendar` and `year_tip: 'Year: {0}'` under `project.card`, in the file's key order.
5. **The flags' descriptions.** The comment of `config/catalogs/vocab.yml` lines 55 and 59 and the descriptions of `src/lib/data/shapes/catalog-files/vocab.ts` lines 153-155 and 168-170 name what the two flags now decide together: a card draws the project's year at a stage that carries both `terminal` and `public`.
6. **The page.** `docs/HELP.md:298` names the year in the top row's list where it is drawn: the year the paper appeared ("Year: 2025"), drawn only at a stage that carries both `terminal` and `public` (accepted and published in oculus's words). `docs/HELP.md:296` names the year among a lane card's tags, before the role, at such a stage, which with oculus's lanes is on no lane.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The glyph is `calendar`, the one glyph of the catalog that stands for a date.
2. On the board card the tag stands after the chips and before the code status, so the facts about the paper's state (its lane, form, chips, year) come before those about its code and compute. On the lane card the year is the first fact after the marks, for the same reason.
3. The rule lives in `focus.ts` beside `laneFacts`, in the state layer, since the lane card's facts are made there and the board card reads `focus.ts` already (`CardFacts.svelte:33`).
4. The tag is drawn wherever `ProjectCard` draws a project, since one row of facts serves every view that draws the card.
