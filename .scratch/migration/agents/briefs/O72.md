## What to build

1. **The bound.** The closest word is named only when its Levenshtein distance from the word written is at most half the length of the word written, rounded down: `distance <= Math.floor(word.length / 2)`, the length being that of the text the finding shows the value as (the string itself for a text value, the JSON of any other value, as `statusFindings` passes it today). The bound lives in one place, `closestWord` (or a function beside it in `vocab-keys.ts` that every caller uses), so no caller can name a word past it. Its doc comment states the bound and why: a word past it is no likely misspelling, and naming it misleads the reader.
2. **Three outcomes for each of the three findings.** For a value outside its list:
   - the list holds no word: the `*_no_vocab` sentence, as today;
   - a word of the list lies within the bound: the `*_not_in_vocab` sentence naming it, as today;
   - no word lies within the bound: a new sentence that ends after the list's name.
   The caller tells "no word in the list" from "no word within the bound" by the list itself, never by the same `undefined`.
3. **The new sentences**, in `config/text/findings.yml` beside the others under `findings.validate`, word for word; the first two are the ruling's words ("end after 'is not a word of the stages list' (or of the statuses)"), the third follows the existing Position Lead sentences (decision 3):
   - `status_unknown: 'status {0} is not a word of the statuses list'`
   - `stage_unknown: 'stage {0} is not a word of the stages list'`
   - `lead_status_unknown: 'status {0} is not a word of the Position Lead statuses'`
4. **The sentences that state the hint** say it is named only within the bound. Each states what holds now, in plain words: the finding names the closest word of the list when that word is within half the written word's length in single-letter changes, rounded down. The five reader-facing lines `docs/HELP.md:519`, `docs/data-map.md:199`, `config/options/catalogs/vocab.yml:18`, `config/catalogs/vocab.yml:101` and `:158`, where only the clause about the closest word changes; and the comments `vocab-keys.ts:6`, `validate.ts:219-221` and `:316-319` (each doc comment naming the third outcome) and `schema-rules.test.ts:10`.
5. **The tests follow, under G56 and G126.** `schema-rules.test.ts`'s `STATUS_ROWS` gain three rows: a collection status `xyz` and a collection status `scope` (one past the bound: length 5, bound 2, "scoping" at 3), and a lead status `zzzz`, each expecting the new sentence, with `statusSentence` giving it; the row type says which sentence a row expects, its comment (`:180`) says so, and the test title (`:294`) states what the rows check, the closest word named only within the bound. A new table in `schema-rules.test.ts` holds the stage finding, one row per outcome: a manifest at `submited` (the word named), at `scope` (no word within the bound) and a stages list that holds no word (the `stage_no_vocab` sentence). `hub-vocab.test.ts:77` expects `'stage "nascent" is not a word of the stages list'`. No new guarded entry.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The length the bound halves is that of the written value as the finding shows it (the text, or the JSON of a non-text value), the word a reader compares the hint with. Under it, over the tree's lists: "submited" names "submitted" (1 of 4), "in_prep" names "in-prep" (1 of 3), "revison" names "revision" (1 of 3), "scope" names nothing ("scoping" at 3 of 2), "draft" names nothing ("nascent" at 6 of 2), "accepted" as a stage names nothing ("nascent" at 5 of 4), "finished" as a stage names "published" (4 of 4). The last is the ruling's own consequence at the edge of the bound. The comparison is case-sensitive, so a word written in capitals such as `SUBMITTED` (9 edits from "submitted") gets no hint; no value of the hub is written so today.
2. The new sentence keys are `stage_unknown`, `status_unknown` and `lead_status_unknown`, internal keys of the text catalog.
3. The Position Lead status finding takes the bound too: it goes through the same `statusFindings` and `closestWord`, and a bound on one status finding and not the other would name a far word for a lead and not for a collection entry. Its sentence, `lead_status_unknown`, is not worded by the ruling; it follows the existing Position Lead sentences (`findings.yml:304-305`).
