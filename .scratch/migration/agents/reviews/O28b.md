4. **Two identical branches.** `src/lib/server/sessions/json.ts:72-73`. No comment says so, and a reader meets what looks like a copy-paste defect.
5. **`close()` queries the whole document.** `views/Memories.svelte:80`: `document.querySelector('[data-tile="..."]')`. With two Memories panes open the focus can return to the other pane's tile. Every other locator in the view is scoped by `data-instance`.
