## Defects in the round's own addition

**A. The tab strip is not one stop of the focus order, which is what two documents now say it is.** Only the tabs and their close controls sit inside the tablist (`Tabs.svelte:182-262`). The two scroll controls (`:172-179`, `:266-274`) and the menu opener (`:277-281`) are outside it and keep their own tab stops. So a pane strip is two stops with its pane menu, and four when the strip overflows. `HELP.md:56` says "Tab steps past a whole strip in one press" and `DESIGN.md:305` says "every strip of tabs" is one stop. `tests/ui/primitives.test.ts:707-714` counts `role="tab"` elements only, so no check would fail.

**B. The strip's `tabindex` 0 does not follow the arrow keys.** `Tabs.svelte:211` keys it to `tab.id === active`, so after ArrowLeft the focus sits on a `tabindex="-1"` tab while the stop stays on the showing tab. Tab out and Shift+Tab back returns the reader to the showing tab, not to where they were. The rail does move its stop (`ActivityRail.svelte:230-238`, `:261`), so the two strips follow different rules under one sentence in `DESIGN.md:305` ("tabindex 0 on the control at the stop and -1 on the rest"). It also departs from the ARIA tabs pattern, where the 0 moves with focus. `tests/ui/primitives.test.ts:709-713` and `tests/browser/keyboard.spec.ts:184-192` both assert the current form, so the deviation is locked in by tests.

**C. Delete and Backspace close a pane tab with no confirmation.** `Tabs.svelte:136-138` calls `onclose`, which for a pane tab is `PaneView.svelte:252` to `closeInstance`, unguarded; the only `Confirm` in `PaneView.svelte` (`:276`) is for closing a whole pane. An Editor with unsaved fields (`views/Editor.svelte:71`) goes with it. Clicking the close control has always been unguarded, so this is not a new bypass, it is a new one-keystroke path on a strip Tab now reaches, and Backspace is easy to hit on a focused tab. A page tab is guarded: `PageStrip.svelte:102-107` asks when the page holds more than one pane.
